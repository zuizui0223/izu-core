### Predeclared 2×2 and estimand

Rows: original t200 genomic **origin** evolved-A or fixed-A. Columns: new t200→1000 **future expression/mutation permission** fixed A=0.5 versus evolvable A. The within-origin future E−F comparison starts from *exactly the same t200 diploid alleles*. Original origins arise from distinct past treatment paths, so **between-origin contrasts are descriptive genetic-state contrasts and cannot isolate early assurance causation**. All outcomes are signed **new future I(t1000)−I(t200)** contrasts; a future E−F within one origin equivalently equals the final I difference because initial I is matched. Bootstraps resample independent **visitor histories**, not cells, individual plants or time points; 4,096 resamples per setting, descriptive 95% percentile intervals.
