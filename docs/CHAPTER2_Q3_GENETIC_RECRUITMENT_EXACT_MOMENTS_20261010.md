# Q3 finite genetic realization: exact Poisson-capped diploid transmission

**2026-10-10 — deterministic original-source analysis, not a new preregistered experiment or ecological population observation.** No newly sampled visitors, genetic trajectories or stochastic demographic paths. Source code is the unchanged Model 3 reproductive ledger, parental lottery and Mendelian inheritance, using controlled pollen background B48. The original experimental findings #411 and #442 remain unchanged.

## The exact gap in the four-question logic

The Chapter 2 structure remains **閾値 → 順序 → 実現 → 帰結**. Q1 local marginal fitness or F/P/S changes do **not** tell us what alleles will be transmitted. Q2's experimentally assigned phenotype timing does not manipulate spontaneous genetic order. The Q3 transition depends on a finite number of retained offspring and on their actual parental genotype distribution.

Earlier still-unmerged Draft [PR #455](https://github.com/zuizui0223/izu-core/pull/455) already demonstrated one-step parental lottery versus Mendelian variance **conditional on a fixed number of offspring**. This analysis adds the missing piece to the original source model: **integrate every possible resident recruitment count** `R=min(Poisson(mu),K)`, including extinction `R=0`, with no survivor-only misclassification.

## Original source equations

The original `reproduce_kb` ledger gives the donor-row `i`, recipient-mother column `j` matrix `F_ij` of viable outcross offspring, plus the maternal viable selfing diagonal `S_j`. Let `C_ij=F_ij+1(i=j)S_j`, `mu=sum(C)`, and `p_ij=C_ij/mu`. This is exactly the parent-pair lottery of `population.advance`: conditional on `R` recruits, parent pairs are independent draws with probability `p_ij`.

For trait locus `k` in a diploid parental genotype `a_{i,0},a_{i,1}`, mean `g_i=(a_{i,0}+a_{i,1})/2` and homolog difference `d_i=a_{i,0}-a_{i,1}`. A Mendelian offspring inheriting one homolog independently from father `i` and mother `j` has

```text
E[z_child|i,j] = (g_i+g_j)/2
Var(z_child|i,j) = (d_i^2+d_j^2)/16

m = sum_ij p_ij * (g_i+g_j)/2

V_parent = sum_ij p_ij * (((g_i+g_j)/2) - m)^2
V_Mendel = sum_ij p_ij * (d_i^2+d_j^2)/16

P(R=r) = PoisPMF(r;mu),       0 <= r < K
P(R=K) = PoisSF(K-1;mu)

P(R>0) = 1-exp(-mu)
E[zbar_next | R>0] = m
Var(zbar_next | R>0)
    = (V_parent+V_Mendel) * E[1/R | R>0]

E[allele-copy trait sum next generation, including extinct R=0]
    = 2 * E[R] * m
```

The distinct last two lines matter: **when a source population goes extinct, its next-generation mean allele value is missing, not zero**, whereas its total inherited allele-copy mass is zero. The survivor-conditioned mean is *not* a claim of a survivor-conditioned long-run evolutionary effect.

The analytic reduction holds because adult survival, immigrant seeds and mutation are zero **for the current source experiment**, and each retained offspring uses the unchanged independent original parental draw. If resident adults survived, genetically heterogeneous immigrants entered, or allele mutations were active, this expression would require extra terms and cannot be repurposed unchanged.

## Exact six source examples: full K8 versus K48 recruitment lottery

The source starts with eight adults, four **fixed, artificial** visitor types, B48 and ovule budget 6. The first two fixtures have identical expressed floral investment and consequently identical reproductive ledgers (including the same pollen transfer and total seed intensity). The only difference is whether each investment locus is homozygous `0.35/0.35` or heterozygous `0.20/0.50`. Both have expressed investment 0.35 in **every adult**.

| Homozygotes, identical phenotype | 8 | 0.999876 | 7.2680 | 0 | **0** |

These numbers are generated by two independent calculations of the transparent **synthetic source states** and checked against the original model through regression tests. They are analytical conditional probabilities/moments, **not** six observed natural island populations, six independent evolutionary simulations or inferred confidence intervals.

## Three scientific points established *within this restricted model*

1. **Phenotypically identical populations may have radically different inherited variance.** The homozygous and heterozygous cases have the same adults' expressed traits, pollinator treatment, parent fertility, `mu` and immediate occupancy. Yet Mendelian variance of a single child's investment is exactly **0.01125** with hidden `0.20/0.50` heterozygosity and **0** with `0.35/0.35` homozygosity. This is standard Mendelian theory applied to the original model, not a newly discovered biological phenomenon.

2. **K modifies the magnitude of finite genetic realization noise without changing its one-generation conditional expectation.** Both capacities have `P(next occupancy)=0.999876` and the same expected genetic offspring mean, but the smaller K yields fewer retained recruits and a larger `E[1/R|R>0]`. Accordingly, the heterozygote next-mean standard deviation changes from **0.03793 (K48)** to **0.04048 (K8)**. This is a source-state test of *one-generation* genetic realization, not a causal estimate of the independent #442 80-update K effect.

3. **Expected directional response is not guaranteed finite realization.** In the selected heterogeneous-investment fixture (parent investment means `0.20, 0.25, ..., 0.55` with `±0.02` homolog deviations), the expected offspring investment shift is **−0.001407**. But the predicted conditional next-mean standard deviation is **0.04209** at K8, about 30× the magnitude of the mean shift. The change direction can therefore differ between finite outcomes; we do **not** infer an exact reversal frequency from only the first two moments, nor claim that this fixture matches the historical #411 realized genetic evolution.

The source heterogeneous fixture's **single-child variance** also separates a parental-lottery term **0.0119253** from a Mendelian homolog-segregation term **0.0002000**, emphasizing that both are present and conceptually distinct. Their proportional contributions depend on the chosen standing variation and ecological ledger.

## Q3→Q4: what is and is not solved?

**Solved analytically:** a transparent first-generation mapping from a source genomic population, pollinator-dependent reproductive parentage and individual/inbreeding-adjusted offspring weights, through Poisson truncated recruitment, to the **conditional expected inherited trait and variance** plus unconditional expected allele-copy mass.

**Not solved:** whether the native genome's evolved investment change *causes* its own 80-generation population survival difference. Subsequent density dynamics, visitor history, standing allele covariances, rare mutation, extinction selection and the competing expression/genotype interventions need to be tracked through matched **histories**. The exact clonal 80-update null [PR #461](https://github.com/zuizui0223/izu-core/pull/461) excludes genetic variation and therefore cannot resolve this question.

**Next scientific gate:** audit original finite historical trajectories with these stage-specific estimands, recording expected genetic shift before offspring draw, realized next mean where R>0, unconditional recruit counts, parent-gene contribution, and subsequent survival. All exposed source histories must be included. Without prospective independently seeded cohorts and an identifiability-proofed genetic intervention, this remains a retrospective mechanistic decomposition, not a confirmed evolutionary mediator.
