## Supplementary definitions and audit notes

**A (autonomous assurance)** is the inherited capacity to fill ovules not fertilized by outcross pollen. Fixed **expression** leaves selfing and latent A inheritance intact. Under cost-free delayed selfing with available viable offspring, the expected maternal marginal seed return is nonnegative, but direct costs, prior selfing, pollen discounting, inbreeding depression, and paternal export can reverse broader lifetime-fitness conclusions. **M (visitor matching)** has an externally assigned matching optimum and no additional allocation cost in the tested forecast comparison. **I (floral allocation)** is an inherited investment coordinate, not flower diameter, corolla pigment or floral symmetry.

**Order and prediction are different quantities.** A-first requires two sustained threshold events in a specified order; A-only is not A-first. Repeated identical endpoints may reflect fixation at the upper standing-allele bound even if near/far *changes* are identically zero. Held-out error reduction in one crossed cohort does not estimate the probability of a new island following the same path. The 25,600 crossed cells share only 160 founder and 160 visitor source identities; they are not 25,600 independent evolution experiments or real islands.

**Genetic support is direction-specific.** The registered R4 genetic target requires retention of founder-relative high-I-compatible alleles under closed inheritance. The distinct 2,304-history original lower-I experiment concerns *additional decrease* of I, not restoration of ancestral higher I. A population retaining target-compatible alleles may have no segregating I variation left, may already satisfy the founder target, or may fail to respond under the destination's selection within the studied time. The manuscript neither demonstrates a universal recovery trap nor infers a speciation/diversification rate.

**Survival and genetic effects are not one estimated mediation.** At B8 all registered comparison populations survive: differential extinction cannot explain its high-I loss contrast. A policy can still modify population-size histories and correlated parental contributions among survivors. At B4.5 extinct populations remain in the all-start survival denominator; post-extinction traits are undefined, not numerically zero. The resource-dependent survival effect and B8 genetic support effect are separately registered rather than a single demonstrated genetic-cost-of-rescue pathway.
