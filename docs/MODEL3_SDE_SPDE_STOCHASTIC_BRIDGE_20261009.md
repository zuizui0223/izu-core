# Model 3 finite stochastic bridge — SDE and trait-space SPDE feasibility (2026-10-09)

**Scope: steps 1 and 2 only.** No geographic or observational INLA–SPDE, no Chapter 1/3 trait fitting, no tuning to islands, and no modified canonical biology. All files here are independent read-only diagnostics outside `scripts/model3_island/`.

## Aim and current research status

The confirmed Model 3 biological result is that reproductive-assurance evolution compresses floral-investment divergence over four reproductive settings. The later 64-independent-history assigned-expression-order experiment gave a pooled history-level DID −0.0135891 [−0.0228119, −0.0043024], whose entire interval fell inside the preregistered practical-equivalence window ±0.05. This new engineering work **does not rerun or alter** either result.

The unresolved mathematical question is whether stochastic finite-population genetic realization can be represented by a justified SDE/SPDE approximation without quietly changing pollen, mating, mutation or inheritance. The existing positive-mutation three-locus deterministic/PDE model did **not** pass its declared grid-refinement test; no SDE/SPDE is permitted to be called a validated substitute on the strength of a one-step diagnostic.

## Stage 1: exact one-step restricted finite-ABM moment target

Run canonical `reproduce(state,visitors,config)`, then inspect the parental recruitment weights exactly as `population.advance()` does:

\[
W_{fm} = \mathrm{outcross}_{fm} + \mathbf1_{f=m}\,\mathrm{self\_viable}_m, \quad
R=\sum_{fm}W_{fm}, \quad \pi_{fm}=W_{fm}/R.
\]

With adult survival=0, mutation=0, seed immigration=0, carrying capacity (K), Model 3 recruits

\[
N=\min(\operatorname{Poisson}(R),K).
\]

For each recruited offspring, its three diploid allele means have conditional parental-pair mean

\[
m_{fm,k}=(\bar a_{f,k}+\bar a_{m,k})/2.
\]

For independently segregating loci, conditional within-pair variance along trait (k) is
((d_{f,k}^2+d_{m,k}^2)/16), where (d=a_0-a_1). Mixing the paired offspring distributions by (pi_{fm}) yields (mu) and the **full 3×3 covariance** (Sigma_{\rm offspring}), retaining cross-trait covariance generated by correlated parental pairs.

Given (N=n>0), the variance of the recruited population's trait mean is (Sigma_{\rm offspring}/n). With stochastic (N), the **occupied** population trait-mean covariance is

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(\bar Z\mid N>0)
=\Sigma_{\rm offspring}\,E[1/N\mid N>0].
\]

The extinction probability is exactly (e^{-R}), given the stated restricted case. Trait means are **undefined following extinction** and are not assigned numeric zero.

The module `scripts/audit_model3_stochastic_bridge.py` computes this kernel analytically; `tests/test_model3_stochastic_bridge.py` calls the **original** Model 3 `advance()` to test it with independent seeded draws. `draw_gaussian_trait_surrogate` additionally samples an **Euler-style, conditional Gaussian one-step candidate** using the derived covariance divided by the actual sampled number of offspring and preserving explicit `N=0` extinction. The test checks its matched moments against the derived target. It is *not yet a calibrated continuous-time SDE*, nor does moment agreement validate distributional tails or guarantee shape-bounded states.

## Stage 2: genotype-frequency noise and trait-space SPDE feasibility

On a small **exact fixed allele support**, canonical parents and Mendelian gamete distributions give next-offspring genotype probabilities (q_i). Conditional on (N=n>0), a finite offspring cohort has categorical sampling noise

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(\hat q_i,\hat q_j\mid N=n)
=\frac{q_i\mathbf1_{i=j}-q_iq_j}{n}.
\]

The off-diagonal correlations are in general **negative**; the covariance rows sum to zero and conserve total mass. Independent white noise on each genotype would violate this constraint. `draw_exact_frequency` provides a nonnegative multinomial representation of this *restricted* stochastic genotype-density step. In contrast, `gaussian_frequency_boundary_risk` computes rigorous lower and union-bound upper probabilities that unconstrained Gaussian noise with exactly that covariance yields a **negative genotype frequency**. This boundary diagnostic tests one necessary SPDE feasibility condition; it is **not** an SPDE solver and is not an independent biological model.

### Feasibility verdict criteria

- **PASS, analytical one-step precursor:** exact Mendelian mean and covariance agree with exact genotype distribution on the same small support (numerical tolerance 1e−12); mass-conserving covariance is PSD to 1e−11 and has zero row sums; finite ABM seeded Monte Carlo agrees with analytic occupied mean, covariance and extinction under fixed engineering tolerances.
- **NOT YET ADMISSIBLE, full SDE:** to move from the discrete one-step kernel to a continuous-time SDE, derive and check the scaling of time and population size, deal explicitly with (N=0) and capacity boundaries, extend conditional moments to age-structured survival and incoming seeds, and incorporate the actual mutation kernel without changing it.
- **NOT YET ADMISSIBLE, full trait-space SPDE:** stochastic field equations must preserve normalization/nonnegativity and full nonlocal sexual inheritance, retain cross-locus correlations, handle extinction and finite populations, and match the ABM in a population-size-refinement experiment. A first/second-moment Gaussian field with unconstrained additive noise is insufficient. Positive-mutation 3-locus deterministic grid fidelity must also be resolved or separately bounded.
- **STOP:** no automatic extension to geographical space, no INLA data fit, no publication claim of a complete Model 3 SDE/SPDE, no post-outcome relaxation of numerical thresholds and no updating already frozen ecological outcomes.

## Additional three-update validation: genotype-count Markov reference

`genotype_counts_to_canonical_state` reconstructs distinct diploid individuals
from their full-joint three-locus genotype **counts** on exactly the original
allele support; it never computes only three trait means or imposes linkage
equilibrium. `genotype_count_markov_step` then calls the **unmodified** Model 3
`reproduce()` to preserve individual-level self-pollen exclusion, obtains
the exact Mendelian offspring genotype law (q), samples the correct
capped-Poisson recruited census and multinomial offspring counts, and allows
extinction to be absorbing (no untracked immigrants).

Because there is no survival, mutation, immigration or individual-specific
fitness beyond genotype, genotype counts are a *sufficient Markov state* for
this restricted experiment. The new three-update test independently
compares its endpoint recruitment, extinction and surviving phenotype means
with repeated canonical `advance()` rollouts. This is an exact stochastic
**discrete-time alternative realization of the restricted Model 3**, not an
independently supported ecological mechanism and not an SDE or SPDE.
The comparison is invalid once age structure, new mutation or immigration is
turned on unless corresponding state variables/transitions are explicitly added.

This prevents a misleading shortcut: a mean-trait-only SDE would lose
diploid variation and parentage information, so matching a one-step mean
alone cannot certify its multi-generation validity. A full stochastic
genotype-frequency limit must preserve the complete offspring distribution
and the nonlinear reproductive map.

## What this actually tests

The new result can establish a *consistent stochastic-noise construction under restrictive assumptions*, including uncertainty and extinction probability missing from a deterministic density model. It cannot automatically attribute the existing finite-versus-density gap to drift: the full Model 3 pair additionally differs in pollen self-exclusion and density regulation. Nor would a future SPDE be an external independent biological-model validation if it inherits Model 3's same mating kernel.

## 2026-10-09 next stage: canonical mutation, 1/N noise, multi-update conditional audit

- New numerical diagnostic: `scripts/audit_model3_stochastic_mutation_scaling.py`. For every transmitted allele value `x`, it computes the first two moments of the **original** birth mutation `Y = x` with probability `1-u`, otherwise `Y=reflect(x+N(0,sigma²))`. The one-dimensional normal integrals are split at reflection cusps and numerically evaluated; their omitted 10-standard-deviation tails have total Gaussian probability below approximately 1.6e−23. This is quadrature of the **original jump process**, not a replacement with the heat PDE.
- Combining the per-allele moments with independent Mendelian segregation and the **full maternal/paternal pairing matrix** yields the exact *one-birth* 3×3 trait mean/covariance, within floating-point quadrature precision. It retains genotype mixing correlations and the `assurance_mode=fixed` mutation prohibition. The founder/visitor reproductive operator remains unchanged.
- New sampling scaling: for **fixed child genotype probabilities** `q` and **fixed realized census** `N>0`, `Cov(frequencies | N)=(diag(q)-q q.T)/N`. The tests compare the exact 1/N law to independent multinomial draws at N=8,16,32,64,128 and measure the Gaussian negative-frequency boundary risk. **This is NOT a claim that increasing Model 3 carrying capacity by itself scales demographic noise exactly as 1/N**: changing capacity changes the reproductive payoff denominator, and survival/fecundity may vary.

### What remains blocked

A full process-level SDE/SPDE needs a **valid stand-alone inherited-state closure**, not just correct conditional moments on observed ABM states. A mean-only three-trait diffusion loses full diploid segregation, self-pollen exclusion, rare allele retention, state-dependent extinction, and the nonlocal sexual inheritance operator. The mutation-enabled three-locus deterministic density solver also has unresolved grid convergence. Therefore any claim that the candidate matches long-run mutation-enabled ABM evolution remains **not established**, regardless of these source-conditioned passes.

## SPDE feasibility counterexample: positive simplex noise is not enough

An attractive frequency-valued stochastic surrogate is `Dirichlet((N-1)*q)`. At fixed realized `N>=2`, it stays on the nonnegative simplex and matches the exact finite multinomial mean `q` and covariance `[diag(q)-q q.T]/N` exactly. **But it has a different support and cannot remove a rare genotype whose expected probability is positive.**

For a rare genotype at `q=0.02`, and `N=8` new individuals, the true finite multinomial transition loses it with probability `(1-0.02)^8 = 0.85076...`; the moment-matched Dirichlet transition loses it with probability **zero**. Even a noise model that solves positivity and covariance can miss the central finite genetic-memory mechanism. We treat this mismatch as a *negative feasibility gate* for any naive positive continuous-density SPDE, not as evidence that all SPDE formulations are impossible. A discrete allele-loss boundary or full frequency-valued measure process is needed to represent the exact finite model.

Numerical test: `test_simplex_covariance_matching_cannot_reproduce_rare_genotype_loss` and preflight JSON `simplex_noise_genetic_loss_diagnostic` using fixed `q`, `N`, a seeded 10,000-draw diagnostic. This is **not** a new ecological visitor-history replicate or a simulation of the full genetic-evolution model.


## Dynamic-support measure-valued stochastic bridge: process-level holdout

The earlier one-step conditional moment checks reveal where a Gaussian SDE **candidate** agrees with finite Model 3 moment targets. They do not propagate genetics. To establish a *stand-alone finite stochastic reference*, the new `scripts/audit_model3_stochastic_measure.py` keeps a **full joint diploid 3-locus empirical genotype measure**: canonicalized allele pairs and integer multiplicities. It evolves on its **own** across generations, and when mutation creates novel continuously valued alleles, they are retained exactly: **no predefined genotype lattice, no zero-frequency fractional reservoirs**.

The transition deliberately reconstructs distinct individuals for every genotype copy before calling canonical `reproduce()`, preserving finite individual pollen self-exclusion, then generates capped-Poisson offspring, parental pair selection, canonical `inherit()` and the original Bernoulli/reflected-Gaussian mutation. No Model 3 source was edited. Exact one-step pathwise tests compare its full genotype measure with `advance()` from identical expanded starting states and identical dedicated RNG streams. Multigeneration tests propagate the measure from its **own prior state**, without any canonical ABM feed-back, and preserve genotype multiplicities, mutation support and absorbing extinction.

Scope: complete annual turnover, no seed immigration, no pedigree or ancestry statistics (those identifiers are deliberately not retained); any ageing, gene-flow, survivor trait or lineage output requires a richer state. A full process limit with drift and extinction is **not** supplied. The measure-valued chain is still a second **representation of the same finite ABM stochastic law**, not an independent biological theory and not a continuous-time SDE/SPDE.

An executable old-history holdout, `scripts/run_model3_stochastic_measure_preflight.py --out measure-preflight.json --draws 512 --updates 3`, compares two **independently sampled** 3-update endpoints under exactly the same archived visitor history `26110601`. It checks occupancy, population size and conditional-on-survival trait means and archives provenance. It uses nested demographic samples, not new independent visitor histories. Comparison against the full biological model is limited to compatible observable moments, not genealogy or mutation flags; broad CI and focused CI must both pass before promotion.

**Implication for SPDE design:** The valid reference object is a random genotype **measure**, with an absorbing zero-mass state and atoms that can actually disappear. A mean-only Gaussian diffusion and a strictly-positive Dirichlet field cannot carry all of the finite Mendelian memory. Further approximations must be explicit about atomicity, boundary fluxes, nonlocal mating and birth-time mutation; an apparently smooth `SPDE` that loses these is scientifically non-equivalent.
