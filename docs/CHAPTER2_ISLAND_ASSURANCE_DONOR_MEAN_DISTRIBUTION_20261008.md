# Inherited assurance: population-mean versus allele-distribution effects (2026-10-08)

**Status:** Completed, **post-outcome explanatory** assay on the existing 16 independent visitor histories. The original prospectively frozen allele-transplant hypothesis **FAILED** and remains failed. This is not a new confirmatory experiment or a mediation estimate.

## Current scientific question

Post-establishment island pollinator history changes inherited reproductive assurance and floral investment. The full paired-diploid allele swap in the independent16 campaign showed that transplanting far-history assurance alleles into near-history populations can increase immediate viable maternal output and, in a specified synthetic small-population bottleneck, later occupancy. Its prespecified test of whether this occupancy effect was stronger under pollen discount than prior selfing was inconclusive: **+0.140625 [−0.078125, +0.359375], FAIL**.

The missing mechanistic distinction is whether the far-donor result requires the **shape and ancestry of the allele distribution**, or is already closely approximated by the **difference in population-mean assurance**.

## Matched interventions

Each of the original 16 visitor histories was rerun for exactly 400 prehistory updates under both original near/far visitor regimes and the **correct independent16 demographic repeat 35101801**, not the earlier four-history repeat. Two recipient genetic backgrounds × four intervention variants were crossed with two new postvisitor regimes and both stress budgets (3, 4), for **512 genetic states and 2,048 poststress trajectories**.

- **Native:** unmodified recipient diploid genotype, ancestry and other loci.
- **Full donor:** rank-aligned diploid donor assurance locus with allele origin/flags, keeping recipient investment and matching.
- **Recipient mean target:** keep recipient A-locus rank/allele origin and use a monotone bounded affine shift of A allele values to match the far-donor **population mean**.
- **Centered donor distribution:** rank-transfer the donor A locus, then apply a bounded affine shift returning its mean to the original recipient mean.

All future visitor histories and demographic RNG are matched within a setting/history/background/stress condition. The bounded affine operation has an exact target mean but **compresses or otherwise changes the allele variance**. This assay therefore **cannot** identify a pure mean-versus-variance causal mediation, and the centered donor also changes cross-locus genotype associations.

## Reproduction from correct independent16 seeds

An initial technical run mistakenly imported the earlier exploratory demographic-repeat seed, giving mismatched native/full controls. That entire run was rejected; the runner was corrected to repeat seed **35101801** and all cases were recomputed.

After correction, **256 native/full-donor genetic states matched every comparable original archived measurement at absolute tolerance 1e−12, with zero mismatches** for immediate viable maternal output, female outcross, pollen export, terminal occupancy and terminal population size.

All 512 new states and 2,048 postshock outcomes were completed and archived. The interpretive result is based **only** on the corrected run.

## Current explanatory result

Near-history recipient background; all differences are against the native recipient.

| Reproductive rule | Full donor A: viable maternal | Mean-target A: viable maternal | Full donor A: occupancy | Mean-target A: occupancy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Delayed control | +0.1382 | +0.1381 | +0.2969 | +0.2344 |
| Prior selfing | +0.1228 | +0.1227 | +0.2031 | +0.2188 |
| Pollen discount | +0.2159 | +0.2159 | +0.3125 | +0.3125 |
| Direct assurance cost | +0.0192 | +0.0196 | +0.0000 | +0.0156 |

For **far-history recipients**, shifting assurance toward the near-donor mean reversed the direction of viable maternal output. The full-versus-mean-target effects remained closely aligned: delayed −0.1890/−0.1890; prior −0.1535/−0.1534; pollen discount −0.2620/−0.2620; assurance cost −0.0609/−0.0609.

**Crucial:** in all eight setting × recipient backgrounds, the centered-donor transfer (donor allele distribution with the recipient's original A mean restored) changed **immediate viable maternal output** by less than 0.0005 model units. Its 80-update **occupancy** contrast, however, varied in sign, from about −0.078 to +0.063 under the fixed poststress grid. The mean-targeted perturbation also modified A allele variance, sometimes substantially, so do not infer that genetic variance is dispensable or that all persistence is generated by a mean-only mechanism.

History-level visitor-unit bootstrap summaries, including female outcross and pollen export, are in the local raw/summary archive.

## Interpretation and implications for island ecology

At fixed contemporaneous pollination, the declared reproductive accounting predicts immediate viable output primarily from the current distribution of reproductive assurance, investment and costs. An allele-mean targeted intervention can closely reproduce the **instantaneous maternal return** of full donor substitution in these settings. This is a useful closure diagnostic and partly follows from the model equations; it is **not novel evidence that natural selfing systems lack genomic constraints**.

The incomplete match in long-term occupancy is unsurprising: finite stochastic reproduction, recruitment, genotype segregation, future mutation, demographic thresholds and altered within-population genotype associations intervene between expected first-generation payoff and final persistence. These are still intertwined, not fully causally partitioned here.

The broader island question therefore cannot be answered solely by present-day selfing capacity or flower size. In independent natural-island systems, one would need breeding-system assays, viable maternal seeds, pollen-export / outcross male fitness, population genetic distribution, effective pollinator function, and longitudinal persistence. Existing synthetic near/far histories are visitor-functional-type replenishment controls, not calibrated island distances.
