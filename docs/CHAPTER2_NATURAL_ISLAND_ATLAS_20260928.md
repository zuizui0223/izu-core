# Islands as natural experiments: Model 3 to observed island systems

Date: 2026-09-28. Retrospective synthesis of already locked evidence. No model parameter was fitted and no new outcome cohort was opened.

## Ecological argument

The chapter asks how geographic isolation and assembly history become a plant response. Pollination supplies measurable intermediate steps; islands supply repeated contrasts in connectivity, mutualist continuity and establishment history. The three-stage argument is:

**Repeated synthetic islands separate mechanisms → global observed communities establish that contrasting contexts exist → Izu tests the measured functional links and exposes where they fail to propagate uniformly.**

Chapter 1 motivates the question through recurrent multivariate responses and context-dependent floral composition. Its corrected H2 associations do not identify direct selection after selfing adjustment, and H4 is not historical mediation. The chapter therefore does not claim to reconstruct those four regional histories. The investigator's source-bound motivation and corrected baseline are documented in `CHAPTER2_CH1_MOTIVATION_AUDIT_20260928.md`.

## Ecological interpretation and next falsifiable link

Islands allow a distinction between lack of mutualist opportunity, the identities and traits of the partners that do arrive, and the history and size of the plant population that responds. Fixed assurance capacity in Model 3 separates investment change from evolution of that capacity, but does not remove selfing or freeze realized selfing. Investment costs are not made larger by isolation; the return relative to the same cost changes within the declared reproductive model.

For a quantitative natural-island prediction, the missing quantities are plant population and genetic state, direct effectiveness/reproductive dependency and inherited temporal change measured in a linked design. Present observations locate ecological contexts and test partial functional links. They do not validate the 200-year model trajectories. Izu is selected for measurement continuity, not proximity to Atami, representativeness or a guaranteed match.
