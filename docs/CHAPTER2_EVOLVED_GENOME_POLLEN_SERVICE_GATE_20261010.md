# Original evolved genomes: does assurance-associated investment change reduce shared pollen service?

**10 October 2026 — original frozen biological histories reanalysed. Result: partial mechanism support, no population-level damage identified.**

This is a narrow post-discovery audit of the *actual original 2026-10-06 four-setting evolutionary experiment*. It is NOT a new independent preregistered study, genetic causal mediation, new island comparison, nor a demographic persistence result.

## Stage 1: observed evolutionary differences versus a within-genome investment clamp

For each history and reproductive setting, we read the same t400 original **fixed- and evolving-assurance plant allele checkpoints** and the identical external visitor context. In the **evolving** diploid population only, we artificially reset every plant's investment alleles to the *mean investment of that history's matched fixed-assurance population*, leaving evolving assurance, matching, population size and all source visitor types unchanged. We then reran **one original source reproductive ledger**.

This is a static within-genome intervention identifying whether the observed investment state matters for the source model's pollen-transfer rate. **It is not mediation** of the original longitudinal assurance treatment; the fixed and evolving branches have different genomes, parents and matching distributions. An alternative source assay resets only assurance alleles to the common founder value a=0.5 at fixed investment. When pollen_discount=0, this assurance reset changes expected pollen delivery by exactly **zero**, as required by the original biological equations.

At **t400, higher-replenishment near** (64 histories × **one** of the eight original demographic repeats):

| Original reproductive setting | Evolving − fixed mean investment | Evolving − I-restored total delivered pollen | Histories with negative within-E receipt contrast | Evolving − I-restored total viable maternal seeds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cost-free delayed selfing | −0.01829 | **−1.35285** | 33/64 | **−0.24292** |
| Prior selfing | −0.01652 | **−1.38631** | 37/64 | **+2.47235** |
| Pollen discount | −0.04401 | **−0.68898** | 37/64 | **+1.76051** |
| Direct assurance cost | −0.03672 | **−1.80055** | 37/64 | **+0.88272** |

Thus the original evolved genomes have, on average, lower floral investment near and lower total pollen transfer than if their investments were artificially reset to the partner fixed-branch investment means. **But the sign is not history-universal**. Most importantly, **net viable maternal seeds are greater with the original evolved investment in 3 of 4 source settings**: lower investment also saves ovule-allocation resources, interacts with autonomous selfing, and is not equivalent to fewer viable descendants. The original near/far actual-genome delivery difference cannot be partitioned causally by simply subtracting these arm means.

At t400, **50/64 far visitor-history populations have zero visitor types**, so in those source states changing investment cannot change pollen receipt. This mechanistic null is preserved, not excluded as missing ecological data. The separate t200 records are retained to show that early and middle time signs can differ; they are NOT an evolutionary equilibrium test.

## Stage 2: unilateral investment of an actual evolved adult benefits OTHER mothers

In each original evolving-assurance plant population at t400, we increased **ONE native focal plant's investment alleles by +0.002**, or took an admissible signed −0.002 derivative at an allele boundary, while holding **every other genotype and the identical historical visitor community** fixed. We measured the original one-generation reproductive ledger for the **other mothers**, not the focal mother. This directly tests a within-population pollen-mediated externality on **genuinely evolved diploid source genomes**, rather than only on artificial monomorphic eight-plant fixtures.

For near, each setting has **3,072 focal interventions (64 history states × 48 adult focal plants)**:

| Reproductive setting | Mean marginal effect on OTHER mothers' viable seeds | Positive / negative / numerical zero focals | Histories with positive mean nonfocal effect | Mean focal mother's OWN viable-seed derivative |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Cost-free delayed | **+0.67068** | 3024 / 0 / 48 | 63/64 | −0.88233 |
| Prior selfing | **+0.26905** | 2976 / 0 / 96 | 62/64 | −0.94766 |
| Pollen discount | **+0.39121** | 3024 / 0 / 48 | 63/64 | −0.78054 |
| Assurance cost | **+0.60789** | 3024 / 0 / 48 | 63/64 | −0.49219 |

This source demonstration is stronger than simply seeing a negative focal selection coefficient with positive group seed effect: it measures an **explicit nonfocal seed benefit under an isolated one-parent investment intervention**. It survives four original mating-system settings and real evolved genotypes, including the setting where other mothers' viable selfed seeds decline because their outcross fertilization improves. Source far has 50 zero-visitor histories and exactly zero nonfocal pollen externality in all those population states.

**But it does NOT establish evolutionary underinvestment, community-maintained pollinator abundance, a naturally inherited selection coefficient, or extinction.** Multiple plant perturbations within each visitor history are correlated. A negative *focal maternal* derivative need not equal negative total parental allele fitness, because the focal individual's paternal outcross contribution must be included. Group viable seed is not census recruitment or occupancy. Only one repeat/history was evaluated here; the original four-setting result used eight repeats and a different preregistered estimand.

## High-upside hypothesis: verdict after the first causal test

**Status: PARTIAL source support, causal chain NOT identified.** We verified the exact original plant-by-visitor reproductive architecture, observed investment differences across fixed and evolving arms, measured a within-E pollen-transfer intervention, and directly measured nonfocal maternal benefits in actual evolved genomes. But **the strongest claimed end-to-end mechanism is not verified**:

`evolving assurance → inherited investment reduction → nonfocal pollen loss → lower GROUP viable reproduction → worse future population persistence`

The above results do not identify the first arrow as a valid genetic mediation coefficient, and the fourth arrow is directly contradicted as a universal short-term implication by 3/4 settings' increase in total viable maternal seeds under source-level lower investment. The original evolution primary had occupancy 1.0 in every arm; its field-equivalent survival cost is neither estimated nor available through those histories. The separate 80-update order/K study is not the same genetic histories or mediator.

**Decision:** Do **not** retitle the single Ecology Letters manuscript around an "evolutionary tragedy of floral public goods" or a general survival cost on these data. Retain the confirmed no-necessity/compression effect as its main claim. Any attempt to promote the stronger hypothesis now requires a frozen, independently justified same-genome multi-stage intervention with a nonsaturated occupancy/growth outcome. Preserve settings where the group viable seed loss fails.
