## Material integration checks identified in the pre-integration Article

At the reviewed baseline `d2c24cea`, the text already retained original Q1 versus fresh-founder transport, 51/64 versus30/64 order, finite/density mismatch, lower-support loss versus the separate high-I target, the pooled S33 persistence result, and the lack of established private/public extinction conflict. These are retained findings, not omissions.

The following were identified for explicit main-text qualification and/or supplementary routing. The accompanying current supplement now addresses all six groups; this list preserves why that integration was necessary:

1. **Original environmental interpretation (S2/F2).** The original near and far regimes are both pollen limited. The new baseline factorial has a genuinely high-outcross arm under its own protocol; it cannot retroactively make the original near arm pollen sufficient. The failed saturation grid and nonstationary separate long-horizon audits remain available.
2. **Persistence is supported selectively (S3/S33/F7).** The current broad statement that imposed expression schedules mostly lacked a general survival effect should retain the positive fixed-B48 capacity/viability interaction, its different estimand, resource-window failure, early inconclusive K/B tests and inconclusive timed-viability result. S33 is pooled across settings; its prior-selfing interval includes zero. None supplies a Q1-to-Q4 mediated chain.
3. **Access results include unsuccessful generalization (S14/S17/S23/S26/S30).** The current structural-support account needs a direct supplementary pointer to both failed registered ecological-order reversal tests, the unresolved original restoration primary, the positive conditional fixed-state follow-up, and the distribution contrast that did not replicate direction across founders. Leaving only structural losses and conditional restoration successes would overstate mechanistic closure.
4. **Mutation can reopen support without completing response (S30/S31).** The current discussion correctly rejects universal irreversibility, but the historical renewal result supplies a concrete constraint: most reopened histories did not complete the sustained I event, and nonresponse was not uniformly a selected decrease blocked by noise. These results bound, rather than establish, a recovery clock.
5. **Order, state and time are distinct endpoints (S5/S12/S18–S20/S25/S27/S28/F5/F8).** Retain all six order categories, tempo occupancy ceilings, founder-dependent natural-order associations, threshold sensitivity, and endpoint/duration sign reversals. These prevent reading order agreement or endpoint survival as a universal adaptive advantage. F8's fixed-clone experiment must remain separate from S12's inheriting finite-kernel experiment.
6. **Mechanistic and verification limits (S4/S6/S9–S13/S15/S16/S22/S29/S32).** Source accounting and exact technical identities strengthen auditability but do not isolate a unique natural mediator. Original runtime/annual-genome gaps, the limited event-audit scope and outstanding external-deposit status cannot be replaced by a blanket independent-reproduction statement. Post-outcome far-side retention remains mechanistically unresolved.

Supplement consolidation may group related sections, but must preserve their cohort identities, original decisions and source links.
