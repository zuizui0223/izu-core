## One central question and one answer

**Question:** *Why does pollination-dependent reproductive selection not determine a single realized trajectory of floral investment and reproductive assurance?*

**Answer supported **within one synthetic Model 3 family**:** Visitor limitation changes maternal/paternal reproductive returns and can directly favour lower floral investment without requiring the evolution of reproductive assurance. Evolving assurance then changes the **magnitude** of between-environment floral differentiation, but the order and realization of specific phenotypic events are further filtered by accessible inherited alleles and finite parent/Mendelian transmission. Separately controlled demographic experiments show that the consequences for persistence depend on reproductive preparation, viability conditions and demographic capacity. These are **connected biological questions examined using different experimental cohorts**, **not** one prospectively confirmed Q1→Q4 mediation pathway.

**One-sentence ecological contribution:** *A recurrent pollination constraint need not generate a uniform floral response because reproductive selection, genetically reachable responses, their finite transmission, and population persistence are distinct ecological-evolutionary filters.*

The strongest conceptual novelty candidate is **a concrete model-specific demonstration of the genetic-transmission filter after maternal/paternal/selfing reproductive weighting**, NOT discovering genetic drift, selfing syndromes, phenotypic countergradient compensation, or density effects for the first time. The strongest **prospectively confirmed** experimental evidence is still the four-setting Q1 fixed/evolving-assurance intervention. **Strength of evidence and size of conceptual novelty must be kept separate.**

**Proposed English working title (not adopted in the active manuscript yet):**
*From reproductive selection to floral evolution: genetic accessibility and finite transmission under pollinator limitation.*

The currently adopted title, *Reproductive assurance compresses floral-investment divergence under pollinator limitation*, accurately describes a confirmed Q1 result but does not fully describe the increasingly central Q3 mechanism. A final retitle requires matching abstract, title/word limits, actual figure assets, reviewer packet and tests together; a story note alone does not authorize submission.

## Complete biological story in four acts

### Q1 — The ecological environment changes reproductive payoffs before evolution

A change in visitor functional-type replenishment reduces maternal and paternal outcross returns to floral investment I; its intrinsic resource cost is unchanged. On fixed plants, the delayed/costly case's I-return diagnostic changes **+0.5793 near → −0.7004 far**, with the outcross component **+1.6523 → +0.0854**. This is a source-model mechanism, not measured natural visitation.

**Prospectively confirmed causal intervention:** with assurance capacity A *fixed at 0.5*, far-minus-near evolved I is negative across **four registered mating settings**, −0.4413, −0.3018, −0.3305 and −0.4337 (64 independent visitor histories × eight nested demographic repeats). Permitting A evolution reduces that environment contrast by **+0.1605, +0.2200, +0.1915, +0.1007**, all original history-bootstrap intervals excluding zero. This means *A evolution is not necessary for I decline in the declared regimes*, though realized selfing still occurs at fixed A.

A evolution has a larger *additional effect relative to fixed A* in the **near** arm, accounting algebraically for 78–90% of the divergence compression. This is **not** an overall larger investment decline near: total I decline remains greater far. The near arm was itself pollen-limited (fixed-state outcross fertilization ~22% versus far ~1%), **not a fully pollinated mainland**. Cost-free delayed selfing structurally favours some assurance when unfertilized ovules remain; the direct A-cost setting supplies the more discriminating control.

**Role in the main paper:** establish biological payoff and the counterfactual necessity test, not advertise already known selfing-selection relaxation as a world-first finding.

### Q2 — A favourable direction does not specify a universal realized order

In the independently repeated delayed-selfing/direct-A-cost regime, the 0.05 event maintained 20 censuses classified **51/64** visitor histories as A-first; a separate prior-selfing regime classified **30/64** that way. The definition of “first” and allele accessibility matter. A-first can coexist with direct selection against investment while A is genetically frozen; earlier change is **not causal necessity**.

The separate 2,304 original *fixed genetic access* ecological-order histories do not identify a reproducible supply/composition-driven reversal: **all 18 simultaneous intervals include zero**. The completed distinct 2,304 original-I-mutation renewed paths and independent replays also did **not** supply a replicated reversal. These are not equivalence proofs. Artificial F1 co-access and static high-W double-trait states can clarify segregation versus immediate payoff (S29), **not observed population sequence**.

**Role in the main paper:** show the mismatch between prospective reproductive opportunity and an inherited event clock; avoid turning threshold definitions or null ecological-order tests into the leading discovery.

### Q3 — The concrete finite-genetic bottleneck: expected parental return is not guaranteed transmitted allele access

In the original native source reproduction ledger, individual parent genotypes are weighted by **W_i = 0.5 F_i + 0.5 P_i + S_i**. Their conditional expected next-generation I response is the Price identity **Cov(I,W)/mean(W)**; then only finitely many parental pairs and Mendelian homologs are actually retained. The Price identity is standard mathematics; the source-specific inherited-access readout makes its consequences directly inspectable.

**Original no-I-mutation/no-immigration cohort, 2,304 histories (not 2,304 independent islands):** a specified *new future* founder-relative −0.05 I event became genetically unreachable in **703 histories before full I fixation**, with expressed I variation still present, and in **535 histories at fixation**. These sum to 1,238 selected first-exclusion transitions within those same 2,304 histories; one of the 703 had *already* completed an earlier response event, so “future new event impossible” is not retroactive nonresponse.

At the 1,238 selected first-exclusion transitions, the conditional **expected frequency of the required low-I allele class increased** in **315** and decreased in **923**, but the realized next generation retained **zero** such copies in both groups. Within the selected 315 favourable-weighting transitions, the median source state had only **2 required copies among 96 parental homologs**, and the original parent/Mendelian lottery assigned median **0.326** probability of missing that class among 48 recruited offspring. This is a **conditional source-state probability given a selected loss transition**, not the prospective loss rate among all populations or proof of neutral drift overriding positive invasion fitness.

The separate original-mutation finite versus conditional density study predicted A-first in two low-visitor/matched contexts, but only **3/32** and **2/32** corresponding finite ABM histories realized that *threshold-defined* event, with **32/32 occupied in each**. Both backgrounds still showed I decline. The older separate prospective 128-visitor-history zero-mutation bridge yielded density versus ABM mean far–near I **−0.4510 versus −0.1446**; it is a different design, and **density is not the exact stochastic expectation of finite ABM**. These studies cannot be numerically pooled into a “drift contribution”.

Adding I mutation in a distinct source-cohort reopened support in **914** cases, but **758** of those renewed cases still did not complete the defined I event; reopening genetic access is **not sufficient** to guarantee a threshold event. Cohort and event denominators require the source S30–S31 separation.

**Additional source replay from PR #540 (first of eight original demographic repeats, original authentic genomes at 0/200/400/1000):** far evolved-minus-fixed I is already positive at t200 in all four mating settings, whereas t400 one-step expected Price-response contrast is negative in all four. At t400, 50/64 far histories had no visitor type; the source-native within-genome A–I weighting/alignment is small and setting-dependent. This does **not** identify why the full 1,000-step far difference appears; in particular, neither a local β sign nor the zero-visitor A–I alignment alone is a complete dynamical mediator.

**Role in the main paper:** THIS is the high-information biological hinge. Put an actual **reproductive W → expected allele frequency e → finite offspring transmission → remaining reachable I event** visualization in the main figure sequence after source/denominator audit. The result is conditional and model-specific, but positive and mechanistic, not just “stochastic and deterministic simulations differ”.

### Q4 — Demographic persistence depends on the resulting reproductive state, but the natural order-to-survival chain is not identified

**Separate independently frozen common-future history test:** 32 newly generated visitor histories, four mating settings, 400-step high/low pollinator prehistories, controlled A evolution, and a fixed pilot-informed K8/ovule-budget 2/3/4/5 × future visitor factorial for 80 updates yielded pooled historical-survival interaction **+0.1083984** (95% history-bootstrap **[+0.06543,+0.15137]**). This is a **supported conditional evolutionary-history-by-A-evolvability effect** under synthetic demographic stress and not a general island rescue law or an identified Q3 genetic mediator. It remains distinct from the four-setting main 1,000-update experiment.

**Separate prospective demographic-size moderator:** with 64 fresh visitor-history clusters and matched eight founders at constant pollen background **B48**, the registered K8−K48 interaction in sensitivity to halving viable selfed offspring under **imposed A-first/I-first phenotypic expression schedules** is **+0.0077457** occupancy probability (95% history-bootstrap **[+0.0024972,+0.0130155]**). This is only **0.775 percentage points**, not a survival effect of naturally evolved chronological order. The original geographic-order result was practically equivalent, an independent budget-window test failed, and late/early timing remained inconclusive.

**Role in the main paper:** say that inherited reproductive states can matter demographically **under stated conditions**, not that the particular Q3 transmission losses caused natural extinctions or that A-first always rescues small islands. Present the pooled history test as a *separate conditional supporting finding*, not an unregistered second primary claim of the main four-setting paper.

## Ch1 → Ch2 connection: question, not an inferred historical causal bridge

The active [island Chapter 1 manuscript](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/blob/main/submission/chapter1_current/MANUSCRIPT.md), source contract [chapter1_submission_current.json](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/blob/main/config/chapter1_submission_current.json), reports:

- 8,264-island geographic universe, 4,379 observed H1 islands, 106,295 accepted trait-matched taxa; self-compatibility increases with island isolation in **four regional analyses** across broad and WCVP regional-native-compatible scopes. **Other selfing, flower form, tube and colour responses are not uniformly supported**; H1 primary pointwise tests are not a global multiple-testing-adjusted “island flower syndrome”.
- Independently published GloPL 2,969 pollen-supplementation experiments: corrected isolation versus pollen limitation **β=+0.09191**, finite-publication **p=0.01594**; supplemental-only sensitivity is unsupported.
- H2 accessible floral forms have conditional regional support; H4 exact-species trait-to-current-limitation associations are **post hoc functional compatibility**, not causal mediation.
- **83.65%** of species-on-island origin-status rows remain unresolved. Contemporary floristic composition **cannot identify** immigration filtering, establishment, within-lineage evolution or extinction history.

**Permissible bridge:** Ch1 asks why reproductive functions recur geographically while detailed floral phenotypes are contingent; Ch2 tests distinct mechanisms that *could* allow selection and realized response to decouple. **Not permissible:** treat synthetic visitor replenishment as measured coastline distance, synthetic I as literal flower colour/nectar guide, or the Ch1 cross-sectional flora patterns as independent confirmation of Model 3 historical Q3/Q4 processes.

**Novel, field-testable predictions (NOT yet observations):** matched plant lineages exposed to comparably limited pollination may differ in future floral change because their available alleles and parentage-weighted gamete transmission differ; longitudinal floral traits, pollen deposition, outcross/selfed paternity, segregating loci, and recruited offspring could test the predicted disconnect. Natural colonization/survivorship sorting must be separated using appropriate island/species records.
