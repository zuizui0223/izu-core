## Source-based identification argument after completed admission

This argument concerns the admitted common200-update challenge only, not an arbitrary age-structured or environmentally autocorrelated model. Keep the same ordered diploid allele matrix, census, valid birth years, time, visitors, configuration, mutation masks and future random-number streams. Denote these future-relevant inputs by S and U; H is a past preparation or sequence label. The admitted future program has no H argument in reproduction, recruitment, parental sampling, segregation or mutation. In `reproduce_kb`, traits come from the ordered allele matrix; IDs supply only census length. In `advance`, resident ages/ID intersections are validity checks and reporting; survival is zero and seed candidates are empty here. In `inherit`, ancestry and mutation flags transmit, but do not determine fitness, parent probabilities or mutation probabilities. The future RNG recipe uses block/replicate, not origin.

Thus, for identical valid S and U and either historical annotation H or H', each next allele/census state is identical. Induction gives identical future allele/census paths and occupancy: Y(S,H,U)=Y(S,H',U). The eight paired technical checks confirm the implemented instance; source dependence, not extrapolation from eight effect replicates, supplies the general conditional argument under this law. No original genetic states are asserted equal. The theorem concerns demographic/genetic dynamics, not equality of ancestor labels, parent ID strings or unmeasured natural-island history.

Assigned paired mutation-access schedule Z, realized response order R, inherited state S and future occupancy Y are different variables. Z changes the past trajectory, which determines both R and S; S then changes Y. R is a threshold/sustained-window summary of that trajectory, not an input to the future transition. The existing controlled paired schedule comparison can identify a total access-policy effect on Y. It does not identify a unique effect of naturally realized R: schedules also change initial assurance level, allele variance/association and other traits without those differences being encoded by the order label. Equalizing complete S would block these state-mediated effects by construction. Equalizing only A mean leaves them; removing all parent skew is a different later intervention.

Consequently, conditional annotation invariance cannot establish that actual evolutionary history is useless, nor that naturally evolving assurance first lacks benefit. It establishes the precise remaining identification gap: a historical pathway may benefit future persistence through the state it produces, but current contrasts do not uniquely assign that benefit to its threshold-defined order. No additional biological histories or changed generator are needed for this conditional argument, and it does not close the empirical ecological reversal/root or pure-order-benefit requirements.
