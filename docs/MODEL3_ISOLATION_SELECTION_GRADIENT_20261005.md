# Selection along a visitor-arrival isolation gradient

Exploratory diagnostic, declared before calculation. No plant evolution or stopped high-grid PDE computation was restarted. Existing arrival/loss, pollen and reproductive rules were retained. Thirteen normalized distances0..3,64 paired history seeds, snapshots0/200/400,45 fixed monomorphic states and the two primary reproductive settings produced449,280 gradient values. Distances change continuing arrival, not a prescribed flower optimum.

At snapshot400, mean capacity selection was already positive at distance0 in all45 states in both settings; none left that sign along the sampled gradient. Investment reduction was already favoured in9/45 delayed/costly states and22/45 prior/no-cost states. Every state favoured reduced investment at some distance. This result separates stronger selection from newly arising selection. The tested plants need not wait for isolation to favour greater capacity; isolation can strengthen a pre-existing gradient while changing the sign of investment selection. It does not establish which trait first changes in inherited finite populations. Snapshot averages are not long-run invasion exponents in fluctuating environments. No phylogenetic history or ancestral selfing state is reconstructed.
