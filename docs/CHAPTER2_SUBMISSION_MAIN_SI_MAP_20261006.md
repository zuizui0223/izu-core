# Chapter 2 submission main-text / Supporting Information map — 2026-10-06

## Decision

The 2026-10-05 process result is established as a bounded model-level result.
The submission manuscript should therefore be organized around one biological
claim rather than around the full repository result inventory:

> **In the delayed-selfing, assurance-cost 0.5, positive-mutation regime,
> reproductive assurance can change before floral investment declines, yet
> assurance evolution is not required for that decline.**

The upstream ecological explanation is that visitor limitation can lower the
reproductive return to attraction before plant traits evolve. The pollen-deficit
versus viable-offspring contrast is a reproductive consequence, not a separate
novelty claim.

## Main text

### Introduction

Retain the current causal setup and the distinction between temporal order and
causal necessity. Retain the bounded novelty position against sequential
selfing-syndrome precedent.

### Methods

Retain:

1. ecological scope and claim boundary;
2. visitor replenishment versus island area / plant population size;
3. the primary experiment table and independent ecological denominator;
4. the prospectively frozen 4,096-trajectory confirmation;
5. the common reproductive/inheritance process;
6. the local-selection/fixed-plant assay needed to support the upstream return
   mechanism.

Move detailed 13-rate, bridge, continuum and numerical implementation material
to Supporting Information.

### Results

Retain, in this order:

1. **Visitor limitation lowers the return on attraction before plant traits
   evolve.**
2. **Reproductive assurance often changes first under sustained isolation.**
3. **Assurance evolution is not required for investment decline.**
4. **Local selection conditions explain why the two traits need not form a
   serial pathway.**
5. **Lower pollen deficit does not necessarily mean greater viable
   reproduction.**

The independent confirmation belongs inside Results 2 and 3, not in a separate
paper-level storyline.

### Discussion

Retain:

1. sequence does not identify a selfing-mediated causal pathway;
2. pollination ecology and realized evolution are distinct biological stages;
3. reproductive assurance can preserve trajectories without prescribing one
   floral phenotype;
4. natural systems contain the component processes but not yet the full
   longitudinal causal transition;
5. ecological validity and claim ceiling.

The thesis-specific Q1 handoff should not be part of a journal manuscript.

## Submission-level claim ceiling

Do not claim:

- universal assurance-first evolution;
- full mediation by realized selfing;
- calibrated natural distance or evolutionary time;
- a named island's historical mechanism;
- pollen limitation as a newly discovered imperfect fitness proxy;
- finite-versus-deterministic or PDE closure as evidence for the primary
  biological result.
