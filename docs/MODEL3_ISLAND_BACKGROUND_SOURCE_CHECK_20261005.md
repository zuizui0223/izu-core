Checked 2026-10-05 against primary publisher pages or the authors' indexed article abstract. This is a targeted audit of five existing introduction citations, not an exhaustive literature search. It changes neither model parameters nor frozen outcomes.

## Bibliographic reconciliation

The Traveset DOI resolves to the publisher article headed **Global patterns of mainland and insular pollination networks**, volume 25, pages 880-890, 2016. The publisher's citation widget uses a shortened running title, and an Aarhus institutional record uses **Contrasting patterns of continental and insular pollination networks** for the same DOI. Retain the publisher headline and DOI; do not treat these as separate studies. Early online publication was 2015, whereas the issue year is 2016.

The existing five references' author/year/DOI combinations are supported. The introduction should attach claims to their particular sources rather than place all five after one statement that implies each paper establishes all parts of the mechanism.

## Ecological interpretation retained

Islands motivate a mechanism experiment because replenishment, breeding-system filtering and interaction function can differ with geographic context. The model deliberately manipulates replenishment at fixed plant capacity, rather than declaring an entire island phenotype in advance. Its primary no-plant-immigration experiment tests within-population response after establishment; it does not simulate the between-species colonization filter documented by Grossenbacher et al. This distinction is necessary when connecting the model to Q1 assemblage-level patterns.
