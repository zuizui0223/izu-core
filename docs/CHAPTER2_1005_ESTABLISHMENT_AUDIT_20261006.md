# Chapter 2 2026-10-05 establishment audit — 2026-10-06

## Decision

**Status: scientifically established within the declared model scope.**

The paper-level result is:

> **Under sustained visitor-replenishment limitation, realized reproductive
> assurance change can precede floral-investment decline, but assurance evolution
> is not required for that decline. In the focal delayed-selfing, assurance-cost
> 0.5 setting, isolation can lower the reproductive return to attraction directly.**

This is an established **setting-specific process result**, not a universal
selfing-syndrome law.

## Criterion 1 — frozen claim survives confirmation

**PASS.**

Before the new-history replication was executed, the frozen claim distinguished
realized temporal order from causal necessity and restricted the primary cell to
delayed selfing, assurance cost 0.5, mutation probability 0.01.

The post-confirmation manuscript retains the same claim. No success rule,
threshold, tie window, endpoint or model parameter was changed after execution
began.

## Criterion 2 — independent confirmation of the 51/64 sequence and necessity result

**PASS.**

The prospective replication used:

- 64 new visitor-history seeds: 26100601–26100664;
- eight new nested demographic repeats: 26101601–26101608;
- 4,096 declared trajectories in total;
- 64 visitor histories as the ecological inference unit.

Primary sequence result, delayed selfing / assurance cost 0.5 / mutation 0.01,
threshold 0.05:

- assurance first: **51/64**;
- near-simultaneous: **13/64**;
- assurance-first proportion: **0.796875**;
- 95% visitor-history bootstrap: **0.6875–0.890625**.

The frozen rule required a proportion >0.5 with the 95% interval lower bound
>0.5. It passed.

The fixed-assurance confirmation also passed:

- far investment change: **−0.306021**
  [−0.318054, −0.294111];
- far-minus-near investment: **−0.435389**
  [−0.453330, −0.417191];
- near and far occupancy: **1.0**;
- 64/64 eligible histories.

Thus assurance evolution is not required for investment decline under the
declared conditions.

## Criterion 3 — threshold sensitivity

**PASS for the primary cell.**

For delayed selfing / assurance cost 0.5 / mutation 0.01:

| Sustained-change threshold | Assurance first | Near-simultaneous | Proportion | 95% history bootstrap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.025 | 48/64 | 16/64 | 0.750 | 0.6406–0.8445 |
| 0.050 | 51/64 | 13/64 | 0.797 | 0.6875–0.8906 |
| 0.100 | 59/64 | 5/64 | 0.922 | 0.8438–0.9844 |

The assurance-first majority is therefore not an artefact of the single primary
0.05 threshold within the predeclared sensitivity range.

This does **not** imply robustness to arbitrary threshold definitions or trait
rescalings.

## Criterion 4 — reproductive-setting scope

**PASS as a bounded claim; FAIL as a universal claim.**

The primary result does not transport unchanged to prior selfing. With positive
mutation at threshold 0.05, the prior-selfing cell gave:

- assurance first: **30/64**;
- near-simultaneous: **34/64**;
- assurance-first proportion: **0.46875**;
- 95% interval: **0.34375–0.59375**.

Accordingly, the manuscript must continue to say **can precede** and must keep
the sequence claim restricted to the delayed-selfing/costly setting.

The fixed-assurance investment decline itself also occurs in the prior-selfing
confirmation, but that does not rescue or broaden the temporal-sequence claim.

## Criterion 5 — independence from unresolved numerical branch

**PASS.**

The confirmed paper-level claim depends on:

1. finite-individual maintained-isolation trajectories;
2. a fixed-plant reproductive-return assay;
3. a matched fixed-assurance intervention.

It does not depend on the unresolved positive-mutation deterministic/PDE
high-resolution comparison. The 9-to-13 grid failure and stopped long-horizon
comparison remain disclosed as numerical limits, not biological evidence.

The independent ecological denominator remains **64 visitor histories**, not
4,096 trajectories or the larger exploratory campaign totals.

## Criterion 6 — literature novelty boundary

**PASS.**

The novelty is not that pollinator loss can increase selfing, not that
selfing-syndrome traits can change sequentially, and not that pollen-limitation
metrics can diverge from reproductive fitness.

Those points have direct precedents, including Bodbyl Roels & Kelly (2011),
Gervasi & Schiestl (2017), Ashman et al. (2004), Knight et al. (2005, 2006), and
Van Etten et al. (2015).

The defensible contribution is narrower:

> **The same explicit pollination–reproduction model shows a realized sequence
> and then breaks the inferred serial causal chain with a separate intervention:
> the trait that changes first need not be required for the later trait change.**

The fixed-plant return assay supplies the upstream ecological mechanism by
showing that isolation can directly reduce the outcross return to attraction.

## Final claim ceiling

Supported:

> **Temporal precedence is not causal necessity. Reproductive assurance can
> change first under the declared delayed-selfing/costly conditions, while floral
> investment still declines when assurance evolution is blocked because visitor
> limitation can directly reduce the reproductive return to attraction.**

Do not claim:

- assurance itself is unnecessary;
- assurance-first ordering is universal;
- temporal order proves mediation;
- the exploratory attenuation interaction is independently confirmed;
- model time/distance are calibrated natural geography or time;
- pollen deficit is interchangeable with viable fitness;
- the positive-mutation deterministic/PDE branch is converged.
