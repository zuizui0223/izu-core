## Prospective life cycle and assumptions

One step is a reproductive year, two flowering episodes per year. Founders are mature. Recruits mature by the following year; no seed bank, mutation or migration of plants. Two independently assorting additive diploid loci have continuous allelic effects in [0,1]: access position and attraction investment. Phenotype is the two-allele mean. Attraction investment is not hue or a literal pollination syndrome. Standing variation is inherited, never reset after loss.

Visitor entries represent functional types, not individual insects. Each has a matching optimum and breadth. Type disappearance is local loss; arrivals sample the declared mainland functional pool. No introduced/native efficiency multiplier. Activity is an absolute multiplier independent of type number. Per-type contributions are averaged at fixed total activity to distinguish quantity from composition. Empty communities deliver zero pollen.

For plant i and visitor v, affinity a_iv=(0.1+investment_i)*exp(-((access_i-optimum_v)/breadth_v)^2). With V types and activity A, donor removal fraction for channel v is [1-exp(-A*mean_v a_iv)]*a_iv/sum_v a_iv. Each plant has a finite episode pollen budget. Removed pollen is allocated among recipient plants with a_jv/(sum_j a_jv+background), where background=carrying capacity represents loss outside the focal population. Diagonal deposition is discarded; autonomous delayed selfing is separate. All plants are mutually outcross-compatible. Allocation is a mixing approximation, not tracked insect flights or pollen carryover.

Ovules per year = 8*(1-survival)*exp(-0.5*investment^2); split equally between episodes. This declares equal maximum expected lifetime reproductive effort at constant adult survival, without assuming realized effort matches under extinction/density effects. Per-episode pollen budget = 20*(1-survival)/2. Pollen saturation scale=1 per recipient per episode. Selfing scenarios 0 and 0.5, fixed inbreeding depression 0.5; autonomous selfing capacity is not evolving in this campaign. No purging claim.

Sum expected viable offspring matrices over episodes, then sample a Poisson total of viable recruits and parental pairs in proportion to their expected offspring. This is stochastic expected-count reproduction, not a binomial finite-integer ovule model. Selfed offspring draw maternal and paternal gametes independently from the same parent. Retain each adult with declared survival; fill at most vacant carrying-capacity slots with recruits, sampling without trait-dependent establishment. If offspring are too few, vacancies remain. Zero population is absorbing; never refill or normalize population size.

Controls: `neutral` preserves the selfed and outcross expected totals separately, distributing selfed contributions uniformly over parents and outcross contributions uniformly over distinct parental pairs. It removes reproductive trait selection while retaining stochastic inheritance, the realized selfing fraction and ecological effects on population size. `fixed` uses the selected reproductive ledger but offspring copy the founding mean alleles, and all founders have that constant genotype: no heritable variation, retained demographic/environmental stochasticity. Selection-only interpretation requires the neutral control, not fixed alone.

Extinct endpoints are missing traits, never zero traits; report survivor denominators and extinction jointly. Pollen budgets and receipt are compatible-pollen dose units, not literal grain counts. Autonomous self pollen is separately available and assumed non-limiting. Perennial buffering here acts across years.

No mutation means access allele supports for the two initial states remain disjoint. Convergence denotes reduction of between-start distance, not reaching a common optimum. Retain founder-support constraints in all interpretation. Absence of convergence does not establish ecological multiple attractors.
