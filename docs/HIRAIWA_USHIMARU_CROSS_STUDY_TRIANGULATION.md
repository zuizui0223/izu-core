# Hiraiwa–Ushimaru cross-study response triangulation

## Question

Does the independently characterized 2017 reproductive-sensitivity panel support a simple one-direction downstream response when the same taxa are inspected in the 2024 functional-network dataset?

The comparison is intentionally limited to the three taxa present in both source-locked datasets:

- *Calystegia soldanella*;
- *Vitex rotundifolia*;
- *Lysimachia mauritiana*.

It is **not** a same-estimand replication. The 2017 study measures fruit-set response to long-tongued-pollinator functional exposure and has no Oshima reproductive observation; the 2024 dataset supplies Oshima-to-post contrasts in corrected trait matching, tube morphology and open-pollinated pollen receipt.

## 2017 response modes

The source-native 2017 evidence already contains three distinct reproductive modes:

| taxon | 2017 mode |
|---|---|
| *Calystegia soldanella* | long-tongued-pollinator sensitive |
| *Vitex rotundifolia* | fruit-set resilient to RBLP |
| *Lysimachia mauritiana* | counterdirectional reproductive response |

Thus a one-direction reproductive syndrome was already falsified in the independent 2017 dataset.

## 2024 three-channel profiles for the same taxa

All three have lower corrected trait matching post-Oshima, but their downstream channels differ:

| taxon | matching | tube | pollen receipt | full matching↓ + tube↓ + pollen↓? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Calystegia soldanella* | lower | longer | higher | no |
| *Vitex rotundifolia* | lower | shorter | higher | no |
| *Lysimachia mauritiana* | lower | longer | lower | no |

Therefore **0/3** independently characterized reproductive-sensitivity taxa show the complete uniform-decline cascade in the 2024 data.

This is stronger as a falsification panel than as a coefficient estimate: the subset selected because its reproductive biology is independently characterized does not collapse onto one morphological/reproductive direction despite sharing the same corrected-matching decline.

## What repeats across the two studies

The reproducible level is **heterogeneity itself**:

- 2017: multiple fruit-set response modes under a functional pollinator gradient;
- 2024: multiple tube and pollen directions under a shared post-Oshima corrected-matching decline.

The species-specific signs cannot be required to match because the years, exact sites, exposure definitions and outcome variables differ. In particular, pollen receipt is not fruit set, and 2017 lacks the Oshima reproductive datum needed to form the same second-boundary contrast.

So the defensible synthesis is:

> independent datasets repeatedly reject a uniform downstream plant-response syndrome, while direct dependency/assurance remains missing as the variable that could explain why response modes branch.

## Consequence for the next empirical design

The 2017 response modes are useful biological constraints, but they are **not numeric dependency values** and must not be inserted into the 2024 dependency × FDQ design simulation.

The direct field programme should instead measure, in the same tagged populations:

1. per-visit pollen function;
2. rate-weighted effective service;
3. autonomous / open / supplemented reproductive performance;
4. plant-level dispersion and loss;
5. repeated site-season functional state.

That design can test whether direct dependency/assurance predicts which of the empirically observed response branches a lineage occupies.

## Claim boundary

This is cross-study triangulation of **response heterogeneity**, not replication of a single effect. It does not identify species-specific sign persistence, mediation, direct reproductive dependency, an Oshima–Toshima causal boundary or historical Bombus causation.
