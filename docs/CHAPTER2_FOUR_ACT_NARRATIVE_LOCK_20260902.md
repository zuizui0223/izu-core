# Chapter 2 four-act narrative lock

Updated: 2026-09-02
Status: active journal-facing narrative contract

## One-sentence paper

Community reorganization does not prescribe a single biological response; it creates a relational response geometry in which organismal state is evaluated against the community actually realized, and resolving that geometry in nature requires moving from synthetic theory, through global empirical confrontation and an identifiability audit, to a higher-resolution focal system.

## Four inferential acts

### Act 1 — Theory: simulation defines the response geometry

The synthetic model is used to build a mechanistic coordinate system, not to estimate natural prevalence. The model asks whether the same broad pollinator-community reorganization can generate positive, negative and mixed responses and then decomposes why.

The theoretical object is the trajectory-conditioned interaction-kernel contrast

`G_omega(x0) = K_I,T(Phi_I,T(x0; omega_I)) - K_M,T(Phi_M,T(x0; omega_M))`.

Within the declared design:

- partner loss and arrival organize movement among response regimes;
- starting state is an input coordinate but is not a strong state-only additive predictor;
- realized community is the dominant additive component across the prespecified seed and horizon sensitivities;
- state × community non-additivity remains consequential, including when trait adjustment is zero;
- local filtering reallocates branch identity asymmetrically;
- reproductive assurance modifies downstream magnitude without sign rescue in the tested envelope.

The headline is therefore relational rather than magnitude-specific: response direction depends on state evaluated against the realized community.

### Act 2 — Global confrontation: carry the response vocabulary into real island ecology

The world step is not a fitted projection of synthetic regime labels onto empirical systems. It is a confrontation of the theoretical response vocabulary with the diversity already documented across island research.

The comparative universe contains same-direction propagation, branching, buffering, reproductive-axis decoupling and retained falsification. Their role is to establish that a single directional island syndrome is not an adequate vocabulary for the observed response diversity.

Allowed language:

- project or carry the **response vocabulary** into the global island literature;
- confront the theoretical architecture with empirical response diversity;
- ask whether real systems require a richer set of response states.

Forbidden language:

- assign empirical systems to synthetic regimes from known outcomes;
- claim that the synthetic model predicts the 25 research entries;
- treat the comparative inventory as validation coverage or a prevalence sample;
- call the 25 research entries 25 independent archipelagos.

### Act 3 — Identifiability: diagnose why broad comparison cannot yet identify mechanism

The source audit converts the global confrontation into a measurement result.

The literature is outcome-rich but process-poor for this mechanistic question:

- direct response outcome: 21/25 entries;
- direct community functional shift: 13/25;
- direct local filtering: 9/25;
- direct richness/FD change: 8/25;
- direct source functional state: 5/25;
- direct partner loss: 5/25;
- direct reproductive assurance: 5/25;
- direct partner arrival/replacement: 2/25.

No entry supplies the full outcome-independent state–community–context–outcome contract. Formal external prediction is therefore `not_evaluable`, not failed.

The scientific contribution of this act is not the bare `0/25` count. It is the asymmetric measurement architecture: island studies commonly measure outcomes but rarely jointly measure the process coordinates required by the response geometry, especially arrival/replacement.

### Act 4 — Izu mechanistic-resolution zoom: increase depth where the global audit cannot

Izu is the resolution step, not a validation winner and not a post-hoc best-fitting island system.

The same island series permits source floral state, source and island pollinator composition, quantitative pollinator traits, interaction structure, raw realized matching and null-corrected matching to be connected at higher resolution.

The focal comparison separates two mechanisms that broad global comparison cannot:

1. source-state plus background community-composition structure;
2. additional within-community non-random partner sorting.

The frozen result localizes the current signal to the first mechanism:

- raw realized matching is associated with the frozen source-state projection;
- exact island-centre magnitudes are non-unique;
- a source-position-only comparator is at least as descriptive as the full centre-shift geometry;
- the prespecified Oshima-source bridge is unsupported;
- null-corrected matching is unsupported.

The Izu result therefore increases mechanistic resolution by narrowing where the signal resides. It is not synthetic-model validation and does not establish causal floral evolution, Bombus causation, pollinator selection or beyond-composition sorting.

## Journal-facing funnel

The manuscript should be readable as:

**Theory → Global confrontation → Identifiability → Izu mechanistic-resolution zoom**

Expanded:

**Model the response possibilities and mechanism → carry the response vocabulary into global island ecology → discover the process-measurement bottleneck → increase resolution in Izu to separate composition from sorting.**

The world step and the Izu step have different empirical roles. Global comparison supplies breadth and reveals what cannot be identified; Izu supplies depth and resolves one of those ambiguities. Neither is presented as a held-out validation test.

## Claim ceiling

The paper may claim a general relational response framework with bounded empirical confrontation and a mechanistic-resolution focal analysis (Tier B).

It may not claim:

- natural frequencies of synthetic regimes;
- calibrated ecological thresholds from the synthetic coordinate;
- successful formal external prediction;
- universal island response mechanisms;
- outcome-independent selection of Izu as a global winner;
- within-community sorting in Izu;
- ultimate historical causes of island community assembly, colonization or floral evolution.
