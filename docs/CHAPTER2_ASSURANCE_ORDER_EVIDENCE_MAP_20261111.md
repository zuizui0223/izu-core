## Contribution to use in the paper

Neither insurance by delayed selfing, selection through both sexual functions, departures from uniform island flower size, nor a mismatch between selection and response is new in itself. The contribution is to separate three comparisons usually read from the same phenotype: whether investment decline requires evolving assurance; whether permitting assurance modifies the environmental investment contrast; and whether finite inheritance realizes a favoured change. Assigned timing adds a boundary: a maternal benefit of having assurance is not evidence that acquiring it first gives a large later survival advantage. Replicate concentration, known-source prediction, assigned schedules and genetic-access tests are complementary, not a single identified causal sequence.
