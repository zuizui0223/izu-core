# Model3 K32: Reproductive channels of assurance heterozygosity effects at fixed parental allele copies

## Main question

The dedicated original-source experiment established that altering exactly
TWO parental assurance diploid genotype pairs at the same HIGH allele COPY
count changes the original Model3 next-offspring assurance-high allele direction,
but does not consistently reverse the next matching-high allele direction.
This is a genotype-mediated reproductive response under an artificially
edited parent state, not an experimental fitness measurement.

What source reproductive components produce that strong assurance
self-response? Is the difference mostly in expected viable selfed
seeds, outcross pollen fathers, or outcross maternal recipients?
And do those edits change expected offspring CENSUS as well as
allele transmission?

## Exact source-parent ledger

For each identical original parent state C, use the exact unmodified
canonical reproduce() twice: on a sham assurance diplotype
randomization and on that SAME-PERMUTATION arrangement after HET_UP
or HET_DOWN. The original source Markov simulation supplies C
and is NEVER run on artificially edited genotypes.

For N living parents, n_LL, n_LH, n_HH and A=n_LH+2*n_HH
are the diploid assurance classes and HIGH assurance copies.
HET_UP converts one LL plus one HH to two LH, feasible only when
n_LL>=1 and n_HH>=1; HET_DOWN converts two LH to one LL and one HH,
feasible only when n_LH>=2. Both keep A and N EXACT, change
heterozygosity by precisely +/-2/N, and preserve the original
matching/investment loci at EVERY individual. If infeasible, record
the absence and do not invent lost alleles.

The exact original source viable parent-pair reproduction ledger
is W_ii=self_viable_i and W_ij=outcross_ij for distinct parents,
with T=sum W, S=sum self_viable/T, O=sum outcross/T. For locus-l
diploid dosage b_i and current parental high-allele mean p_l,

    E[p_child,l | C,V,N_child>0]-p_l =
       [sum_i self_i b_il/T - S p_l]
       +[sum_i outcross_father_i b_il/(2T) - O p_l/2]
       +[sum_i outcross_mother_i b_il/(2T) - O p_l/2].

For edited versus sham, p_l is IDENTICAL at every locus,
so the total directional difference must be exactly
the sum of the three corresponding self, father,
mother differences for each source demographic
replicate, reassignment permutation and visitor snapshot.
The original genomic reproduction law and exact
Mendelian inheritance are unchanged; any observed
differences arise from altered hypothetical parental
genetic states.

In addition to these expected gene-transmission
components, independently report:

- absolute expected viable SELF seed intensity;
- absolute expected viable OUTCROSS seed intensity;
- their total viable recruitment intensity T;
- expected fraction of viable selfed seeds S;
- capped-Poisson E[min(Poisson(T),32)];
- probability N=0 under the original source capped
  Poisson offspring recruitment kernel.

The edit keeps parental copy count constant, NOT
source-conditional viable seed intensity or offspring
census. An allele-frequency direction (conditional on
positive offspring) and direct demographic effects
therefore must not be conflated.

## Fixed old-source protocol

Only source history 26110601 / near, K32, zero
mutation, adult survival, seed immigration,
Chapter2 prior_selfing, original 27 diploid
genotype classes, two source ovule budgets 8 and 3.
Source parent years 1,4,8 are generated by the
ORIGINAL eight-year finite Markov kernel; one
matched cohort of source paths still alive at the
beginning of original source year8 is used throughout.
Each source/edited parent is evaluated at year1
and year8 visitor snapshots of that SAME old
history, no new independently sampled environments.

Each source parent uses four deterministic within-path
permutations, edited minus its SAME sham. Average
the four permutations inside a source path before
estimating the nested demographic Monte Carlo SE.
HET_UP vs HET_DOWN feasibility differs, especially
at late assurance fixation; the current audit does
not combine their estimates into an unsupported
population-wide heterozygote slope.

## Interpretive boundary

The 3-way self/father/mother breakdown is an exact
**accounting of transmitted expected alleles**, not
separately manipulable causal selfing/fecundity/
pollinator effects. Two parents' assurance genotype
pairs change, and their associations with matching
and investment phenotypes also change in those
individuals. Pairing shams account for shared
random diplotype assignment but cannot uniquely
identify a physiological heterozygote fitness
coefficient.

The model's natural histories remain one ecological
archive, and all demographic path SE are conditional
on that environment. Any census contrast can arise
directly from changed ovules, assurance and pollen
allocation without indicating a natural plant
population viability difference. No prospective
confirmation histories or real island plant
observations are used. The result is not validated
SDE/SPDE, geographic INLA or causal field ecology.
