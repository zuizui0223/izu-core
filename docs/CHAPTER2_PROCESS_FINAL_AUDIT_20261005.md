## Main ecological statement

Limited visitor replenishment changes two connected reproductive tradeoffs.
It can intensify an existing advantage of autonomous reproduction while making
additional attraction less rewarding. Capacity can cross a founder-relative
change threshold first, but investment can respond first to the additional
replenishment contrast, and investment decline does not require evolving capacity
in the declared intervention. Finite realization and genetic supply constrain
what these reproductive returns become. These are model-conditional process
results, not universal ordering or reconstruction of Q1 regional histories.
