### R1 recovered: the fixed-assurance counterexample is stronger than the original shared-founder display

[Fresh-founder source readout](CHAPTER2_Q1_FRESH_FOUNDER_RESULTS_20261016.md) and [independent read-only original archive receipt](CHAPTER2_TWO_PROPOSITIONS_PROOF_AND_RESULT_20261016.md) cover eight independent founder genomes × eight new visitor histories. With actual expressed A fixed at 0.5, mean far-minus-founder I changes are `−0.217478`, `−0.277802`, `−0.286866`, `−0.219047` for delayed, prior, discount and direct-cost settings. Descriptive crossed 95% intervals are negative in all four, and **all 8/8 founder-marginal means in each setting are negative**. This supports **non-necessity of A *evolution*** beyond the original common founder. It is not a zero-selfing test.

A separate **mathematical conditional bound** supplies the founder premise: without locus mutation or immigration, inherited allele-value support is a non-expanding set. The attainable individual/population mean remains within initial minimum and maximum allele values; the presence of support does **not** guarantee that any target will be reached. This is not a demonstrated arrival-filter mechanism or a general statement that a founder ID determines the final A state. The prospective Q2 carrier change remained inconclusive despite known no-mutation support exclusion in one constructed arm. Source: [two-proposition theorem](CHAPTER2_TWO_PROPOSITIONS_PROOF_AND_RESULT_20261016.md), [registered carrier null](CHAPTER2_Q2_I_SUPPORT_384_REGISTERED_RESULT_20261016.md).

### R2 recovered: the environmental response can change sign, and "far-side preservation" fails generalization

[Fresh 8×8 registered interaction](CHAPTER2_Q1_FRESH_FOUNDER_RESULTS_20261016.md): D = +0.096122 [+.022020,+.168607], +.192465 [+.110756,+.275589], +.163012 [+.047329,+.273690], and +.044271 [−.044065,+.130837] in delayed, prior, discount, direct-A-cost settings. Thus **3/4 setting-specific tests support positive attenuation**, while the **registered four-setting combined decision is inconclusive**. Under the new sources, near-side (A-evolving − A-fixed) I is negative in **all four** settings, but the far-side comparison is negative in delayed and direct-cost settings. Do not replace positive D with the claim that A evolution *always preserves I under strong pollen shortage*. Nor attribute those sign differences only to founder genomes: new visitor histories also differ.

In a different **12-founder, 4-nested-repeat exploratory factorial**, additional visitor decline caused A changes of +.008735/+ .004612 under **strong baseline** limitation versus +.112413/+.088162 under **weak baseline** limitation, for starting A=.3/.5. The weak-minus-strong A interactions were +.103678 and +.083550; founder-bootstrap 95% intervals [.069499,.137804] and [.041450,.128147]. Meanwhile **additional I contrasts were negative under strong and positive under weak baseline conditions**: −.080241 versus +.020725 at A=.3, and −.035699 versus +.024631 at A=.5. These are comparisons against maintained-activity arms, not necessarily absolute I increases. Previous A evolution and absolute pollen fertilization changes differed, so this is **baseline-regime/history dependence**, not isolated evolutionary-memory mediation. Source: [384 audit](CHAPTER2_BASELINE_POLLEN_384_RESULTS_20261019.md).

The [A/I hitchhiking review](CHAPTER2_HITCHHIKING_SECONDARY_EXPANSION_REVIEW_20261020.md) further documents **sign reversals upon neutralizing M as well as I payoff**, preventing an A-specific hitchhiking mechanism from being promoted into R2.

### R3 recovered: endpoint agreement, directional non-neutral response, and fixed alleles are three separate objects

In the **128 history × 4 nested repeat** delayed-control central-start cohort, 107/128 contexts had four identical A far endpoints, versus 17/128 for I; but 103/128 unanimous A far-minus-near signs were **all zero**. The 107 and 103 count different statistics. Thus "repeated endpoint" does not establish consistently positive environment-specific A evolution. This is a post-outcome comparison with no trait-ranking confirmation and with five-state A/I support, no mutation and fixed M. [Matched endpoint source](CHAPTER2_MATCHED_AI_REPEATABILITY_20261019.md).

A **different** saved 160×160 B8 genetic clock (all populations survive) shows A genetically fixed at any allele in **25,582/25,600** A-evolvable histories versus **15,664/25,600** fixed-expression histories. Specifically A=.75 in 25,307 versus 5,145 all-start histories. The fixed-expression arm still segregates and transmits **latent A alleles**; the excess high-A fixation is a policy contrast, **not the percentage of convergence attributable uniquely to selection**. Source: [existing saved-variation readback](CHAPTER2_VARIATION_CLOCK_RESULTS_20261019.md).

A third **old K32, one-history** experiment found mean high-A allele frequency 0.9773 under native Model 3 against 0.5012 with neutral equal-parent Mendelian inheritance at the **same census paths**: high-A direction is not purely random fixation. Yet a retrospectively mean- and census-matched artificial comparator also produced very low terminal variance, so A endpoint compression cannot be attributed uniquely to adaptive stabilizing feedback. These **separate K32 and 160×160 cases are not pooled**. [Neutral comparator](MODEL3_K32_NEUTRAL_MATCHED_CENSUS_BOUNDARY_20261009.md); [mean-matched ceiling](MODEL3_K32_MEAN_MATCHED_CEILING_20261009.md); [separate draft audit PR #617](https://github.com/zuizui0223/izu-core/pull/617), which does not change main.

Finally, [crossed-source known-ID prediction](CHAPTER2_FOUNDER_PREDICTABILITY_RESULTS_20261021.md) under evolving A found I-change leave-cell-out founder-only skill **−0.459%**, visitor-history-only **+0.359%**, additive **−0.093%**; M-change visitor-history-only skill **+40.146%**. This measures **previously observed** source IDs paired in a held-out cell, not newly sampled founders. The **97.622% interaction-plus-realization residual cannot be partitioned into drift and genotype × environment** from one observation per crossed cell. Since M is directly linked to visitor matching optima while I trades off reproductive allocation, the M/I contrast is **design-conditional**, not a universal trait ranking.

### R4 recovered: resource-dependent joint fate, with a registered-versus-exploratory firewall

[Registered 160×160 result](CHAPTER2_CONFIRM160_RESULTS_20261019.md), 160 independently sampled founder sources crossed with 160 visitor histories, each pair tested under two A-expression policies and B8/B4.5. At B8 **all policies are alive 25,600/25,600**. First founder-target high-I genetic closure counts **16,000 evolving** versus **13,868 fixed**, registered G **+8.328125 percentage points**, marginal adjusted 97.5% interval **[+6.693910,+9.962340]**. This excludes differential *extinction* as the explanation of B8 support-closure difference, **not** within-survivor family-size or demographic pathway differences.

At B4.5, 80-census alive = **25,118 evolving** versus **1,385 fixed**, registered interaction S **+92.707031 pp [92.049592,93.364471]**, since B8 survival is complete in both. Five prespecified **descriptive** high-I target tolerances (0/.005/.01/.02/.05) all yield positive B8 policy differences, respectively **8.328125, 8.597656, 8.476563, 8.328125, 8.343750 pp**. These are not five new primary tests. Registered marginal 97.5% intervals use crossed-source pigeonhole variance and a t159 reference without universal finite-sample coverage.

An **exploratory all-start joint state** is alive+target accessible in 11,732 fixed versus 9,600 evolving cells at B8 (**−8.328125 pp**), but 140 fixed versus 9,245 evolving at B4.5 (**+35.566406 pp**). Harsh-budget survival is the dominant difference; survivor conditioning changes which histories are compared. At B8, evolving-A shortens occupied I-polymorphism by **2.628** generations, while at B4.5 it increases that occupied duration by **12.684**, a quantity confounded with extra time alive. Consequently the data do **not** establish a single population-level "survival bought by erosion" mediator, an always-negative effect on survivor-accessibility, or a continuous resource threshold. [Three-state source review](CHAPTER2_N5_COMPETING_RISKS_THEORY_REVIEW_20261019.md); [variation clocks](CHAPTER2_VARIATION_CLOCK_RESULTS_20261019.md).

### R5 recovered: genetic target support is not segregating variation, response, or colonization

The [bounded late transfer](CHAPTER2_READAPTATION_RESULTS_20261019.md) uses **32 source donors**, 64 seed-derived cohorts, five artificial destinations and 32 nested futures per cohort/destination: **10,240 future trajectories** (not 10,240 independently founded populations). Both historical arms permit A evolution **after transfer**. All 32 cohorts with an A-evolving history enter **A=.75 monomorphic**; all 5,120 of those futures remain occupied. High-I founder-target support exists in **24/32** seed cohorts under either historical policy, but I is **polymorphic in only 4/32**; these four give all observed I changes in visitor-present destinations. **Source target support is necessary but not sufficient for a subsequent mean response.** Some I means already meet or exceed original founder I, so no I increase cannot automatically be labelled "failed ancestral recovery".

The no-visitor destination additionally had **27/1,024** futures with I increases after fixed-A history and **29/1,024** after evolving-A history, despite no pollinator presence. The conditional mechanism of those increases is not identified by the endpoint count. Overall no-visitor occupancy is **704/1,024** versus **1,024/1,024**; the historical arms differ in inherited M/I/A, so these are **not A-only treatment effects**, and average performance change is calculated **only among survivors**.

A separately executed [secondary-colonization micro-pilot and exact pedigree readback](CHAPTER2_HITCHHIKING_SECONDARY_EXPANSION_REVIEW_20261020.md) has only **2 founders × 2 visitor histories**, 240 nested futures. At a distant resident mainland-like recipient after t25 island history, selected source markers persist in **2/8 fixed-history** versus **4/8 evolving-history** futures, while actual genealogical descendants persist in **4/8** versus **6/8**, despite recipient total occupancy **8/8 in both**. These are **not independent island-to-mainland populations**. Marker loss ≠ migrant descent extinction; neither ≡ successful adaptation of a separate migrant lineage. Source: [verified genealogy receipt](audits/CHAPTER2_SECONDARY_COLONIZATION_PEDIGREE_VERIFIED_20261020.json).

A separate mutation-renewal sensitivity [reopened standing I support in 914 histories](CHAPTER2_RENEWAL_STANDING_ACCESS_RESULTS_20261015.md), of which **758 did not complete a sustained I-change event by generation 200**; 64 had completed it **before** first deficit and 92 completed it **after** first reopening. It is not a mutation-enabled general probability of failure or proof of permanent irreversibility; renewal changes future genetic histories.

## Publication editing decision (no new biological work)

- **Lead:** R1 causal **non-necessity of A evolution** and R4 preregistered *distinct* G/S outcome contrasts.
- **Conditioning mechanism:** R2 effect **depends on mating regime and pollen-service history**; include the direct-cost inconclusive result and fresh-founder far-side sign reversal.
- **Trait-specific response:** R3 distinguishes endpoint repeatability, actual response direction, founder/history known-ID predictive skill, conditional non-neutral A direction, and fixation boundaries. Avoid stating that **98% of I evolution is drift**.
- **Forward consequence:** R5 is a bounded **possibility/realization/ancestry** test, not actual restoration or successful natural mainland recolonization.
- **Supplementary coverage:** do not remove original inconclusive Q2 carrier tests, assigned-order practical equivalence, alternate allele-support and mutation-renewal work, or the 41 historical S/F entry sources. Negative evidence keeps the main causal story honest.
- **Five existing main figures remain Figures 1–6** in `figures/chapter2_nee_five_results`; original old figures remain extended-data candidates, with no invented new visual or biology. Current proposed source-locked edits only tighten Results prose and cross-links.

**No combined effect size, new uncertainty interval, biological mediator fraction, empirical island-selection conclusion, ancestral recovery probability or new simulation is calculated in this audit.**
