## Novelty boundary confirmed by the source check

The literature already establishes that:
- parallel evolution is quantitative rather than binary;
- direction, angle and magnitude are not interchangeable trajectory properties;
- environmental and demographic heterogeneity can alter evolutionary repeatability;
- shared function can coexist with less-parallel morphology;
- island-syndrome phenotypic parallelism can coexist with population-specific genomic differentiation.

Therefore the candidate novelty is deliberately narrower:

> **Within one explicit eco-evolutionary operator, greater directional sign uniformity can arise while reproducible visitor-history structure either strengthens relative to demographic noise or is erased by environmental averaging.**

The prospectively frozen new-demographic and new-visitor-history validations support this model-level prediction, but they do not constitute natural-island validation or transfer to a different ecological-history generator.
