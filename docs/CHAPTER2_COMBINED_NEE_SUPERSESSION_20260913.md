## Natural-regime placement target

The natural-data task is narrower than the failed historical transition-validation programme. It requires repeated interaction data sufficient to estimate two outcome-independent context coordinates:

1. **realized partner breadth** — primary coordinate: pooled effort-standardized Hill `D1`, with partner richness retained descriptively;
2. **temporal synchrony** — primary coordinate: Loreau–de Mazancourt `phi` from aligned partner/interactions time series, with exchangeable-equivalent correlation reported only as a descriptive bridge to the theory.

Natural systems are placed in a `(breadth, synchrony)` context plane. They are **not** assigned to a synthetic `k` threshold by literal substitution.
