## Special terminal states

### Cayman Islands

Anchors: `10.11646/zootaxa.3970.1.1` and `10.3389/fpls.2021.639368`.

Caribbean *Zamia–Rhopalotria* is an obligate pollination mutualism, and cycad-cone weevil occurrence is documented for the Cayman region. However, the recovered sources do not provide a Cayman-local pollination-effectiveness experiment or matched plant reproductive response.

Terminal state: `terminal_local_pollinator_association_effectiveness_unrecovered`.

This preserves useful local association evidence without upgrading regional mutualism knowledge into a Cayman-local effectiveness test.

### Wallis and Futuna

Primary anchor: `10.1098/rspb.2013.0361`.

The Pacific *Glochidion–Epicephala* co-radiation study samples *Glochidion* taxa from Wallis. In the source-native sampling statement, additional *Epicephala* specimens were recovered from American Samoa, Fiji, New Caledonia, Australia and Asia rather than Wallis.

Terminal state: `terminal_host_phylogeography_pollinator_pairing_unrecovered`.

Wallis therefore has source-native host phylogeographic coverage, while local pollinator pairing and effectiveness remain unresolved.

### Gambier Islands

Primary anchor: `10.1098/rspb.2013.0361`.

*Phyllanthus wilderi* from Mangareva is included directly. *Epicephala* moths were not collected from this species, but mature fruits showed exit holes and limited seed consumption consistent with persistence of the moth interaction.

Terminal state: `terminal_indirect_local_mutualism_trace`.

The Mangareva fruit evidence is retained as an informative local mutualism trace, but it is not described as direct observed pollination effectiveness.

## What “queue = 0” means

It means every target in the current 111-target search frame has reached a documented review state under the current protocol.

It does **not** mean:

- 111 is the number of island systems on Earth;
- every possible paper has been found;
- every geography has direct pollination evidence;
- terminal gaps demonstrate biological absence;
- the 111 targets are independent empirical tests of the synthetic model.

Any terminal target is reopened if a new qualifying source is found. Source-native multi-archipelago studies can also reveal an omitted geography, in which case the search frame expands transparently.
