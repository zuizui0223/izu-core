### S5.3. Sequence, conditional lag and realized investment limits

**A-only is not A-first.** In delayed selfing with cost 0.5 and no mutation, the 53 abrupt-arm A crossers by update 100 comprise eight A-first and 45 A-only trajectories. A-only is the largest category in every delayed abrupt arm at both horizons and every delayed gradual arm by update 400. Prior selfing with cost 0 and mutation 0.01 also precludes a universal A-first-advancement claim: A-first counts are 25 versus 30 by update 100 and 44 versus 45 by update 400, abrupt versus gradual.

Conditional lag is A onset minus I onset among trajectories with both events confirmed by update 400. The paired contrast uses only demographic repeats eligible in both arms, averages within profiles, and then equally weights eligible profiles. It is not the difference of separately eligible arm means. Three settings have no paired lag estimand. The sole descriptive lag interval excluding zero is +53.5 updates [+11, +96] in delayed selfing without cost or mutation, based on **two paired trajectories in two profiles** and 8,813 nonempty bootstrap draws out of 9,999. The largest paired support is 35 trajectories across 16 profiles, with an interval spanning zero. These conditional results do not identify a general advance or delay between two inherited events.

Abrupt schedules have lower mean I at update 400 than gradual schedules in all eight point comparisons. This is a between-schedule difference: **every delayed arm-average I400 remains above the actual founder mean 0.5023530254**. Even the abrupt prior-selfing, cost-0.5, no-mutation mean is slightly above that founder value (0.506583). A transient sustained crossing need not persist to update 400. Lower mean pollen and overlap, or higher selfed-recruit fractions, do not by themselves establish population decline. Time-averaged expected pre-mutation I responses are positive in both arms in all delayed settings, although smaller under abrupt schedules. Sampled genotype-dependent selection gradients can change sign again; they are not exact per-trajectory first sign-crossing times or an irreversible fixed-clonal clock.

## S26. Registered distribution-policy contrasts and limits of maintenance identification

The approved distribution-triangle experiment retained 61 original fixation states and all 64 weighted original slots (three nontrigger policy zeros), with 144 triplets per triggered state: 26,352 trajectories and 8,784 triplets. A and B share exact allele support but differ in heterozygote frequency and variance; B and C share allele variance but differ in width and carrier composition. For the original full-history sustained event, A-minus-B in the main block was +0.057726 [0.008478,0.106974], while confirmation was -0.003038 [-0.051485,0.045409]. The three policy-zero slots remain in the 32-times-144 block denominator.

Thus exact support alone does not determine all tested policy responses, but the assignments do not uniquely identify variance, genotype composition or persistence mechanisms. For example, adding c(w-h)(q-1/16) to a response surface leaves all three assignments unchanged (A/B have w=h=1/16; C has q=1/16) while changing untested assignments. This is a response-surface nonidentification argument, not a claim that every such surface is generated by the native model.

For allele support [L,U], mean m and population variance V, nonnegativity of (U-X)(X-L) gives V <= (U-m)(m-L). Restoring maximal variance h^2 on the original support [v-h,v+h] requires mean v; after upper fixation this immediately lowers mean investment by h=1/16. ## S29. Original-I-constrained F1 genetic access versus conditional reproductive returns

This **post hoc model-mechanistic diagnostic** is separate from the preregistered evolutionary and demographic cohorts. It adds no population transitions, newborns, visitor histories or independent founder sources, and is **not an additional confirmatory test of assurance-first order or Q4 survival**. The authenticated original Model 3 first-census genotypes contain 48 adults in each of two founder backgrounds (main and confirmation). Every original adult carries a heterozygous assurance-capacity A locus (copies 0.4375 and 0.5625), while original I heterozygosity is 24/48 in main and 30/48 in confirmation. Under a narrow comparison fixing each original I homolog and individual expressed M/I/A phenotype, with the same number of A-heterozygote carriers reassigned *within identical-phenotype groups*, the largest achievable change in A/I co-carriage is **zero in main and six in confirmation**. A strict m=0 versus m=12 original-I-preserving comparison cannot be made in either source under this matching rule. This does not exclude separately synthetic-source constructions.

For the feasible confirmation comparison, both hypothetical arms artificially reset **42 of the 48 original A heterozygotes** to A-homozygous 0.5/0.5, retaining six symmetric A-heterozygote carriers. In m=0 these six A carriers have I copies 0.5/0.5; in m=6 they have original I copies 0.375/0.625. All original M and I copies, every adult's expressed M/I/A, initial census 48, and native parental reproductive-return ledger remain unchanged between the arms. The native t0 reproductive function depends on **expressed adult traits** rather than hidden allele composition, so the artificial A reset does not change instantaneous t0 returns. It does change genetic segregation in prospective offspring and is not undisturbed natural island founding.

Analytical integration of the original parental lottery and 16 maternal/paternal homolog-copy combinations shows that the complete separate F1 A and I marginal probability distributions agree across m=0 and m=6 (largest per-bin discrepancy 6.6 × 10^(-15)).
