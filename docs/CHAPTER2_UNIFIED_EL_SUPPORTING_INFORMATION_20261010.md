## S1. Preregistered primary evolutionary study and four-setting result

The original 2026-10-06 confirmatory campaign contains 8,192 positive-mutation evolutionary trajectories, 64 independent new visitor histories and eight nested demographic repeats, under near/far visitor replenishment × fixed/evolving assurance across four predefined reproductive settings. A separate 256 zero-mutation structural-control campaign yielded 128 identical fixed/evolving paired traces. The inference unit is the visitor-history cluster. The primary effect is not a natural mediation estimate or a comparison between an actually saturated mainland and a naturally isolated island.

|---|---|---|
| Cost-free delayed selfing, structural control | −0.4413 [−0.4564,−0.4262] | +0.1605 [+0.1418,+0.1789] |
| Prior selfing | −0.3018 [−0.3172,−0.2866] | +0.2200 [+0.2025,+0.2383] |
| Pollen discount | −0.3305 [−0.3461,−0.3151] | +0.1915 [+0.1762,+0.2069] |
| Assurance cost | −0.4337 [−0.4501,−0.4171] | +0.1007 [+0.0842,+0.1177] |

All four settings passed the original all-four outcome-independent decision rule. Post-confirmation algebraic near/far decomposition localizes 78–90% of the divergence attenuation to additional near-side investment decline; this is not an experimentally isolated mediator percentage. In the cost-free delayed setting, assurance has a structural direct benefit if viable selfing and unfilled ovules coexist, so four settings are not four equally challenging tests of assurance's invasion.

## S2. Original relative pollen availability and finite horizon

The original independent static 48-plant/64-history snapshot-400 delayed-selfing assay at fixed assurance a=0.5 reports mean raw seed deficit 0.3901 near and 0.4948 far AFTER autonomous selfing. Since d_raw=(1−a)(1−F/O), the implied mean fractions of ovules fertilized by outcross pollen are approximately 0.2197 near and 0.0103 far. These are fixed-state assay quantities, NOT measurements of terminal evolving-population unfertilized ovules. An outcome-exposed source-only pollen-budget feasibility grid up to 16-fold failed the prespecified-within-grid near outcross saturation admission criterion. Hence the confirmed main effect is under TWO pollen-limited model regimes.

Separate long-horizon deterministic genotype-density and mutation-accessibility audits failed stationarity criteria; a standing-variation effect-size ranking changed between 800 and 1,600 reproductive seasons. These are different experiments from the four-setting 1,000-update primary; they are not evidence that the four-setting attenuation changes sign after 1,000 updates.

## S3. Assigned expression-order and finite demographic study

The separately preregistered fixed-background comparison used up to eight matched source founders and independently selected 64 visitor-history clusters. It assigned transient A-first or I-first phenotype schedules; it did NOT force spontaneous mutation order. With fixed pollen-background B=48, its original primary K8-versus-K48 difference in sensitivity of A-first-minus-I-first 80-update occupancy to halving viable selfed seeds was +0.0077457 [paired 95% visitor-history bootstrap +0.0024972,+0.0130155]. This equals about 0.775 percentage points of a difference in differences, not an overall K effect or a natural island extinction rate.

The original near/far assigned-expression-history DID was practically equivalent under its declared ±0.05 interval; its resource-window confirmation failed. Early fixed-founder K and B-at-K8 preregistered primary comparisons were inconclusive. Two separate original cohorts gave +0.0134300 and +0.0085803 estimates of the fixed-B48 capacity contrast, but only as POST-OUTCOME SECONDARY/DESCRIPTIVE findings; no pooled interval or threefold confirmation exists. The separately preregistered early-versus-late selfed-seed viability contrast was inconclusive: −0.002965 [−0.007263,+0.001317].

This study has a different horizon and source treatment regime from S1. There is NO randomized experiment linking evolutionarily reduced floral investment in S1 to the occupancy outcomes of S3.

## S4. Original evolved-genome source checks and demographic nulls

Post-discovery audits replayed the actual original matched evolving/fixed t400 genomes for 64 already-exposed visitor histories, using only ONE of eight nested demographic repeats. At the higher-replenishment t400 checkpoint, restoration of investment alleles to the historical matched fixed-mode population average increased total delivered pollen on average across the four settings, but the within-genome seed-production sign was not consistently negative. Changing ONE evolved native adult's investment can increase viable seed production in other mothers, yet paternal-inclusive finite parental genetic effects versus WHOLE-GROUP viable seeds had opposite signs only in a subset of focal perturbations. Group seed differences may be offset by ovule-resource savings and recipient-specific pollen receipt redistribution.

A bounded six-level original-genome ovule-budget sensitivity (0.025, 0.05, 0.125, 0.25, 0.5, 1 times the standard budget) used exact one-generation Poisson-limited recruitment under K=48/B=48. At the 0.125 budget scale, mean evolving-minus-clamped next-census effects across delayed, prior, pollen-discount and assurance-cost settings were −0.0304, +0.3090, +0.2201, +0.1103, respectively. Mean one-step occupancy remained >0.99999 in every setting, even when expected census no longer saturated K. This source-only sensitivity is NOT an independent survival study; it does not support an automatically harmful group-persistence consequence of assurance evolution.
