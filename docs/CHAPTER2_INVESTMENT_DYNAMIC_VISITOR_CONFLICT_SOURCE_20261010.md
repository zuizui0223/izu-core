# The floral-investment externality conflict is pollinator-assemblage-dependent, not census-only

**2026-10-10 — Exact replay of original 384 pilot trajectories plus a same-diploid-genotype/identical-census visitor-assemblage replacement. MODEL-INTERNAL, POST-OUTCOME, NO NEW ECOLOGICAL HISTORY.**

## Scientific question resolved at an intermediate mechanistic level

The original Model3 384-cell finite focal-investment audit showed **14 cases with focal β negative but population-wide collective viable seed Γ_seed positive**, all with floral investment, no assurance conflicts in that grid. A subsequent direct source ledger audit verified **positive net maternal viable seed effects on OTHER conspecific plants for all 14 cases**, via pollen export, not directly by adding new visitor individuals. At the static monomorphic genotype state [.2,.35,.35], pollen background B48 and four fixed visitor functional types, a complete N1–48 gradient scan found a **N6–9 individual-negative/group-positive** investment conflict window. The crucial unresolved issue was whether that window remained meaningful in **genetically variable populations whose visitor communities changed over time**.

**Answer:** With the same N6–9, genetic parents and calendar year, the majority β-negative/Γ_seed-positive conflict can switch on or off depending on the pollinator assemblage fed into the reproductive ledger. The inferred N6–9 conflict is **conditional on the visitor environment**, not a density-only universal rule. An observed beta/Gamma_seed conflict is still NOT a realized down-selection on investment over 80 years, an individual-vs-population long-term fitness crossover, or evolutionary suicide.

## Exact instantaneous visitor-community intervention at identical genotype and N

Correlation between viewer functional richness and conflict under a dynamic program is not a causal test: plant genomes, census and visitor types all co-evolve in source simulation time. We therefore performed a **source-matched visitor-only ledger replacement** on the 96 original *dynamic-visitor, native investment-expression* paths (16 original source seeds ×2 future K ×3 source budgets).

At each of the **1,533** original annual native states with N6–9, the current actual parental diploid genotypes, year t, n, future K, B48 and source cost/reproductive setting were held fixed. The *original* source ledger's β for each surviving plant and group Γseed were calculated once with the actual stochastic visitor community, and a second time with **the originally hand-authored fixed four types** ([.15,.35,.55,.75], bandwidth .18, effectiveness1). **The counterfactual visitor assemblage was NEVER used for actual reproduction/next-year genotypes or census**. Because visitor abundance AND visitor optimum distribution change together, this is not a pure experimental manipulation of visitor richness alone.

## Limits and next discriminating test

This is a *genuine model-operator intervention* on the instantaneous floral reproduction ledger **within one static artificial 4-visitor alternative**, not 1,533 independent stochastic visitor-population replicates. It does not show that increasing investment attracts additional insect individuals to the island, or that the static four-type assemblage is a natural ecological baseline. It also cannot distinguish **visitor count** effects from **visitor optimum/composition** effects or test the evolution of visitor response to flowers.

Future scientifically useful model decomposition should cross **visitor richness held constant × changes in visitor-type optimum distribution**, at matched same genotype/N, *without deriving a publication-level positive claim from this particular visited model's apparent 835 rescued source states*. Independent biological kernels or empirical pollen parentage and visit observations are then essential for general ecological claims.

**Publication-grade framing:** Positive conspecific pollen-mediated reproductive externalities exist in the actual Model3 reproductive ledger; whether these externalities create an individual-versus-group benefit conflict depends jointly on **density AND visitor assembly**. Realized selection/genomic investment change and a reliable, nonsaturated H80 population persistence effect remain unproven. Therefore a continued-commons-tragedy or evolutionary-suicide claim is **not currently supported**.
