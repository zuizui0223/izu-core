This specification is fixed before computing the additional access summaries. No response threshold, mutation rule, cell, cohort, horizon or original verdict may change.

## Fixed definitions

For occupied census t, current standing support is adequate for the original founder-relative investment reduction precisely when `I0 - min(all I homolog values at t) >= 0.05`. This is a necessary condition for an immediate mean response from existing alleles; it is not sufficient for selection, transmission, a sustained response or survival. In the renewal arm, a deficit is NOT permanent genetic impossibility, because later mutation is allowed. Do not treat extinction as a support deficit: its support field is missing.

Recompute the original investment event with the original arithmetic `I0 - mean_I(t) >= 0.05`, 20 consecutive occupied censuses, horizon200. Record both onset and completion time. A deficit-to-adequate transition is a standing-support reopening. For the no-I-mutation/no-immigration original arm, any reopening is a source/logic error. In the renewal arm, every such transition must contain a newly appearing threshold-capable I allele absent from the preceding census, and its mutation flag must be true. This is a law-bound transition check, not a mediation estimate.

For each arm record: ever deficit, first deficit time, ever reopening after a deficit, first reopening time, investment event, and whether event completion follows a reopening. An event completed before the first deficit must be reported separately; it cannot be attributed to reopening. Any event satisfying the mean predicate while standing support is deficient is a hard error. Record occupied final census separately. Tabulate the paired event-status2x2 table, without a new test, intervals or pooled island inference.

## Interpretation and stop rules

The decomposition asks whether enabling mutation actually reopened standing support in these saved histories and whether that was followed by the original sustained event. It cannot isolate variance maintenance, explain the separate320-history density gap, establish natural-order benefit or make post-outcome successful paths an independent sample. All18 original simultaneous order intervals retain their unresolved verdict.

Stop on an absent/extra case, changed hash, initial-state mismatch, invalid occupancy/offsets/alleles/means, original-arm reopening or unsupported mutation-transition explanation. Do not repair raw arrays or silently omit a case. Synthetic tests must cover deficit with remaining variance, reopening without a mean event, sustained-event onset versus completion, extinction/missing support, floating comparison consistency and the forbidden original reopening. An independent saved-record reviewer must check the derivation before scientific integration. No additional native update is authorized by this plan.
