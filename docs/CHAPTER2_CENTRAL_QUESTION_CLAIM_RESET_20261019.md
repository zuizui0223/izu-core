### The question and carefully bounded answer

**中心質問：島嶼で繁殖上の機能的な応答が反復する一方、花形態はなぜ一方向に収束しないのか。送粉者に対する決定論的な選択と、有限集団での確率的な遺伝的実現が、そのずれをどこまで説明できるのか。**

**Working thesis (not a demonstrated natural-island causal law):** *A recurrent reproductive constraint can be associated with recurrent reproductive function without imposing one floral phenotype. Directional reproductive returns (A), visitor-specific matching optima (M), and allocation-dependent investment under inherited/finite-population realization (I) offer three distinguishable mechanisms rather than one compulsory selfing-syndrome pathway.*

There are two nested questions, not two competing manuscripts:

1. **Chapter 1 / macrophenomenon:** Which contemporary island-flora reproductive functions show recurring isolation associations, and which colour/architectural traits vary among regions? This is **observed assemblage association**, not necessarily within-lineage evolution.
2. **Chapter 2 / conditional mechanism:** Given the specified finite diploid Model 3, how do local selection, standing alleles, visitor-specific matching optima and finite offspring recruitment distinguish **A endpoint recurrence**, **M visitor-history-linked change**, **I context-dependent realization**, and subsequent **genetic target access versus survival**? These are model experiments, not a fit of Model 3 parameter values to the Chapter 1 regions.

### Causal and conceptual sequence for exposition — NOT a proven serial mediation

1. **Ecological constraint:** island isolation and pollen shortage are associated in the empirical flora layer; the Model 3 manipulates synthetic visitor availability and function.
2. **Selection and matching:** A can provide conditional viable-selfed-seed benefits from unfertilized ovules; M changes functional correspondence to visitor optima; I scales flower investment and pollen delivery/export against allocation costs. Selection on I can have **different signs without genetic drift**.
3. **Realization filter:** genetic support, founder genotypes, visitor history, finite parentage and demographic draws determine which favoured changes and orders actually occur. M's stronger source-history predictive signal can coexist with I's nonlinear, context-dependent outcome; neither residual variability nor endpoint concentration measures a pure chance fraction.
4. **Persistence/access filter:** assurance-policy effects on reproductive persistence and on retention of a specified future I target need not have the same direction across resources.

The arrows organize *separately supported* evidence families; they are not demonstrated historical transitions among natural islands, and they do not identify one causal selection → drift → allele-loss → survival pathway.

### Distinct terms that must remain distinct

- Chapter 1 **SC/self-compatibility**: capacity for self-fertilization; not necessarily autonomous selfing or realized selfing fraction.
- Model 3 **A**: inherited capacity for reproductive assurance; fixed expression 0.5 still allows selfing. Neither synonymous with SC nor guaranteed to rise.
- Model 3 **I**: abstract floral reproductive investment, not observed flower size, flower colour or nectar-guide expression.
- Model 3 **M**: functional matching to visitors, separate from the amount invested in flowers. The 40.15% M predictability estimate is not an observed floral-colour predictability estimate.
- **Conditional selection direction**, **within-context stochastic endpoint spread**, **across-source prediction**, **allelic target support**, and **survival** are different estimands. Never collapse them into a single “determinism score.”
- **Floristic filtering** (which species are present) differs from **within-lineage evolution** (how a persisting species' inherited trait changes). Chapter 1 does not currently identify their separate historical contributions.

## Working hypothesis, not a predetermined trait ranking

Pollinator reduction changes the reproductive returns on assurance A and floral
investment I through distinct mechanisms. Investment decline need not require
assurance evolution. Which response occurs first, its realized magnitude and
its reversibility depend on selection, inherited variation and finite population
processes. Test which aspects repeat under identical conditions rather than
declaring A deterministic and I stochastic in advance.

Private benefits do not prove deterministic evolution; nonzero costs, mating
timing and inbreeding depression affect A selection. Shared benefits do not by
themselves prove weak I selection. Include maternal and paternal outcross returns
and viable selfing. A capacity is not the realized selfing fraction, and fixed
A does not mean absence of selfing.

## Completed baseline-pollen factorial and variation clocks (2026-10-19)

The new [384-path factorial](CHAPTER2_BASELINE_POLLEN_384_RESULTS_20261019.md)
is distinct from Q2's completed 384-path support intervention. All paths survived.
Additional activity decline produced larger A responses after weak than strong
baseline limitation: weak-minus-strong interactions +0.103678 and +0.083550
for nominal initial A=0.3 and 0.5, with descriptive founder-bootstrap 95%
intervals [0.069499,0.137804] and [0.041450,0.128147]. This is exploratory evidence
from 12 founders; the two starts share founders and are not independent replications.
Strong-limitation populations had already increased A, and 22/48 or 10/48
prefixes were A-monomorphic versus 1/48 in each weak stratum. The factorial
identifies a baseline-effectiveness regime/history interaction, not instantaneous
pollen deficit alone or fixation as the sole mediator. Additional I responses
were negative in strong and positive in weak baseline regimes; universal I
decline under every activity reduction is not supported.

The [stored-genome readback](CHAPTER2_VARIATION_CLOCK_RESULTS_20261019.md)
adds no simulations. At B8 with complete survival, evolving assurance shortened
occupied I-polymorphic duration by 2.628 updates; at B4.5 it extended that
duration by 12.684 alongside substantially improved survival. Fixed-policy A
alleles are latent genetic variation despite constant expressed A=0.5.
Most absent I polymorphism in the transferred seed cohorts was already absent
in donors; transfer added two I monomorphisms in evolving-history cohorts.

The current mechanism statement is therefore conditional: baseline pollen
service shapes selection and prior trait change; inherited variation and
persistence shape subsequent realization. Assurance and investment remain
separable, but neither universal temporal order nor a universal assurance-driven
loss of future adaptability follows. Natural island-flora filtering, genetic
mediation and permanent irreversibility remain outside the established evidence.
The [working article](CHAPTER2_UNIFIED_NEE_ARTICLE_WORKING_20261017.md) incorporates
these positive effects, sign changes and limitations without rewriting earlier
registered null results or adding simulations beyond the frozen allocation.
