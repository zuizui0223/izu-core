# Bounded local test of the remaining central hypothesis

**Current design, superseding the expanded factorial proposal.** On 2026-10-19
the user directed that the experiment stay within the central hypothesis and be
feasible on the actual PC. The 68-million-update / 437-GiB proposal is withdrawn.
Implementation and synthetic technical testing are authorized; production is not.

## What this experiment answers

After pollination restriction, do assurance A and investment I respond repeatedly
to a new pollination environment, or do inherited allele losses limit that
response? Compare repeated direction, attained trait state, timing and
destination reproductive performance. M is retained because it mediates floral
matching, not added as a new central hypothesis. Neither original island
colonization history nor flora-level filtering is inferred from this experiment.

The completed 160 x 160 confirmation already supplies the large-source evidence
for its registered G/S contrasts. Do not rerun that confirmation or use this
small conditional follow-up to replace its inference.

Removed: cost-zero future, functional-composition dispersion contrast, third
activity/optimum levels and extra assay dates. No new biological hypothesis is
introduced to compensate for their removal. This tests one moderate matching
shift (.5 to .7), not all possible new pollinator communities.

Donor selection uses fixed indices without examining which donors give favorable
outcomes. It is a systematic subset of a known archive, not new independent
founder evidence. Report the exact conditional source population and its limits.
Produce viable offspring with the native donor policy before allowing inherited
A expression in the destination. All transferred viable offspring establish at
entry by design; this isolates subsequent response and is not a dispersal or
establishment-filtering experiment. Retain seedless donors and later extinctions
in all-start denominators.

## PC evidence and operational limits

The inspected PC has an i9-13900H, 14 physical / 20 logical cores, approximately
16 GiB RAM and 57.9 GB free disk. At the query, free physical memory was only
about 39 MiB: **no production should start in that state**. Do not close unrelated
applications or processes automatically.

The [preflight receipt](audits/CHAPTER2_READAPTATION_LOCAL_PREFLIGHT_20261019.json)
records synthetic full-capacity operator measurements: about .55-1.36 ms/update,
and .196 ms per focal common-context assay. These are technical operations, not
futures from the scientific donors. Linear serial projections give roughly
7.5-18.6 minutes for updates, up to 3.2 minutes for uncached assays, and about
3.2 minutes for illustrative compression. Admission, disk I/O, verification,
memory pressure and full replay are additional costs. A 30-60 minute working
estimate is provisional, not a completed-run measurement or guarantee.

Full raw state plus parentage upper allocation is 5.64 GB (5.25 GiB) before
containers and telemetry. Budget 8 GiB output and require at least 10 GiB disk
free at start; verify streaming archive size on synthetic fixtures. Require at
least 1 GiB available physical memory. Run one trajectory at a time, persist a
validated checkpoint, and release its arrays before the next trajectory. Do not
load all donor archives or futures into RAM. Pause/resume by task key and hash
at resource limits; never truncate the registered allocation or change the
statistical horizon because a wall-clock limit is reached.

## Interpretation and remaining implementation

Report empirical direction probabilities and full endpoint distributions, with
pointwise exact intervals. Even 32/32 identical outcomes leave a one-sided 95%
failure-probability upper bound of 8.94%; call this repeatability, not proof of
determinism. Do not pool different cohorts to pretend they share identical
initial conditions. Trait direction among survivors and all-start occupancy
must be reported separately.

Performance is evaluated against 47 fixed reference competitors in the same
destination, using maternal/paternal outcross contribution plus viable selfing.
Compare with each cohort's initial performance in that context; increased I or
M change alone is not evidence of adaptation. Archive strict/epsilon allele
support diagnostics without changing their original target meanings.

The strict proposed contract and seed-transfer implementation have 22 passing
focused tests, covering the reduced allocation, source hashes, false production
authorization, native donor-policy seed production, parental orientation,
Mendelian origins, capped seed supply and the five destination controls.
Full stream admission checks 10,304 distinct cohort/future master seeds.
Future telemetry, common assays, resource controls and independent raw audit
still require implementation and review before launch. This document does not
assert they are complete.
