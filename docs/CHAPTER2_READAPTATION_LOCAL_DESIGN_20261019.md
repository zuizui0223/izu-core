## What this experiment answers

After pollination restriction, do assurance A and investment I respond repeatedly
to a new pollination environment, or do inherited allele losses limit that
response? Compare repeated direction, attained trait state, timing and
destination reproductive performance. M is retained because it mediates floral
matching, not added as a new central hypothesis. Neither original island
colonization history nor flora-level filtering is inferred from this experiment.

The completed 160 x 160 confirmation already supplies the large-source evidence
for its registered G/S contrasts. Do not rerun that confirmation or use this
small conditional follow-up to replace its inference.

### Founder-relative support diagnostics

Targets are the original founder mean allele values for I and A, reconstructed
from the declared founder seed and bound to each admitted donor receipt. They
are not estimated from transferred cohorts or favorable future outcomes.
For each occupied census, high-I support requires maximum I allele >= founder
I minus epsilon; low-A support requires minimum A allele <= founder A plus
epsilon. Report all epsilon values 0, .005, .01, .02 and .05, using a fixed
1e-12 numerical comparison tolerance. Retain entry and terminal support and the
first occupied census excluding each target. Extinct censuses have undefined
support and extinction time is reported separately. These bounds do not prove
realized recovery, its probability, or increased reproductive performance.
Without original-source admission the report leaves these diagnostics undefined.
