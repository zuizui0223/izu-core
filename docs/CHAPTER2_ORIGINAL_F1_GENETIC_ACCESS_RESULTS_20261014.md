## Main result and change in mechanism interpretation

The initial assortment has **exactly the same expressed reproductive-assurance value A=0.5 in every founder**, so at t0 expressed A variance and the initial full-parent expected Price response from A are zero. Yet the original founders already have the A alleles that later segregate. In the **nine original frozen original-source ZIPs, all 2,304 individual-genotype trajectories were independently reread**:

| Original-genome question | Source-rechecked result |
|---|---:|
| A expressed-phenotype variance at t0 = 0 | **2,304 / 2,304** |
| A expressed-phenotype variance at first offspring census (t1) positive | **2,304 / 2,304** |
| At t1 ≥1 adult has expressed A at least founder A+0.05 | **2,304 / 2,304** |
| Same t1 support exists among **A-mutation-flag-negative individuals** | **2,304 / 2,304** |
| t1 census with **zero flagged A-mutant offspring** | **864 / 2,304** |
| F1 expressed A variance (all 2,304; population-variance convention) | mean **0.0019303168**, median **0.0019327101**; range **0.0012953–0.0025770** |

**Consequential refinement:** for the *original fixed-access* genetic design, observable within-population A phenotypic variance and a no-new-A-mutation individual capable of exceeding the +0.05 threshold are both present already at **t1 in every run**. Thus “how many generations until the first A phenotypic segregation/access” has **no cross-history variation** and cannot by itself explain different ordering or long arrival times in that original cohort. The previously stated two-stage conceptual clock (segregation-access then sustained response) must not assign its observed stochastic response-order variation to a variable *first-generation segregation delay* when all source histories had t1 access.

The first F1 variance is evidence of **post-meiotic expressed availability**, not of positive direction of selection, successful lineage persistence or a change in population mean. It is **post-t0** and cannot be used to claim a founder-state-only prospective six-class predictor. Original heterozygote standing alleles are already available at t0 even though all founder expressions are 0.5; “wait until F1 expressed variation” is a chosen *phenotypic-access diagnostic*, not a literal mutation waiting time.

## Why early expression does not imply sustained evolutionary arrival

Reapplying the original founder-relative +0.05 A increase / −0.05 I decrease, requiring **20 consecutive occupied annual censuses** (same original tie cutoff ±5), yields the **exact original six-category source totals**:

|---|---:|
| A assurance first | 335 |
| I investment first | 435 |
| Near simultaneous | 358 |
| A only | 895 |
| I only | 10 |
| Neither | 271 |

The **independent raw-mean 201-census reconstructions matched every single original producer's 0.05 A/I onset year and category** (2,304/2,304). All case NPZ SHA-256 digests matched their *own* original `_COMPLETION.json` receipts. Both 128-repeat original founder blocks in all nine visitor conditions remained intact: **18 founder×ecology cells, 256 founder×paired-replicate history clusters**.

| Population-average threshold event (not individual F1 genotype) | A assurance | I investment |
|---|---:|---:|
| Transiently crosses original ±0.05 at least once | **2,106** | **1,339** |
| Reaches a 20-census sustained event | **2,023** | **1,138** |
| Crosses transiently but never sustains 20 censuses | **83** | **201** |
| Median *sustained* event onset, conditional on being observed | **21** | **14** |
| Range of observed sustained onset, annual censuses | **4–181** | **2–170** |

A sustained A event is seen in **87.8% of original histories** despite all histories having t1 A segregation and t1 unmutated high-genotype support. A sustained I event is observed in **49.4%**. These numbers are **descriptive across paired streams and visitor contexts**, not two independent species or estimates of selection-versus-drift causality. Conditioning medians on *event observed* is subject to right censoring/competing extinction and must not be interpreted as an unbiased “A evolves slower than I” result.

The original t0 Price response remains 0 for A; the native genetic source process generated expression variance at t1 without requiring a first-generation A mutation. Subsequent **multi-generation genetic selection, drift, reproductive feedback, mutation, fixation, threshold-crossing fluctuations and event censoring** are plausible mechanisms of stable-arrival variation. **This audit does not apportion causality among them**, nor does it fit a tested two-stage arrival-time law.

## What this predicts for the registered unrun 2×2 pilot

The original 2,304 paths all begin at a single expressed-A access width; the new frozen protocol creates a genuine **mutation-free structural threshold-support contrast** by replacing the founder assurance homologs with **w=0.046875** (highest possible no-mutation A phenotype 0.546875, cannot reach founder+0.05) or **w=0.078125** (can reach). A remains mutating at the unchanged rate0.01, SD0.05; low width is therefore *not strictly unreachability in the real mutating Model3*. The hidden-I h0/h1 manipulation preserves every founding adult's expressed M/I/A phenotypes, but h0 does **not** eliminate between-adult I variation. Both manipulations and their ecological interactions require the next original-seed factorial's **genuine new histories**.

**Refined Q1→Q2 question:** Is it the accessibility of a **population-level 0.05 response given the founder's segregating allele support and later mutation**, together with the stochastic stability of selected responses, rather than *waiting for the first expression of any phenotypic A variance*, that controls the probability of A-first versus I-first versus simultaneous/one-trait-only/nonarrival?
