## Detectability of the registered 128-cluster exploratory pilot

The new pilot has **128 independent founder×paired random seed clusters**, with 12 nested original ecology/genetic conditions each (1,536 actual trajectories after approval). Nominal probability that the *lower endpoint of a two-sided 95% normal CI* on the positive matched cluster mean exceeds 0 is only an **assumption-level** calculation:

\[P(\bar\Delta-z_{.975}\sigma/\sqrt n>0) \approx \Phi(\Delta\sqrt{n}/\sigma-z_{.975}).\]

There is **no actual pilot \(\sigma\)** and the five planned paired biological genetic contrasts use different scales (`A_incidence` from [−1,1], `D` from [−2,2], interaction from [−4,4]). The following rows therefore illustrate possible sensitivity, **not effect estimates or measured power**:

| Assumed SD of paired genetic contrast | Minimum positive Δ for approximately 80% power (all 128 clusters) | Within-founder 64 clusters |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.25 | 0.062 | 0.088 |
| 0.50 | 0.124 | 0.175 |
| 0.80 | 0.198 | 0.280 |
| 1.00 | 0.248 | 0.350 |

For a hypothetical **Δ=0.10**, n=128 gives approximately 62% nominal directional success probability at SD=0.50 and 29% at SD=0.80. For Δ=0.20, those become 99% and 81%. These illustrative values omit fitted-model uncertainty and any multiplicity adjustment, and only describe scenario-level asymptotic precision. The same fixed 128 seeds may be statistically informative for **large causal genotype-initialization contrasts** without providing strong evidence that a *two-stage clock predicts unseen six-way outcomes better than a flexible nonlinear ecological/genetic null*.

**A separate existing calculation** (`data/results/chapter2_clock_power_envelope_20261013.json`) concerns *later independent confirmatory six-category Log loss*, assumes Δ=0.02, uses an unrelated scenario SD0.15 and suggests ≥512 independent confirmation clusters under those assumptions. It must **not** be represented as achieved power of this first factorial pilot, which preregistered only five descriptive matched contrasts and an unknown intervention-score variance.

## Biological interpretation and no-go gates

- **A width:** `w=0.046875` vs `w=0.078125` changes founder heterozygote segregation support. Without mutation, the narrow w cannot reach +0.05 above founder A mean; the broad w can in principle. With **registered A mutation rate 0.01**, the narrow condition is *not absolutely inaccessible*, and the experiment tests an access-width/mutation-mediated process, not an instantaneous hard clock.
- **I hidden homolog:** `h=0` vs `h=1` changes within-individual homolog difference while holding each founder plant's *expressed I* unchanged. `h=0` is not zero across-plant standing genetic variation; outcrossing among different adults can create segregating variation in descendants. Do **not** interpret it as an I mutation manipulation or a clean zero-variance condition.
- **Statistical grain:** preserve 64 seeds per founder, 3 environmental regimes and all four treatment arms within each of 128 founder×seed clusters. Do not turn 1,536 nested tracks into independent sample size, and do not claim generalization from only two genotypic founder templates or three fixed ecological levels to natural island populations.
- **Primary goal:** after honest execution and full original genomic re-read, report all six class frequencies and the five precommitted matched contrasts. A strong positive access effect would give controlled mechanistic *support within the synthetic model*, not prove natural-island genetic process or Q4 order-caused demographic benefit.
