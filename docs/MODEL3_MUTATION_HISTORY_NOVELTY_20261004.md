# Model 3: mutation, history, and the narrow novelty boundary

Research date: 2026-10-04. This is a focused, non-exhaustive primary-source audit for an Evolution Letters framing, not a systematic review, priority certification, or prediction of journal acceptance. No simulation source, parameters, or running campaign were changed.

## Assessment

Finite-population constraints on adaptation, historical effects under a common environment, mating-system-dependent genetic variance, and pollinator-loss-driven selfing are established. More directly, a 2025 preprint already models failure to return to outcrossing after pollinators are restored. Therefore neither “evolution remembers history” nor “restoring pollinators need not restore the ancestral phenotype” is a defensible standalone novelty claim. The references below identify overlapping questions, not exact duplicates of Model 3.

A potentially distinctive contribution is a **quantitative, mechanistically separated prediction for the duration and trait composition of evolutionary recovery after different visitor-immigration histories**, with separately inherited access, investment, and assurance, continued mutation, and explicitly finite populations. This remains a candidate contribution. Three traits, an ABM, or a PDE representation by themselves do not establish substantive novelty.

## Current result and ceiling

The task-provided frozen result is delayed selfing, cost 0.5, mutation 0.01, after 200 historical periods and 800 common-environment periods: far-history minus near-history investment = -0.130106 and assurance = +0.055755. These are a bounded finite-horizon pattern, not evidence of irreversible evolution or alternative stable equilibria. The execution ledger records capacity 48, uncalibrated reproductive periods, no plant immigration, joint evolution of all three traits, and a distinction between occupied-arm summaries and paired jointly occupied contrasts. This research note does not independently recompute those values.

The positive-mutation density refinement gate failed in the completed campaign. The 13-node refinement was active when this assignment began; its eventual result is not assumed here. **A finite-ABM-minus-converged-density difference is presently not established.** Nor does numerical convergence of a heat operator establish its equivalence to birth-event jump mutation. See [execution ledger](MODEL3_FULL_MUTATION_LEDGER_20261004.md) and [full results](MODEL3_FULL_MUTATION_RESULTS_20261004.md).

## Closest theoretical comparison: inspect the process, not just the title

The [Acoca-Pidolle preprint PDF](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/object/10067/download/18650/) was read in full, including its appendix. It combines floral size/herkogamy into one trait, holds its standard deviation fixed, and includes delayed selfing, fixed inbreeding depression, maternal/paternal fitness and pollen carryover. Evolutionary direction compares fitness at mean plus/minus three standard deviations. Its analytic threshold differs from an 800-period recovery delay. Text and Figure 2 caption interchange hardness-endpoint labels; an equation-level reproduction must resolve that inconsistency.

That prior model makes a selection-feedback explanation a serious comparator. Model 3's separate traits and explicit inheritance could change that explanation, but extra resolution is useful only if it changes a prediction or discriminates mechanisms. The present positive-mutation density failure prevents that discrimination from being attributed to finiteness.

## Controls needed for stronger claims

These are prospective discriminating controls, not authorizations to alter frozen runs or descriptions of completed tests.

1. **Separate delayed recovery from distinct destinations.** Retain full trajectories and the predeclared terminal-change summaries; estimate recovery relative to a declared tolerance and horizon. A nonzero contrast at period 800 supplies no proof of irreversibility. Longer runs should be prospectively bounded and retained even if the difference disappears.
2. **Separate founding means from available variation.** At the common-environment switch, compare full joint genotype distributions, allelic support, variance/covariance and selfing/heterozygosity. A variance-restoration or distribution-swap intervention must preserve the intended mean and explicitly declare what other properties it changes. More mutation alone changes multiple processes and cannot identify the mediator.
3. **Separate demographic sampling from mating/selection effects.** A predeclared capacity series and matched mating/inheritance controls can test finite-size scaling. Hold ecology comparable; capacity changes may also alter pollination and density regulation. Report extinction and occupancy separately to expose survivor conditioning. Current capacity-48 results alone contain no scaling evidence.
4. **Separate deterministic genetic feedback from finite stochasticity.** Compare matched founders, visitor histories, demography, selfing timing, reproductive accounting and jump kernels. Establish density-grid convergence of relevant distributions and contrasts before interpreting an ABM difference as a finite-population effect. Four projected-founder histories are numerical controls, not population-level precision.
5. **Separate jump-mutation biology from the heat approximation.** Compare birth-event kernels and boundary behavior directly; show where any small-step approximation is adequate. Approximation and grid errors are different. Neither an algebraic implementation test nor terminal mean agreement establishes both.
6. **Separate reproductive assurance from genetic-load evolution.** A fixed cost permits a conditional ecological prediction. Claims about purging, inbreeding depression feedback or selfing dead ends require those mechanisms to be represented and independently tested; they cannot be supplied by biological analogy.

## Defensible wording now and a stronger candidate later

**Supported description, conditional on the frozen result audit:** In this model and parameter setting, different visitor-immigration histories leave investment and reproductive-assurance differences after 800 periods under a shared visitor environment despite continued mutation. The duration, ecological scope and genetic mechanism remain model-dependent.

**Potential contribution if the controls support it:** Identify when independently evolving access, investment and assurance recover on different schedules after a shared ecological restoration, and quantify which component of the lag arises from available genetic variation, mating structure, deterministic feedback, or finite sampling. A cross-setting rule or discriminating empirical prediction would be more substantial than relabeling a residual contrast as memory.

**Withhold:** “first demonstration,” permanent genetic trapping, irreversible loss, bistability, a new general island law, mutation-independent memory, and a finite-versus-converged-density mechanism. A failed numerical gate is an unresolved comparison, not a biological result.
