## Causal arrows explicitly NOT established

1. **Q1 → Q2:** lower pollen delivery changes reproductive returns and events, but no cross-cohort proof that an earlier A selection gradient *caused* the observed inherited A-first classification. Ordering is threshold-, mating-, visitor- and founder-dependent.
2. **Q2 → Q3:** A-first 51/64, artificial scheduling ROPE, and registered I-carrier inconclusiveness **do not** identify A-first as causing later high-I closure. The 703 lower-I exclusions and 1,455 same-cohort higher-I exclusions are directionally distinct retrospective outcomes.
3. **Q3 → Q4:** G is identified in B8 with complete survival; S is identified across B8/B4.5 budgets. Because their endpoints, resource exposures and competing extinction are different, **no genetic-closure-mediated survival pathway** is identified.
4. **Q4 → future adaptation:** more survivors do not prove faster flower recovery. The transfer starts from post-treatment-selected donor genotypes, has 32 donors but multiple nested descendant trajectories, and often lacks standing I polymorphism despite a retained target-compatible allele.
5. **Four parts → natural islands:** neither model traits nor imposed synthetic visitor histories identify natural geographic loss, historical causal sequence, phylogenetic filtering or the causes of species presence/absence on real islands.

## Non-obvious, supportable paper conclusion

**The same pollination constraint can alter the ecological payoff of flowers, the order in which inherited traits cross chosen thresholds, which alleles remain available, and the probability of population persistence in different and sometimes opposing ways.** In this model, evolving autonomous assurance is **not required** for an investment decline. Under a favorable-resource registered regime, A evolution increases the probability of losing the *high-I genetic support bound* even with full survival, whereas under a severe-resource regime it preserves occupancy and increases the *time populations remain alive and polymorphic*. Thus investment reduction, inherited access and persistence are not a universal selfing-syndrome clock or a single monotone loss-of-adaptability trajectory.

The decisive new combination is the **conditional dissociation of persistence, target accessibility, and standing variation** superimposed on the original **selection–order–realization–consequence** framework. Do not claim a newly proved general theory of irreversible floral evolution. The known mathematical allele-support containment theorem requires no I mutation or immigration; it does not extend to future mutation-enabled recovery without further study.
