## Trait returns, environmental contrasts and order

The original Q1 result concerns low versus high visitor replenishment in four mating settings; fresh-founder transport supports compression in three of four. Both original near and far conditions are pollen limited. The 384-path baseline factorial instead changes visitor effectiveness and later activity. A positive I response to an additional activity decline in a weak-limitation regime therefore cannot be suppressed to preserve a universal small-flower narrative. Sources: S1–S2, [fresh-founder result](CHAPTER2_Q1_FRESH_FOUNDER_RESULTS_20261016.md), [factorial](CHAPTER2_BASELINE_POLLEN_384_RESULTS_20261019.md).

The original A-first counts, the retrospective A-width comparison and the newer I-carrier experiment answer different questions. The A-width contrast (+0.669271 in the six-category order score) comes from two founder backgrounds and changes initial support, variance and threshold geometry with A mutation enabled. The I-carrier experiment's two registered primary intervals both include zero. These do not identify a pure variance-mediated ordering mechanism. Sources: S8/S20, [paired clock](CHAPTER2_GENETIC_ACCESS_PAIRED_CLOCK_20261014.md), [registered Q2](CHAPTER2_Q2_I_SUPPORT_384_REGISTERED_RESULT_20261016.md).

The 1,024-path abrupt/gradual study holds the final visitor composition equal, and calibrates cumulative pollen only in a reference clone. It changes functional composition chronology, not insect abundance. All eight setting contrasts, including null and negative ones, remain in [the full result](CHAPTER2_SEQUENCE_ABRUPT_GRADUAL_FUNCTIONAL_LOSS_RESULTS_20261011.md). The four assurance-cost settings have greater by-100 A-event probability under abrupt change; all arms remain occupied. These observations do not establish a survival benefit of naturally evolving A first.

## Predictability, support and realization

The initial-state 48.48% six-category lookup used observed outcomes to choose a modal class in each of 18 groups. It is not prospective accuracy or a universal ceiling. Two-axis holdout excludes both tested environmental context and seed fold; the t0 full-parent response improves simpler predictors but does not robustly outperform flexible ecological/genetic alternatives. Both founders remain represented in training, so it is not a new-founder validation. Sources: S18–S19, [identifiability](CHAPTER2_INITIAL_CLOCK_IDENTIFIABILITY_20261013.md), [double holdout](CHAPTER2_INITIAL_CLOCK_TWO_AXIS_CV_20261013.md).

The 703 pre-fixation LOW-I exclusions and 1,455 HIGH-I exclusions reuse the same 2,304-history cohort for different targets. Density/finite discrepancies (3/32 and 2/32 A-first) concern another cohort. Price accounting closes a conditional decomposition, not stochastic variation itself, and cannot assign the whole density gap to drift. Sources: S7/S14/S21, [source map](CHAPTER2_Q3_SELECTION_REALIZATION_SOURCE_MAP_20261015.md), [high-I readout](audits/CHAPTER2_NEE_HIGH_I_SUPPORT_SOURCE_READOUT_20261017.json).

## Genetic intervention results, including their nulls

The original 64-pair restoration primary remains unresolved. The conditional follow-up used 61 previously fixed states, 64 future pairs per state and three zero-policy slots. It restored symmetric I homologs without changing initial individual mean I. Native-minus-restored terminal I was +0.016745 overall, +0.020934 in the main block and +0.012556 in the confirmation block. Both directional intervals exclude zero; the confirmation block does not establish an effect exceeding 0.01. The effect is further I decline, not ancestral flower recovery. Futures do not provide new independent founders. Sources: S17/S23, [conditional follow-up](CHAPTER2_RESTORATION_CONDITIONAL_FUTURES_RESULTS_20261014.md).

The 26,352-path distribution experiment compares carrier distributions, including equal-support and equal-variance pairs. The main-block equal-support event contrast is +0.05772569 [0.00847761, 0.10697378], while the confirmation estimate is −0.00303819 [−0.05148544, 0.04540905]. No directional contrast is resolved in the same direction in both blocks. All terminal-mean directions remain unresolved, although some intervals satisfy declared operational equivalence bounds. Those bounds are not biological SESOIs. Source: S26, [all eight decisions](CHAPTER2_Q3_TRIANGLE_RESULTS_20261014.md).

Ecological order reversal remains unresolved in the fixed-access and mutation-renewal tests. In renewal, all 18 simultaneous cell intervals include zero. Of 914 histories with reopened I support, 758 did not complete the sustained mean response by 200, 64 completed it before first deficit and 92 after reopening. The 758 split into late reopening (33), no continuous 20-census support window (420), no mean threshold despite such a window (248), and some attainment without a completed sustained run (57). These are selected-path restrictions, not intervention-specific causal fractions. Sources: S30/S31, [renewal](CHAPTER2_RENEWAL_STANDING_ACCESS_RESULTS_20261015.md), [birth accounting](CHAPTER2_RENEWAL_NONRESPONSE_ACCOUNTING_RESULTS_20261015.md).

## Population consequences and conditional history

The original demographic family includes inconclusive primary expression-order effects and setting-dependent capacity/viability interventions. The fixed-B48 capacity/viability interaction was +0.0077457 [0.0024972, 0.0130155], while the resource-window confirmation failed and the timed-viability contrast remained inconclusive. They remain alongside the independent 32-history interaction +0.1083984375; the delayed-only +0.15234375 is not that pooled effect. Fixed-clone time/density sign reversals concern population interventions with genetic evolution disabled. None identifies a general survival advantage of natural A-first trajectories. Sources: S3/S4/S9–S13/S16/S22/S33 and F7/F8 in the disposition map.

Far-side genomic and parentage accounting documents time-dependent historical contrasts; it does not identify a unique drift or covariance share. A matched-genotype future-policy comparison does not reproduce a common positive later investment effect across the four settings. These constraints remain relevant even where the Q1 average interaction is supported. Sources: S32, [annual account](CHAPTER2_FAR_ORIGINAL_0_200_MECHANISM_RESULTS_20261015.md), [future-policy swap](CHAPTER2_FAR_T200_HISTORY_SWAP_64H_RESULTS_20261015.md).

## Eight-history restoration micro-pilot

This is distinct from the later equal-initial-A pilot. The eight-history pilot crossed two founders, two visitor histories and two A policies, all surviving through census 45. One evolving history lost founder-relative I support at census 39 after forced visitor removal at 35. A saved-state visitor-return assay gave positive local I and negative A return under assurance cost 0.5; the maximum I allele 0.5000 was below the founder mean 0.51171875. Its 144 continuations used environment-specific demographic seeds. Unconditional high-I attainment was 4/8 versus 3/8; regaining it among previously declined survivors was 0/2 versus 1/6. These nested, differently conditioned counts do not identify a randomized recovery effect. Sources: [clock readout](https://github.com/zuizui0223/izu-core/blob/analysis/chapter2-pollinator-recovery-pilot-20261018/docs/audits/CHAPTER2_RECOVERY_GENETIC_CLOCK_CI_READOUT_20261018.json), [denominator audit](https://github.com/zuizui0223/izu-core/blob/analysis/chapter2-pollinator-recovery-pilot-20261018/docs/audits/CHAPTER2_RECOVERY_POLICY_DENOMINATORS_READOUT_20261018.json).

## What coverage does not establish

No natural island filtering, evolving deleterious-load mechanism, full probability-defined recovery window or permanent irreversibility has been demonstrated by this synthesis. Retained historical unexecuted proposals remain unexecuted. A file inventory, source link, passing test or editorial gate alone is not independent scientific reproduction. Original source reports retain their denominators, stop records, hashes and exact uncertainty definitions.
