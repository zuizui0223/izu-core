## Frozen primary and decision

Primary: original minus restored terminal mean investment, averaged over all 64
paired histories. Positive means restored variation permits greater reduction.
If either member of any pair is extinct at the terminal census, this mean-trait
primary is not_evaluable. Never zero-fill an extinct trait or drop pairs.

Use 19,999 paired bootstrap draws, seed 2026101364, resampling within each fixed
32-history block and preserving equal block weights. Two-sided percentile 95%
interval. Support only if its lower bound is positive and each block estimate is
positive. Negative upper bound and both block estimates negative supports the
opposite direction. Otherwise unresolved; no equivalence claim or amplitude tuning.

Descriptive secondary outputs: full I mean/allele and expressed variance curves,
expected I response, census and terminal occupancy, maternal/paternal/outcross and
viable self output, realized selfing, A response and genotype persistence. Undefined
trait means remain null. These do not create new significance families.

## Stop rules and claim ceiling

Any source/hash/inventory/native replay drift stops intervention launch. A failed
case is not replaced. Completed outputs are not overwritten. Only technical
implementation repairs are allowed with evidence and unchanged frozen biology.
No pure natural-order benefit, unique causal fraction, island historical mechanism,
or broad absence of selection follows from this probe. A positive result supports
the causal importance of recoverable I variation under this particular intervention.
