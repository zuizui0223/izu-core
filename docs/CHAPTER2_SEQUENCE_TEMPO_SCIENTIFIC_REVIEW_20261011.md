## Fixed-time sampled gradients: context and limitations

All10 designated times were retained (t0,10,20,40,60,80,100,120,200,300), both traits, all settings, with positive/negative/near-zero/boundary/evaluated counts and eligible support in the complete factual tables. At t0, both source schedules have positive mean sampled investment medians (~.418 delayed;~.201–.204 prior) and positive assurance medians in all settings. At t40, abrupt investment sample-median means are negative in every setting (about−.307 to−.415); gradual values remain positive except prior/cost0, where they are already negative. This supports different **sampled temporal selection paths** within this model, but does not identify exact per-trajectory first sign-crossing times.

Feedback is visible: in both delayed/mutation.01 settings, investment sample-median means are again positive by t200/t300 in both schedules. In delayed/cost.5/mutation.01, the gradual value is already positive at100 while abrupt is slightly negative; at300 assurance medians can approach/cross zero. Thus no irreversible universal negative-selection clock should be inferred from the fixed-clonal prediction.

One source gradient median is unavailable for one gradual prior/cost0/mutation.01 trajectory at t200 (assurance), yielding63 rather than64 paired eligible trajectories; all16 profiles remain eligible. This is sampled-gradient availability, not extinction. Medians are conditional summaries of at most eight selected adults per trajectory, not population-wide selection estimates or independent individual replication. Averaging medians can also hide mixed sign counts; the full counts remain essential.

## Recommended factual Q1–Q4 spine update

- **Q1 (threshold):** Distinguish existing fixed-clonal sign-clock predictions from realized, genotype-dependent sampled gradients. Report the abrupt/gradual sampled paths and late mutation-associated reversals; neither identifies a universal ecological tipping point or an exact inherited first-crossing clock.
- **Q2 (order):** Add this registered environmental-tempo contrast alongside, not as replacement for, the assigned-expression and mutation-access experiments. Crossing probabilities and categorical trajectories differ by timing/cost/mutation context. Show A-only separately from A-first and both horizons. Do not claim a general mutation-order effect.
- **Q3 (realization):** This cohort now measures actual native finite inheritance and retained genomes/parentage under controlled visitor schedules. Report concrete trajectory/genotype/recruitment changes and loss/maintenance of standing mean variance. A local gradient is still not identical to a realized crossing, and passive parentage alone does not estimate genetic mediation.
- **Q4 (consequence):** Lower realized pollen/overlap and altered self/outcross recruitment do not identify persistence harm/benefit: every arm remains occupied at100/400, with recruitment near capacity. The model-specific occupancy endpoint is saturated. Do not promote the observed genetic trajectories into an evolutionary-suicide or rescue mechanism.

## Contradiction/missing-evidence risks

1. Treating A-only as A-first or using full400 categories to describe by100 primaries would materially change the result.
2. Declaring a general10pp effect ignores the zero/negative settings and interval uncertainty; this design registers descriptive intervals, not a new confirmatory significance/equivalence procedure.
3. Treating[0,0] occupancy intervals as demonstrated practical equivalence or proof against any possible benefit contradicts the pre-execution interpretation clarification.
4. Subtracting separately eligible lag means fabricates paired support where none exists. Two-profile apparent lag evidence is fragile and conditional.
5. Using lower schedule-relative I as a universal founder-relative decline contradicts the reported absolute means.
6. Treating equal reference pollen as equal actual evolving pollen contradicts both registration and observed realized histories.
7. Treating the assigned visitor schedule as randomized A/I mutation order or established selection→genotype→survival mediation is unsupported. These remain separate causal questions.
8. Earlier #411/#418/#472/#474 and independent K-moderation experiments retain their evidence ranks. This single16-profile synthetic cohort does not supply natural island/general-model validation.

No material contradiction in the complete readout prevents bounded result admission. The principal limits are inferential, especially saturated persistence, A-only prevalence, sparse paired lag support, and genotype-dependent selection feedback. Preserve all registered results and uncertainty, rather than selecting the largest costly-setting A contrast as the sole conclusion.
