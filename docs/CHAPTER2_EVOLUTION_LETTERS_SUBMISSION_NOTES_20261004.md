## Teaser text

Two evolutionary systems can look equally parallel in direction for opposite reasons. In a validated island-floral model, larger populations made history-specific evolutionary effects more reproducible, whereas averaging pollinator histories erased the history signal—even though both produced more uniform directional responses.
