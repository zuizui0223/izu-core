## Integrated reading order

Visitor replenishment and pollen supply change reproductive returns. Joint inequalities identify selection conditions; they do not supply realized timing. Parallel ABM/density propagation and inheritance determine attainable trajectories. Time-to-change measures order. Capacity interventions test necessity separately. Loss and supply of variation explain why a direction of selection need not be sustained as an evolutionary response. Pollen deficits and viable offspring measure consequences.
