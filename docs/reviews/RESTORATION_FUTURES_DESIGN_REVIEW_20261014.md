# Independent review of proposed future-stream design

Reviewed the restoration-access audit and its summary/independent receipt, the written conditional-futures protocol, preflight source and receipt, and relevant archived native randomness/population and original restoration-adapter source. No biological producer was run. A separate raw-only check below verifies design inputs and arithmetic, not new model behavior.

## Verdict

The final written design resolves the scientific-target, survival-conditioning, precision, replication and resource-rule concerns. It is scientifically defensible for a **fixed-state, coupling-specific, pair-survivor conditional diagnostic**, with no unconditional or new-founder claim. The 1,981,472-call hard limit explicitly covers fixtures, production, verification, failures and resumptions; extra full replay requires fresh approval. **No remaining design must-fix findings.** Production remains closed pending user approval and implementation admission. This verdict does not certify the yet-unwritten producer.

## Conditional coverage

Let independent future-pair draws be X_ir, with S_ir meaning both terminal arms are occupied, and let G be the intersection of all S_ir. Conditional on the fixed captured states and G, the joint law factorizes as the product of laws X_ir conditional on their own S_ir. Thus each state's R draws remain iid from its paired-survivor conditional law. Independence across states is enough; identical distributions across states are not required. This requires positive survival probability for every state and independent stream pairs. Shared within-pair randomness is permitted.

Since unchanged I equals v and restored I remains in [v-h,v+h], D lies in [-h,h]. For equally weighted original slots and R draws per triggered state, Hoeffding and a union bound over K=3 overlapping means give exactly the proposed half-width h sqrt(2 n_trigger log(2K/alpha)/(R Nslots^2)). Overlap of the three means does not invalidate the union bound. Conditional simultaneous coverage is at least 95% given G. This is a repeated-computation statement conditional on G, not evidence that extinction probability is zero or negligible.

For h=1/16, R=64, alpha=.05: pooled half-width .00295014927; main .00420619997; confirmation .00413780191. Minimum R for the .005 bound is respectively 23,46,44. R=64 is sufficient; describe it as a fixed batch choice rather than the minimal required size. Precision does not ensure a decisive result.

## Scientific conditions checked against the written protocol

1. **Target identity:** theta_i is E[I_control-I_restored | both survive under the declared coupling]. The conditioning stratum can change with coupling; it is a selected, cross-arm model contrast. Label every inference accordingly. Its all-slot mean averages state-specific survivor expectations equally, rather than pooling survivors with survival-probability weights. The three no-trigger slots are fixed policy-zero slots, not newly sampled surviving futures.
2. **Question answered:** CIs determine whether opposite expected signs persist for these fixed states and this survivor target. They do not determine how probable the old mixed-sign realization was, nor prove that drift, support, refixation, or timing uniquely caused it. Any predictive frequency of a single-future mixed-sign pattern needs its own frozen readout and uncertainty rule. Do not add that silently after seeing new output.
3. **Survival gate:** Any terminal extinction makes all three mean inferences not_evaluable. No redraw, survivor-only salvage, zero filling, or early stopping with partial mean inference. Record every pair's survival category, including both extinct. Runtime/corruption failures are technical failures; do not silently condition on successful execution. Exact-seed resumption is acceptable; new seeds replacing failures are not.
4. **RNG and state:** Freeze complete sufficient captured states, absolute calendar years, original kernel/configuration, original horizon, exact edit, and pair-stream derivation before any new outcomes. Guarantee unique independent replicate streams across all 61x64 pairs, never reuse original futures, and document common-RNG coupling after arms consume different numbers of random draws. Mutable RNG objects must not be shared sequentially between arms.
5. **Controls:** Full native unchanged futures remain necessary for the specified both-arm survival target, even though their occupied terminal I is deterministically v. A third biological sham arm is not statistically required. Before the production run, use deterministic fixtures to show native-versus-no-op continuation matches under identical initial state and RNG, and that edit handling consumes no unintended random numbers. Do not count technical fixtures as new scientific replicates.
6. **Operational margin:** delta=.01 is a stated operational convention. Being one fifth of the old event threshold does not establish biological negligible effect. The margin is chosen after the earlier results existed, even if new futures remain unseen. Describe the stage as prospectively frozen for fresh futures, not globally outcome-blind.
7. **Claims across blocks:** Concordant signs of point estimates alone do not establish sign replication. Define directional support by both block intervals being wholly on the same side of zero; use the delta thresholds separately for practically meaningful direction. All three intervals within [-delta,delta] supports only operational equivalence of the three averaged conditional contrasts, not equivalence of each state, survival, or other outcomes.

## Options

- Keep the proposed strict gate and narrow target. This is the smallest implementation consistent with the proposed math and preserves a possible not_evaluable result.
- If an unconditional target is scientifically required, preregister a separate endpoint defined under extinction or a two-part survival/occupied-trait analysis. That is a different design requiring explicit scientific choices; do not retrofit the present bound or impute undefined terminal trait means.

The 7,808 new trajectories and 990,720 maximum annual updates are consistent with 61 states, 64 pairs per state, and 7,740 total remaining updates. They bound trajectory/update counts, not wall-clock compute cost. Approval should receive a runtime/storage estimate and stop/recovery rules as well as these counts.
