# Publication strategy and novelty test for the single Model 3 Article

**Scientific/editorial decision, 2026-10-19 (PR #609).** This is a critique of the existing paper relative to prior literature and the original registered evidence, plus one **post-outcome, descriptive-only** arithmetic decomposition of the admitted 160×160 result. It is neither a new biological experiment, a new preregistration, a reclassification of original null results, an exhaustive global priority review nor a journal-submission or acceptance claim.

## Recommendation / 現時点の投稿戦略

**NOW:** Keep one **mechanistic theoretical/evolutionary-ecology Article**, but make the main question *whether reproductive buffering predicts preservation of the genetic capacity for a later floral response*, not “selfing causes a dead end” or “pollinator decline always makes flowers smaller.” Keep original Q1–Q4 as **non-equivalent estimands**, and place the completed one-time transfer as a conditional test of later responses, not as a demonstrated continuous restoration clock. The stronger, source-backed contrast is that the same A policy may affect **persistence and retention of target-compatible alleles differently across resources**. The registered G and S experiments independently support two policy endpoints; the exploratory all-start three-state view unifies their **presentation**, not their causal identification.

**Novelty status:** Plausible *model-specific new quantitative test*, not proven conceptual priority or a new general biological principle. Existing research has already studied selfing-mediated reproductive rescue, subsequent genomic variation loss, selfing–attraction coevolution, and mathematical irreversibility after pollinator loss. The paper must identify its **specific falsifiable counterfactual and independent genetic/demographic endpoints** and avoid universal “first” language.

**Recommended scientific title (source-bounded):**

> **Reproductive assurance differentially affects floral genetic accessibility and persistence after pollinator loss**

This is an editorial candidate, not a manuscript filename or an extra paper. Less compact alternative: *Population persistence and floral evolutionary access respond differently to reproductive assurance under pollinator limitation.* **Avoid** “Evolutionary rescue causes irreversible flower loss,” “A selfing-induced trap across islands,” “Restoration window closes before pollinators disappear” and other currently untested causal or natural-history assertions.

### New three-state all-start view of the registered 160×160 experiment

**Derived by [read-only script](../scripts/audit_chapter2_three_state_publication_20261019.py) from [complete original confirm160 compact audit](audits/CHAPTER2_CONFIRM160_COMPACT_RESULTS_20261019.json), not from another simulation.** All values are exactly count-based; denominator 25,600 per policy per budget. Define:
- **Alive + target accessible:** occupied at census80 and maximum surviving I allele is at least that founder's census0 mean I;
- **Alive but target closed:** occupied and maximum surviving I allele below that fixed target;
- **Extinct:** no living state and no observed I mean.

**Evolving−fixed, alive and accessible:** B8 **−8.328125 percentage points**; B4.5 **+35.56640625 percentage points**; between-budget difference of those two descriptive policy effects **+43.89453125 percentage points**. These are arithmetic differences of existing randomized-policy source counts, **not a new prespecified third primary endpoint, a tested interaction, biological equivalence bound, significance claim or new confidence interval**. Registered G/S retain their original definitions and uncertainty. Under B4.5, a policy can increase the number of *living accessible* groups primarily because fewer populations go extinct, even if most of its surviving populations no longer carry a target-compatible upper-I allele. Critically, **the B4.5 G-first event count cannot be equated to the number alive-and-closed at census80**, because some genetically closed populations later die. The corresponding three-way current-state totals and first-competing-event totals are different legitimate partitions.

**Interpretation:** This three-state framing makes the paper's thesis falsifiable at the correct all-start denominator. It also exposes why the phrase “assurance protects evolvability” is not warranted by a high B4.5 joint success count: genetic conservation within survivors has **not** been isolated, and conditioning on survivors would introduce post-treatment selection. The three-state “reversal” is a **post hoc scenario pattern**. Whether it survives independent parameter regimes, founder distributions and mutation-permitted response remains untested.

## One manuscript, five Results sections (not four autonomous papers)

1. **Changed returns do not impose one floral response:** original Q1 fixed-A experiment and 3/4 fresh-founder attenuation; append exploratory baseline-history sign difference without treating A fixed as zero selfing.
2. **Observed sequence is not evolutionary necessity:** 51/64 vs 30/64, independently randomized *expression schedule* practical equivalence under its specific ROPE, and registered Q2 carrier both-inconclusive. Do not put “order causes rescue” in abstract.
3. **Finite inheritance constrains realized responses:** original two density-versus-finite A-first exceptions, precise genotype-support loss, distinct LOW-I vs HIGH-I target directions, no drift-fraction claim.
4. **Reproductive insurance changes persistence and genetic opportunity differently:** foreground registered G at B8 and registered S across budgets; show the **exploratory** three-state stacked fate distribution and interpret competing extinction honestly; add read-only I duration data as a qualified retrospective companion, not confirmation.
5. **What remains for subsequent adaptation:** only the bounded late seed transfer (nested donor cohorts), reduced segregating I variation vs target allele support, survivor-conditioned performance; make explicit that full restored-pollination recovery curves, natural island causation and mutation-enabled irreversibility are not tested.

### An editor-level novelty paragraph (candidate, not original registered Results)

> Previous studies have demonstrated reproductive assurance under pollinator scarcity, floral changes with self-fertilisation and genomic consequences of mating-system transitions. Here we distinguish three outcomes that those broad predictions do not identify as a single trajectory: persistence, the presence of alleles compatible with a specified floral target, and its realized response. In a founder-by-pollinator crossed diploid model, permitting autonomous assurance evolution increased loss of high-investment allelic support in a regime where all populations survived, yet strongly altered population persistence under a resource-poor regime. Descriptive all-start three-state counts show that the probability of remaining both alive and genetically target-compatible varies oppositely with assurance policy across those regimes. Separate genetic, phenotypic-order and bounded-transfer experiments show why temporal precedence, selected allele availability and actual later response cannot be assumed equivalent. The resulting insight is a **conditional separation** of reproductive buffering and later floral evolutionary opportunity, not evidence for a universal irreversible selfing trap.

Avoid “for the first time” in this paragraph without substantially broader full-source priority and model-robustness tests.

### Evidence ladder to establish *stronger* novelty (not automatically authorized experiments)

**Level A — Already achievable without new biological runs:** exact registered G/S provenance; all four source states; current-state three-way partition; original and new nulls; source-specific methods, matched exposures and independent unit labels; a decisive comparison table versus Lepers/Cheptou/Busch/Acoca-Pidolle/Hartfield. This substantiates the **particular model finding**, not generality.

**Level B — For a stronger general theoretical paper:** prospective, independent, power- and budget-reviewed *same three-state endpoint* across a preselected modest resource grid, at least two reproductive timing/cost regimes and genuinely independent founder and pollinator-history axes. Declare biological-effect thresholds before outcomes. A genuine selection-on-return contrast and a source-consistent separate genetic rescue treatment are needed for causal claims. No favorable B interval, threshold or sample size should be selected post hoc.

**Level C — For “evolutionary irreversibility/recovery window” or NEE-level cross-system claim:** restored-pollination futures at several historical times, joint *unconditional* recovery probability including extinction, direct allele-reintroduction or mutation/immigration comparison, and independent model or real multi-island evidence. This is a *different study requiring scientific authorization*, not a mandatory embellishment to a truthful existing-data paper.
