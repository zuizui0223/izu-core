## What has actually been established

The already exposed technical Model3 six artificial source genotypes and two artificial resource/visitor environments have exact one-update establishment propensities `p_{ij} = P(arrive) × [1 − exp(−Λ_{ij})]`, with one Bernoulli arrival lottery followed by Poisson successful recruitment. A read-only [source-propensity audit](../scripts/audit_two_origins_predictability_20261019.py) now evaluates the **population expected** Brier score of an *oracle that already knows those true per-source probabilities*, not a predictor fitted and evaluated on unseen source lineages.

For equally weighted source propensities `p_i` under a fixed island environment,

`Brier_oracle = mean_i p_i(1 − p_i)`,
`Brier_constant = p_bar(1 − p_bar)`,
`Brier_constant − Brier_oracle = Var_i(p_i)`.

Therefore the share of binary-fate variance a *perfect* source-informed predictor can explain is `Var(p_i)/[p_bar(1−p_bar)]`, conditional on a chosen source distribution, island environment and arrival mechanism. This is a known Bernoulli law-of-total-variance identity, **not a new ecological theory, observed CV skill, or a claim that 6 artificial lineages represent natural islands**.

Using **only the 4-decimal probabilities printed in original technical Actions** and arrival chance .8, the illustrative perfect source-model reduction in expected Brier error is about **4.24%** of constant-predictor error at no-visitors/B4.5, versus about **0.57%** at visitors/B8. Do not report these as held-out prediction improvements or broad-scale trait-selection effects; refer to the reader's exact current result and SHA before final decimals are admitted.

## Target question and endpoints for a NEW independent source × island experiment

**Core question:** For a previously unseen mainland source lineage and an unseen island ecological context, are its **probabilities** of arrival, founding establishment, later persistence and post-establishment I/A response identifiable from baseline source A/I genotype? Does reproductive function versus floral investment have a *different relative causal contribution* to current floristic composition and different prediction precision?

**All-start lineage event vector:** Each member of a candidate source lineage×island×reset frame remains in the denominator, categorized as `not_arrived`, `arrived_not_established`, `established_then_extinct`, `surviving_no_directional_response`, `surviving_I_increase`, `surviving_I_decrease` with response thresholds, censoring/undefined traits and any A changes fixed **before new run**. End-of-horizon descendant mean changes are undefined for extinct lineages. No artificial zero phenotype is imputed for extinction. This outcome inventory is illustrative; the precise registration and sample-size/power contract have **not** yet been authorized.

**Mechanistic endpoints:** (i) `P(establish | arrived)`; (ii) `P(establish | candidate)` with source-independent or known arrival probability; (iii) `P(persist | candidate)`; (iv) calibrated held-out distribution of I/A response with the extinction category retained; (v) species-weighted source→arrived→established→persisted-source→persisted-final means for A and I. Individual stochastic outcomes and repeatable group averages are scored separately.

**Main counterfactuals:** Matched candidate genomic source, visitor environment, initial arrival and establishment RNG streams; vary **A expression**, **I expression**, both and a trait-neutral initial reproductive ledger with true alleles transmitted unaltered, followed by native/no-A-change/no-I-change post-establishment expression policies. The intervention can modify which descendants later survive; a descriptive four-stage telescoping identity is **not uniquely identifiable mediation** of sorting and evolution. To identify historical genetic mediation will require separate interventions, including allele support and immigrant genotypes.

- Reserve a **NEW SHA-identified source-genotype namespace** disjoint from all six exposed source seeds 73000–73005, independent-genotype pilots and registered #609 founder/visitor RNGs. Do not reutilize the current technical source list for held-out confirmation.

Pre-specify resource budgets/visitor functionality and hold out complete island classes and entire source genotype groups, never only stochastic repeats from the same founder.
- Reserve all-start failure states, paired policies and every stochastic replay. Within-lineage demographic repeats are nested; treat independent **source genotype and island environment** as crossed axes in uncertainty, with source×environment and within-cell noise treated honestly. The required design/power budget and quantitative SESOI are still unknown; **do not invent independent n or claim 80% power**.
- Evaluate an arrival-opportunity/ecological **baseline** against A-only, I-only, A+I, and full-source-M/A/I models. Report source-group-and-island-blocked held-out Brier/log-loss/calibration. Compare A-vs-I information on the **same binary outcome** and standardized prediction difficulty; do not compare raw within-trait terminal variance across different scales as a determinism verdict.
- Include **negative controls**: source-independent arrival; expression-neutralized establishment; no-evolution-after-establishment; and ideally interspecific competition / shared-pollinator interference with separately declared mechanisms. The present noninteracting-lineage pool remains a clearly labelled null, not a community model.
- Prespecify genuine falsifiers: A state adds no out-of-source held-out establishment information after arrival; I has equally strong or stronger establishment effect; A evolves significantly after establishment; I enters founding selection through allocation; floral direction is highly reproducible when visitor trajectories are controlled; predicted source filtering fails to reconstruct the observed Ch1 trend. Any one could weaken the exclusive two-origin narrative.

## Ch1/empirical boundary

Corrected [Ch1 evidence](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/blob/main/submission/chapter1_current/MANUSCRIPT.md) supports recurrent **self-compatibility** association with island isolation, but species-level self-compatibility labels are **not measured within-species island-versus-mainland variation** and are not the continuous autonomous insurance A of Model3. Natural sorting and evolutionary contributions cannot be partitioned from Ch1 records alone. Same-species studies already report greater island selfing (Schueller 2004) and rapid post-establishment increases in selfing with floral change (Acoca-Pidolle et al. 2024). Include both in falsification rather than dismissing their counterexamples.
