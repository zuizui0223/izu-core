## Study type and simulated units

The study consists of **mechanistic computer simulations and read-only analyses of archived synthetic diploid plant populations**, not field samples from natural islands, participants, sampled animal colonies, or repeated populations from geographic island sites. Model 3 specifies independent, inherited floral investment **I**, visitor matching **M** and autonomous reproductive assurance **A** components; these are model traits, not measured floral colour/flower size or observed self-compatibility. Reproductive bookkeeping combines maternally received outcross fertilization, paternally exported pollen, viable selfing under the rule and stated inbreeding depression, and ovule-allocation costs. Finite population recruitment and Mendelian offspring inheritance create variability. Functional pollinator visitor communities are **externally imposed**, not coevolving with plants. See [canonical reproduction](../scripts/model3_island/reproduction.py), [offspring transitions](../scripts/model3_island/population.py), and [model configuration](../scripts/model3_island/types.py). Do not assume a shared parameter regime across historically distinct experiments.

For main R3/R4/R5 source models, a synthetic reproductive time step is treated as one generation/update. The latest 160×160 R4 model uses plant carrying capacity K=48, ovule resource budget B=8 or B=4.5, no adult survival, mutation or seed immigration, delayed autonomous selfing, depression=0.5 and assured expression fixed or evolving from the *same initial genotype* with expressed A=0.5. The fixed-A policy **does not eliminate self-fertilization or suppress transmission of latent A alleles**. In its historical visitor schedule, six visitors are present before update 5, one through update 34, and none from update 35; visitor identities have varying matching optima. Thus nominally shared count history does **not** represent natural mainland versus island pollination. [Pinned 160×160 runner](../scripts/q3_native_runner_20261019.py), [initial-genotype balancing](../scripts/chapter2_q3_balanced_assurance_founders_20261018.py), [execution authorisation](protocols/CHAPTER2_CONFIRM160_EXECUTION_20261019.json).

## Computational reproducibility, randomization and exclusions

1. **Random-number streams and pairing.** Synthetic founders, visitors and demographic offspring streams are recorded in their original source contracts. The fresh-Q1 genotype/visitor factorial used common random numbers across its paired four treatment cells and a two-axis bootstrap; Q3 uses deterministic pinned SHA-bound RNG seed namespaces. Reusing an original visitor seed across a crossed matrix is not a new independent ecological history per cell. The present editorial readbacks add no pseudorandom population draws.
2. **Predefinition and decision rules.** The original Q1 and Q2 registered outcomes, fresh-founder Q1 four-setting conjunctive rule, and R4 G/S were frozen before their respective result exposure; retrospective R1 confirmation wording, R2 baseline-pollen, R3 known-ID predictions, R4 joint states and R5 destinations retain their declared **exploratory or conditional** evidence ranks. The post-outcome 10 percentage-point G reference is **not** an endorsed biological equivalence threshold. R4's five preselected genetic-target tolerances are sensitivity descriptions, not new co-primary tests.
3. **Exclusions / conditioning.** Fresh-Q1: all 1,088 expected cases and 32 exact zero-mutation matched fixed/evolving trace pairs were admitted; all 1,024 mutation-active main paths survived. R2 factorial: all 384 paths survived. R3 matched repeatability: compute traits only when all four paired near/far repeats occupied, and preserve all-start arm occupancy separately. R4: all 25,600 source pairs retained; B8 all occupied; B4.5 extinctions remain in original denominators and genetic closure is considered as a first event competing with extinction. R5: every original cohort/destination replicate belongs in the all-start denominator; extinction counts remain visible and post-extinction traits undefined. No researcher-chosen favourable source replacements.
4. **Statistics and precision.** Distinct source cohorts are not pooled; evidence ranks, estimands and horizon are not retrofitted into one cross-experiment p-value. Original raw archives and hashes are read by separately published audit scripts; read-only Python test success verifies internal source-value consistency, not independent ecological model validity. The existing source readback records tolerated post-execution floating-point differences without modifying predeclared outcomes or replacing original stored bytes.
5. **Blinding and ethics status.** This is synthetic modelling; no natural-field plants, pollinators, human participants or wildlife experiments were enrolled for the results presented here. Standard field-treatment randomization, observer blinding or respondent sex/gender strata do not apply to the simulated cohorts. Author/journal confirmation of ethics, computation allocation, interests and full author contributions is **not supplied** by these source files; do not invent declarations.
