# Current mechanistic leverage

## Bottom line

The current Izu programme supports **heterogeneous response modes**, not one universal island-flower syndrome.

Evidence is kept at three levels:

1. **response shape established** — what changes in a defined channel;
2. **mechanistic leverage** — whether explicit alternatives or source-native functional data constrain interpretation;
3. **causal attribution** — still blocked. `data/predictive_meta/current_mechanistic_leverage.csv` keeps `causal_claim_allowed = no` for every current evidence unit.

The strongest current breakpoint remains the focal *Campanula microdonta* autonomous-reproduction transition. Independent source-native plant/network data do **not** show a universal Oshima-to-post response across morphology, interaction breadth or reproductive function.

## Campanula: continuous channels and breakpoint channel must remain separate

### Flower size

The historical focal literature supports island-series floral-size erosion, but source-native population-genetic evidence also supports ordered colonisation history. The historical size cline is therefore a strong descriptive pattern but a weak mechanism discriminator.

A completely independent contemporary field dataset now supplies site-level corolla-tube means measured from five flowers per plant species at each site. In those data, *Campanula microdonta* has Oshima mean `26.9825 mm` and mean across Niijima, Kozu, Miyake and Hachijo `19.4534 mm` (`-27.9%`). This independently reproduces the lower-post morphology direction.

However, the contemporary series lacks Toshima and the archived site table lacks within-site SD/SE. It therefore cannot distinguish a smooth cline from an Oshima/post step or enter the A-grade inverse-variance holdout.

### Multilocus outcrossing

Outcrossing declines strongly along the historical island series. Tested climate PC1, mainland distance, island area/connectivity and pre-1986 volcanic-recency axes do not reproduce the ordered erosion as well as island order.

But island order is not a causal exposure. The allozyme literature supports progressive southward colonisation, while the RAPD literature does not support one universal diversity-by-distance axis and identifies Miyake as a multiple-immigration exception. The continuous mating-system pattern is established without assigning it uniquely to pollinator turnover.

### Autonomous reproductive capacity

This remains the strongest current breakpoint channel. The Oshima-to-Toshima transition is much sharper than the continuous morphology/outcrossing patterns and is not reproduced by tested climate, mainland distance, static geography or pre-observation volcanic-history alternatives.

That gives this channel more mechanistic leverage than the two continuous clines, but it still does not prove historical Bombus causation: an unmeasured breeding-system, founder or demographic threshold could coincide with the same boundary.

### Contemporary Campanula network function does not copy the historical breeding step

In the 2024 source-native network data, *Campanula microdonta* shows **higher**, not lower, post-Oshima values for both realized functional generality (`+0.508`) and corrected trait matching (`+0.571`) relative to Oshima.

Thus the historical autonomous-reproduction step is not a synchronous all-channel ecological step. Morphology, mating system, autonomous capacity and contemporary network function must remain distinct response domains.

## Independent directional and dependency gates

### No independent directional source currently demonstrates the shared second step

The B-grade source-native directional layer includes Weigela, Ligustrum and Hosta. None of their currently accessible original-source evidence explicitly localizes and demonstrates the predeclared Oshima-to-Toshima shared second step.

- *Weigela coraeensis*: gradual mainland-distance corolla decline; exact island table unavailable; effective Bombus dependency unresolved.
- *Ligustrum ovalifolium*: source-labelled generalist; Izu shortening with Hachijo strongest; exact second boundary not localized.
- *Hosta longipes*: southern-Izu corolla shortening plus complex variation in other floral parts; dependency and exact localities unresolved.

This is **absence of independent directional support for a universal breakpoint**, not statistical falsification of a conditional pollinator-regime hypothesis.

### Clean Bombus-dependent within-lineage holdout remains blocked

Source-native dependency classification is independent of floral form.

- *Ligustrum* is source-labelled generalist.
- Weigela and Hosta remain dependency-unresolved.
- mainland *Goodyera henryi* is directly Bombus-dependent, but the Kozu comparator is hybrid rather than pure *G. henryi*.
- *G. similis* is a direct non-Bombus scoliid-wasp dependency control.
- island *Lilium auratum* var. *platyphyllum* supplies a Lepidoptera-specialization alternative mechanism, but variety identity is confounded with geography.

Therefore the independent Bombus-dependent same-lineage quantitative holdout is still closed for a biological reason, not because missing numbers were imputed away.

## Direct contemporary network evidence: universal response is falsified, subset signal is real but bounded

The reproducibly acquired Hiraiwa–Ushimaru 2024 Figshare data span three Honshu sites, Oshima, Niijima, Kozu, Miyake and Hachijo across five seasons.

Seasonal rows are aggregated within `plant × site` before Oshima-versus-post contrasts.

### All network plants

With Oshima plus at least two post-Oshima sites:

| response | eligible species | lower post | higher post |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| functional generality | 14 | 6 | 8 |
| corrected trait matching | 16 | 10 | 6 |

There is no universal species-level second step in contemporary network function.

### Source-defined pollen-success target plants

The original study independently selected 10 dominant outcrossing insect-pollinated plants for pollen-receipt measurements. Among eligible members:

| response | eligible species | lower post | higher post |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| functional generality | 8 | 3 | 5 |
| corrected trait matching | 8 | 8 | 0 |
| pollen receipt | 9 | 5 | 4 |

Corrected trait matching is therefore directionally coherent in this source-defined functional subset, while interaction breadth and pollen receipt are not.

The trait-matching pattern is not a single-island artifact but is not perfectly leave-one-island invariant: it remains 7/7 after omitting Niijima or Kozu, becomes 6/8 after omitting Miyake, and 7/8 after omitting Hachijo.

This is a functional boundary signal in a source-defined subgroup, not a universal plant response or an independent-species sign test.

## Direct site-level morphology also rejects one common response direction

The same archived dataset stores species × site mean corolla-tube lengths based on five measured flowers per species per site. Within-site SD/SE are not archived, so these are B+ numeric directional data rather than A-grade effect sizes.

Among eight eligible source-defined pollen-success target species, Oshima-to-post tube-length direction is:

- shorter post: 3 species;
- longer post: 4 species;
- equal: 1 species.

Thus direct floral morphology also does not show a universal second-boundary direction.

### Farfugium: high interaction breadth does not mean phenotypic or functional stasis

*Farfugium japonicum* was prospectively selected from the source-defined 10-species set using only realized functional generality and coverage. It ranks first among coverage-eligible species (`mean FG_Pla_sp_z = 1.675`, eight sites). Response variables were not used for selection.

Its current channels are deliberately non-synchronous:

- functional generality: essentially unchanged Oshima-to-post (`+0.00265`);
- corrected trait matching: lower post (`-2.80665`);
- pollen receipt: lower post (`-0.88773`);
- site-mean corolla tube: `11.276 mm` on Oshima versus `10.339 mm` post mean (`-8.3%`);
- visible head-display photo score: currently all eligible observations score 3, but Oshima and post each have only one independent observation.

The targeted Oshima iNaturalist recovery increased the usable Oshima photo cell from `n=0` to `n=1` after blind stage-0/score locking. A wider research-grade search found no second independent flowering observation. The GBIF preserved-specimen audit retrieved 33 Japanese *Farfugium* image records but zero candidates in the fixed regional proxy cells.

Therefore the visible-signal control is **descriptively flat but inferentially inconclusive**. No equivalence/no-change claim is allowed.

## Direct reproductive sensitivity is itself heterogeneous

The 2017 Hiraiwa–Ushimaru natural experiment supplies reproductive responses to loss of long-tongued pollinator biomass. Oshima fruit-set data were unavailable after a landslide, so these results cannot localize the focal Oshima/post breakpoint, but they strongly constrain any universal reproductive story.

- long-tubed self-incompatible *Calystegia soldanella*: fruit set decreases as long-tongued-pollinator biomass and visitor proboscis matching decrease;
- self-incompatible *Vitex rotundifolia*: fruit set shows no significant relationship with those exposures;
- *Lysimachia mauritiana*: fruit set changes in the counterdirection with long-tongued-pollinator biomass.

Thus even within one source, one exposure produces sensitivity, resilience and counterdirectional reproductive modes.

## Other independent response modes

### Goodyera

Mainland pure *G. henryi* is Bombus-dependent. On Kozu, pure *G. henryi* was not recovered; plants initially identified as that taxon are *G. henryi × G. similis* hybrids and use the scoliid-wasp interaction also seen in *G. similis*. The correct response mode is hybrid replacement plus interaction rewiring, not same-lineage floral adaptation.

### Lilium

The source documents an alternative Lepidoptera timing mechanism and direct day/night reproductive-effectiveness evidence. Variety identity is confounded with geography, so it remains an alternative-mechanism boundary rather than a staged-Bombus replicate.

## What the paper can now argue

The defensible claim is not:

> Bombus loss causes every southern Izu plant to shrink, mismatch and self.

It is:

> **Plant responses to altered pollination environments in the Izu Islands are channel- and lineage-specific.** In focal *Campanula*, continuous morphology/outcrossing erosion is separated from a sharp autonomous-reproduction transition. Explicit static geography and pre-observation volcanic-history alternatives do not reproduce that multichannel pattern, but continuous island-order responses remain confounded with colonisation history. Independent contemporary network, morphology and reproductive-function data reject one universal response direction and instead reveal functional mismatch in a source-defined subset, reproductive sensitivity/resilience/counterresponses, and alternative modes such as hybrid-mediated interaction rewiring.

The pollinator hypothesis is therefore a moderator hypothesis about **dependency class × response mode × boundary**, not a universal island syndrome.

## Decisive next evidence

1. obtain a clean source-resolved Bombus-dependent lineage with pure mainland and island comparators;
2. recover population-level uncertainty for independent morphology (Weigela, Ligustrum, Hosta or equivalent) to open A-grade effect-size synthesis;
3. add quantitative equivalence/ROPE controls only where independent sample hierarchy and uncertainty are adequate;
4. match population-history covariates to the same trait populations rather than using island order as history;
5. test dependency-class moderation in a hierarchical site-aware model using the source-native contemporary network data without treating species sharing sites as independent island replicates.

Until those gates are met, causal attribution remains closed.
