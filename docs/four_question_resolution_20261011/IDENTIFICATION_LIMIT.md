# What an order effect can mean in current Model3

## Source-supported identification limit

Inspected source: main44b90bc scripts/model3_island/reproduction.py and population.py. Reproduction reads current alleles and visitor/configuration inputs. advance reads the current ledger, census/genomes, immigrant candidates, configuration, keyed streams and update year. Past event-order labels are not inputs. Origins, flags, IDs and birth years are retained for inheritance/accounting and validity; they are not a separate historical-order fitness bonus.

Let X_t include the complete biological and environmental state needed by these operators, and let K_t specify the common future transition law. Conditional on the same X_T and future law, the distribution of later occupancy does not depend additionally on whether assurance or investment crossed a threshold first. With the same random-stream states, equality of complete inputs also gives the same realized trajectory. This is a property of the existing Markov model, not a general claim about organisms or evidence against an adaptive pathway.

Therefore a meaningful path effect in this model must operate through (i) survival before the common horizon, (ii) different full genetic/census states reached, or (iii) a changed future environment/configuration. Matching only mean assurance and investment does not match genotype variance, covariances, allelic support, census or environmental state. Imposing identical full endpoints would remove the very state-mediated effect that a pathway can create.

## What the new experiment identifies

Randomized mutation-access preparation followed by a common challenge identifies an all-start preparation-policy effect. It deliberately retains the end states produced by each preparation. The positive registered contrasts support a future persistence effect of that policy under the selected challenges. They do not identify an abstract order-label effect or mediation solely through both-event sequence. Most preparations do not achieve both events; preparation-end assurance differs.

Neither selecting only both-event histories nor conditioning on surviving assurance-first histories resolves this issue: these are post-intervention subsets and change the randomized comparison. A natural-order causal contrast requires a defined intervention and a complete model of its state-mediated effects, rather than additional regression on observed order labels.

## Remaining scope

The current campaign establishes an informative policy contrast and an explicit identification limit. It does not establish that naturally assurance-first evolution is universally advantageous. Selection-onset explanation, individual route-specific survival mediation, and all-three-mutating-locus PDE remain separate unresolved questions. No new simulation or altered frozen decision rule was used for this source-level argument.
