## Decision

The current source-owning evidence does **not** supply a clean Level-3 or Level-4 external prediction test of the Chapter 2 conditional-response geometry. No entry combines an outcome-independent source/mainland plant state, a directionally identified partner perturbation, community functional change, local filtering, reproductive assurance and a response that was withheld until after the mapping and target were frozen.

Four entries contain prior **prospective-like tests of narrower model layers**: Dominica (a publication-aware signed-position projection retained as a directional falsification), Menorca, Cabrera and Giannutri (network/local-realization tests, all already consumed). None meets the full Chapter 2 plant-response contract. In the current admission ledger, Dominica is therefore retrospective explanatory evidence and the three network targets are reality boundaries; their earlier pre-target chronology is retained separately. Five additional targets remain source- or design-gated for the requested challenge.

Accordingly, this audit supports retaining Chapter 2 at **mechanistic HOW plus model-conditional proximal WHY**. It does not justify calling the external layer formal prediction, estimating natural regime frequencies, or inferring ultimate historical causes.

## Audit rules

Each evidence cell uses one of four explicit states:

- **DM — `direct_measurement`:** the primary article or source-owning deposit measures the construct at the stated study unit.
- **SP — `source_derived_proxy`:** the primary source supports a contrast or proxy, but not the named construct itself. Cross-sectional depletion, inferred historical replacement and undirected turnover remain proxies.
- **UA — `unavailable`:** the checked primary/source-owning material does not supply the construct in a compatible form.
- **NA — `not_applicable`:** the construct is outside the source's declared design. This code is used only when absence is structural, not simply missing evidence.

A dagger on `UA` (`UA†`) marks the source-gated subset: compatible evidence may exist, but raw bytes, source-native numeric rows or a valid row linkage are unavailable or failed a frozen admission gate. Thus `UA†` is still the requested `unavailable` table state; the dagger preserves why it is unavailable.

“Partner loss” and “partner arrival/replacement” are directional transition variables. Lower richness in a cross-sectional comparison, or high turnover between different places or years, is not relabelled as observed loss or arrival. “Reproductive assurance” means a controlled autonomous-pollination/dependency quantity; self-compatibility, breeding category or an open fruit-set outcome alone is not treated as full assurance. A plant trait measured only on the focal island is not a source/mainland state unless an outcome-independent source comparator exists.

The classification terms have deliberately narrow meanings:

- **admissible prospective-like challenge:** a repository mapping and exact Chapter 2 plant-response target were frozen before those target values were calculated, the required predictor vector is available, and any prior publication awareness is disclosed;
- **retrospective explanatory test only:** predictors and outcomes come from an already-read paper or were assembled after the outcome pattern was known;
- **reality-boundary only:** the source constrains a narrower empirical layer but lacks the linkage needed to challenge response geometry;
- **source-gated/unusable:** source admission or compatible design is not currently open.

## Geographic and publication overlap

The 25 entries are analytical targets, not independent archipelagos. At minimum, the following clusters must remain nested or explicitly de-duplicated:

- **Repeated publications within one focal system:** the Izu 2017/2024 papers, the two Hawaiʻi lobelioid papers, the two *Nicotiana* papers and the three Caribbean Gesneriaceae papers contain linked systems, sites, taxa or data layers. Publication count is not replication count, and the linked rows are not assumed to be the same tagged individuals unless the primary source establishes that join.
- **Ogasawara:** strict *Psychotria* and Quitián/Tian 2026 are two datasets in the same archipelago.
- **Seychelles/Mahé:** the strict invasive-ant study and the Mahé restoration-network target both use Mahé restoration/inselberg settings. They are not two independent archipelagos and may share site/program context.
- **Balearic Islands:** Menorca and Cabrera are different islands and datasets within one archipelago/region. Cabrera's repository contract already states that a pass cannot establish broad geographic generality beyond Menorca.
- **Caribbean/Antillean studies:** Dominica *Heliconia*, the Caribbean Gesneriaceae synthesis and Puerto Rico–Mona are distinct biological datasets but share a Caribbean macroregion; the Gesneriaceae evidence itself pools related clades and studies. They must not be presented as independent draws from a global archipelago population without a frozen geographic hierarchy and site/publication de-duplication.
- **New Zealand/Southwest Pacific morphology:** Rhabdothamnus, Ciarle and Hendriks overlap the broader New Zealand/Southwest Pacific source geography. Ciarle and Hendriks share at least Antipodes, Auckland, Campbell, Chatham, Kermadec, Lord Howe, Norfolk and Three Kings island groups; Ciarle additionally includes Macquarie/Snares and Hendriks includes Stewart in their reported group frames. Ciarle's 129 colonization events and Hendriks' 35 flower-area pairs are nested within ten archipelagos and nine populated island groups respectively, not 164 independent systems.
- **Pacific sister-taxon synthesis:** Hetherington–Rauth–Johnson contains 136 contrasts across seven Pacific island systems and includes Galápagos/other Pacific strata represented elsewhere. It is one multi-system study with nested contrasts, not 136 independent systems and not automatically independent of the Galápagos or Hawaiian entries.
- **Within-study multiplicity:** 40 Izu networks, 10 Galápagos islands, 64 Mahé site-month networks, 29 Giannutri daily networks, 42 Thousand Island Lake spatial networks, and repeated sites/campaigns in Menorca/Cabrera remain nested observations. Thousand Island Lake is a fragmented reservoir landscape, not an oceanic archipelago.

Because exact cross-study site membership is not recovered for every synthesis, a defensible independent-archipelago denominator cannot be computed from the current registry. The safe report is **13 strict challenge systems plus at least 12 additional named analytical targets before geographic de-duplication**.

## Can an outcome-independent Izu mechanistic-anchor score be justified?

**No formal scalar score or ranking can be justified retrospectively from the current repository.** A descriptive statement that Izu is the programme's deepest prepared anchor is source-supported, but it is not equivalent to an outcome-independent selection result.

The blockers are structural:

1. The eligibility universe, scoring criteria, weights, missing-data penalties and tie rules were not frozen before Izu was designated.
2. Several tempting criteria are outcome-adjacent or outcome-dependent: reproductive response availability, “historical phenotype”, Chapter 3 phenotype and the existence of an ongoing Izu field programme. Using them would reward the focal programme for evidence accumulated after selection.
3. Izu does not monotonically dominate the alternatives. Teide/Mahé connect networks to reproductive outcomes; Nicotiana connects per-visit pollen function and assurance; Dominica has a source-native selection surface; Ogasawara has a physical access mechanism. Different defensible weights change the winner.
4. Izu's strongest current signed-position result is raw realized geometry. Exact island-center assignment is non-unique, starting state alone fits at least as well, and the source-paper null-corrected non-random matching target is unsupported (`slope = 0.0333`, two-sided `p = 0.823`). A score cannot convert that boundary into an identified filter mechanism.
5. Izu still lacks the matched prospective chain required by its own gate: plant-specific visitor weights and traits → single-visit pollen deposition → direct reproductive dependency/mature seed outcome.

A future anchor-selection exercise could be made outcome-independent only by freezing, before outcome inspection, an eligibility frame, non-outcome data-availability criteria, missingness rules, geographic independence rules and a tie procedure. It should exclude response sign, model fit and Chapter 3 phenotype and preferably report a Pareto set rather than a tunable scalar. This audit does not construct such a score and does not select weights that make Izu win.

## Claim ceiling and next valid gate

The source audit permits the following statement:

> Across a heterogeneous, geographically overlapping evidence universe, published island systems show partner loss, replacement, disruption, network reorganization, buffering and divergent reproductive consequences. These sources establish reality bounds and several retained layer-specific falsifications, but they do not yet provide a fully outcome-independent external test of the Chapter 2 conditional-response geometry.

It does not permit:

- treating 13 or 25 entries as independent archipelagos;
- calling the retrospective source matrix formal external prediction;
- using repeated islands, sites, species or network layers as independent archipelago replication;
- mapping observed regional vectors to synthetic joint-surface regimes after seeing outcomes;
- treating simulation design frequencies as ecological prevalence;
- retuning any frozen model, threshold, mapping or inclusion rule to recover a preferred result;
- inferring historical Bombus loss, universal pollinator selection, or other ultimate-WHY causes from these sources;
- using Chapter 3 phenotype as outcome-independent support for Chapter 2 anchor selection.

The next valid Level-3 gate is a **new, frozen-before-outcome** system (or prospective Izu plant-level programme) with source functional state, directionally identified community perturbation, partner-weighted local filtering, independent assurance and a withheld reproductive response. Until that gate is passed, external evidence should be presented as audited retrospective projection, reality boundary and retained falsification—not as a successful formal prediction layer.
