# Chapter 2 scientific gate run

Updated: 2026-08-27

This run executes the scientific reassessment gate opened after the manuscript critique. It is not a seed search and does not calibrate to empirical island outcomes.

## Fixed run

- Matched response-geometry stability: 96 pollinator-community realizations across the full 0–1 plant starting-trait grid.
- Joint transition surface: 48 fixed-seed Latin-hypercube points across the 10 declared perturbation/matching parameters, with 24 matched realizations per point.
- Local-context and assurance thresholds: 12 replicates per saturation, 4 contexts, 24 lineages and 120 steps; local filtering stress spans 0–0.75 and assurance spans 0×–4×.
- Integrated decision: route the Chapter 2 product to a Research Article candidate, a realization-contingent Research Article, or a conceptual Review/Mini-review according to the declared gate.

## Failure honesty

The design-space frequencies are robustness descriptors, not natural ecological prevalence. Thresholds are synthetic model sensitivities, not empirical ecological thresholds. If mixed response geometry is absent or confined to a narrow parameter corner, the model is not retuned toward branching; the three-layer island-syndrome decomposition becomes the stronger product.
