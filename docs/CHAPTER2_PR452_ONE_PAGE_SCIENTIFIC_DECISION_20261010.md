### 2. The NEW direct externality check strengthens the public-good mechanism

Rather than inferring facilitation from β<0 and Γ_seed>0, we changed **only one focal plant's investment**, and read the native Model3 source reproductive ledger for **the other mothers' viable seeds**.

**All 192 original investment source states were checked:** **128 positive nonfocal seed externalities with visitors**, **64 precisely zero with no visitors**, **0 negative**, **0 inconclusive**. Crucially, the **14/14 original investment β-negative / Γ_seed-positive cells all have positive nonfocal externalities**. Full source-run: `scripts/audit_chapter2_investment_nonneighbor_externality.py`; original raw [artifact #11658777024](https://github.com/zuizui0223/izu-core/actions/runs/38021533525/artifacts/11658777024); compact receipt `data/results/chapter2_investment_nonneighbor_externality_receipt_20261010.json`.

One canonical finite K8/B48, delayed-selfing, fixed four-visitor condition (matching=.2, investment=.35, assurance=.35) has:

| Effect of increasing investment of ONE parent | Expected viable seed derivative |
|---|---:|
| Outcross viable seeds of other mothers sired by focal parent | **+0.470555** |
| Outcross viable seeds of other mothers sired by other parents | −0.027163 |
| Selfed viable seeds of other mothers | −0.077594 |
| **Net reproduction of all OTHER mothers** | **+0.365799** |
| Focal mother's own viable seeds | **−0.128587** |
| Whole-group viable seed effect of the unilateral shift | **+0.237211** |

In the **same condition** the *different-unit* original focal genetic log gradient β=−0.063174 and collective-trait log group seed Γ=+0.158229; do NOT subtract these log derivatives from the absolute viable-seed derivative table.

**Conservative mechanism statement:** Within the fixed-visitor Model3 source, floral investment creates a **positive reproductive externality to conspecific nonfocal mothers through pollen delivery**, while the investing mother's own viable seeds decline in the example. A public-good analogy is justified for **sharing pollen-mediated reproductive benefits**, not for visitor recruitment, interspecific magnet effects, natural group selection, or a demonstrated tragedy-of-commons equilibrium. Pollinator counts do **not** respond to investment in this fixture.

**Novelty boundary:** Conspecific floral facilitation by attractive displays and plant-pollinator magnet effects were studied before, e.g. Toräng & Ågren (2006; DOI 10.1890/0012-9658(2006)87[2113:FIAIHW]2.0.CO;2), Toräng, Ehrlén & Ågren (2008; DOI 10.1890/07-1283.1), Ghazoul (2006; DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2745.2006.01098.x). The unresolved novel target is **quantitatively predicting where individual genetic fitness conflicts with net indirect reproduction, and whether evolved investment affects unconditional persistence**.

## 4. The failed Hoeffding significance gate is not a no-effect finding

The **precommitted** condition for the fresh 192-history source was: both a 168-history clustered bootstrap interval **and** a distribution-free Hoeffding interval must have lower bound above the practical **+0.05** effect threshold. Observed K8 assurance-only mean difference was **0.193452**, but Hoeffding worst-case half-width was **0.209560** at eligible n=168. Its lower bound was **−0.016107**, automatically failing.

With effect fixed at the observed 0.193452, satisfying only the algebraic bound condition requires:

```text
n > 2*ln(2/0.05) / (0.193452 - 0.05)^2
n_min = 359 independent ELIGIBLE source histories
```

Since the fresh source t20 donor eligibility proportion was 168/192=87.5%, **~411 original source histories** would be needed merely to EXPECT ~359 eligible *if that eligibility fraction remained valid*. This is **NOT** a design with 80% statistical power: it ignores possible outcome variability, eligibility uncertainty and both-sided sampling risk. Do NOT relabel the completed primary result as "significant" post hoc. Report both original intervals and the **precommitted INCONCLUSIVE** verdict.

**Mandatory future design gate:** state the estimand, smallest ecologically meaningful effect, inference unit, bound/CI type, minimum admissible eligible histories, prospective power (e.g. simulation under realistic history-cluster variance and null), conditioning/exclusion rule, number of independent biological model systems and stop rule **before** generating future outcomes. The no-after-outcome-sample-increase stop remains enforced.

## 5. One candidate next manuscript and its falsifiers

**Working title:** *Individual floral-investment selection and pollen-mediated reproductive externalities: when does reproductive conflict reduce finite population persistence?*

Hypotheses that must be genuinely falsifiable:
- **H1 (already source-supported, not externally confirmed):** a unilateral investment increase can yield higher viable seed production by OTHER conspecific mothers even if the focal genetic selection gradient favors reducing investment.
- **H2 (NOT supported yet):** when standing genetic variation allows investment to evolve downward in a declared pollen-limited environment, unconditional long-run occupancy is lower than under a properly matched controlled investment-expression intervention. A negative or zero occupancy effect, invalid genotype freeze or saturated ecological floor/ceiling refutes/blocks this claim.
- **H3 (ecological transport):** the H1 externality depends on visitor availability, pollen-source contribution and within/between-plant reproductive coupling; true visitor recruitment and interspecific facilitation require a NEW explicit biology and ideally measured plant populations, not relabeling Model3's static visitor fixture.

**Future experiment must keep a clean discovery/exploration versus confirmatory split**. The existing explored budget4.5–6 and visitor/artificial histories are a pilot only; source-locked independent experimental histories (or external empirical populations), a pre-calculated adequate power, founder state/genome variation, pollen denominator B, recipient K, reproductive viability, exact native vs control genomic/expressed trait operators, complete F/P/S and nonfocal maternal reproductive ledger, and unconditional occupancy endpoint must all be fixed. A reproduction/trait-expression freeze still permits diploid drift, so any "evolutionary suicide" claim needs a clearly defined and biologically defendable counterfactual, not simple allele redraw.

**Publication scope:** keep #411's independently confirmed floral-investment result and #451's separately registered capacity companion intact. Keep #452 Draft as exploratory mechanistic program until original hypotheses, controls and external evidence reach a defined publication gate.
