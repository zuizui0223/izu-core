## R4 — effective-community representation CLOSED

The primary natural community object remains the **background-controlled effective-service composition vector**.

```text
w_bg = visit_rate_per_flower_hour
       x background_adjusted_single_visit_conspecific_pollen_deposition

p_bg = w_bg / sum_g(w_bg)
```

Guardrails remain unchanged: missing/uncontrolled effectiveness is unavailable, not zero; negative adjusted weights are not clipped; zero-total-service blocks do not manufacture a composition; total effective service stays separate; no outcome-informed PCA/clustering/grouping/thresholding is allowed.

## H5 — conditional prediction CLOSED; precision OPEN

The old one-sided H5 has been replaced before focal reproductive outcomes by two prospectively distinguishable conditions.

**H5a lower shared dependence:** aggregation should reduce community-realization contribution and increase plant-state contribution; rank reversal is tested only in a pre-outcome crossover-capable domain.

**H5b higher shared dependence:** shared/synchronous stochasticity should preserve a community-realization floor, so rank reversal should be weakened or absent.

Dependence strata, or a continuous dependence-by-aggregation interaction, must be frozen from predictor/exposure data before focal reproductive outcomes are opened. A numeric field cutoff cannot be selected from outcome direction, and `rho=0.25` cannot be imported as a natural cutoff.

The pre-pilot synthetic screen shows why this matters. At low benchmark dependence, crossover separation rises from `0.5425` at 16 blocks to `0.835` at 32 and `0.900` at 48 under the benchmark design. Near the generic boundary, even more than 1000 recruited plants can remain indecisive. At high dependence, evidence instead accumulates for the predicted no-crossover side. Therefore **independent block count and dependence regime, not nominal flower/visit counts, are the H5 bottleneck**.

These rates are synthetic order-of-magnitude diagnostics, not empirical power, and cannot replace R3.
