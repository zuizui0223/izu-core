## Where the paper should put the newer transfer experiment

Completed [bounded transfer](CHAPTER2_READAPTATION_RESULTS_20261019.md): 64 seed cohorts from 32 donors (4 founders × 4 histories × 2 past policies × 2 cohorts/donor) into five externally imposed visitor conditions, with 32 futures/destination; **10,240 futures are nested**, not 10,240 founders. All 32 evolving-history seed cohorts had A fixed at .75 without A polymorphism, and only **4/32 per policy** retained segregating I variation even though **24/32 per policy** retained allele support meeting a high-I target. This is a **downstream conditional response study** after Q4, not a fifth independently confirmed link, a full probability-defined loss/recovery window, or proof of permanent irreversibility. No mutation or immigration was allowed. Performance changes were survivor-conditioned and policy histories began transfer with different M/I/A genomes.
