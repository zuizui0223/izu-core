# Island ecology and Chapter 2 answer audit: scope and design

Date: 2026-09-27. Supersedes no frozen model or result. User requests autonomous completion of the ecological question audit and necessary validation. Q1 remains motivation only.

## Intent and claim boundary

The new question is whether isolation-driven visitor assembly changes the distribution of inherited floral responses, and whether reproductive assurance, seed connectivity and finite communities explain that distribution. A detailed reproduction operator alone does not demonstrate an island-specific mechanism. Island processes need not be exclusive to islands: explicit isolation, founding/inheritance and source connectivity must be the explanatory contrasts.

Old Ch2 measured island-minus-mainland service differences across starting traits and histories. Model 3 measured inherited investment changes and persistence. Endpoint replacement is not replication of the old result. Finite plant populations and finite visitor communities are distinct axes. Keeping the same external visitor history in an individual/density comparison does not average the visitor environment.

## Stage A: audit stored evidence without new simulation

Before inspecting new sign counts, record this retrospective analysis rule. Use every one of the six transport cells and both 128-history cohorts, with both demographic repeats: 3,072 stored cases. Source arrays and input receipts must match frozen manifest and SHA256. Three starting investment means (0.3,0.5,0.7) are different initial populations, not coexisting plant species or individuals.

Report terminal-minus-initial investment separately at visitor distance 0 and 3, then paired distance-3 minus distance-0 change. Preserve identical initial-state pairing and extinction eligibility. Average the two demographic repeats within each start/history for descriptive sign classification; also report repeat-specific classification disagreement. Mixed means at least one start above +epsilon and one below -epsilon. Report all epsilon values 0,0.01,0.05; do not select a favorable threshold. Undefined histories require any missing start/repeat; do not turn extinction into zero. Use both individual and density endpoints and equal-weight descriptive S/C/I with within-cell variance. New counts are retrospective, not a newly blinded confirmation from the previously inspected held-out cohort. No raw mixed-sign proportion is a noise-free biological branching probability.

## Stage B: the minimum unanswered mechanism tests

1. Distinguish visitor richness/arrival-rate effects from realized visitor composition. Pair isolated and connected island histories, then a response-blind richness-matched visitor-history intervention. Preserve plant capacity, founder distribution and reproductive operators. Empty-year matching may make both arms empty and is a substantive intervention, not missing data. Report it explicitly.
2. Distinguish finite visitor histories from finite plant populations. Independently vary environmental averaging/community sampling and plant capacity. Never call increased plant capacity a finite-community control. Any pooling intervention must specify whether activity, functional composition, identities and nonlinear reproduction are averaged; it cannot be labelled lifespan k.
3. Numerical gate before biological claims: separate projected initial founders, later allele projection and demographic sampling. Use resolution comparisons of the actual focal contrasts, not unrelated control means. Freeze additional seed cohorts and maximum computation before outcomes, retain old precision failures, and permit an inconclusive terminal outcome.

The exact intervention and numerical grid budget require operator-level tests and an outcome-blind resource pilot before production. Do not start a large unreviewed Cartesian simulation or promise a universal C-I-S sequence. Rank reversal is an outcome, never a success criterion.
