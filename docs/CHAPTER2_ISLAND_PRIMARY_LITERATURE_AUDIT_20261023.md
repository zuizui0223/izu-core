**Scientific gap sharpened:** empirical data already establish some aspects of nonuniform island floral change and can contrast establishment filtering with in situ morphological evolution. Our contribution is therefore *not* discovering selfing/reduced display for the first time or showing that natural evidence can never distinguish filtering. It is experimentally separating the **necessity of evolving assurance**, the conditional investment response, and the different resource-specific consequences for population persistence versus **retained founder-relative high-I allelic options**, within one finite synthetic model.

**Still open:** final author reference formatting and the complete DOI/data deposit, real-island generality and model-to-measurement calibration. These three papers provide primary ecological context, **not the raw data used in this synthetic experiment**. The editorial update performs no new biological simulations, no meta-analysis, and no new preregistered hypothesis test.
