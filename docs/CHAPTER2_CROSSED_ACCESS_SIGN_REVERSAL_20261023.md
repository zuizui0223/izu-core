## How heterogeneous are the policy effects?

The founder-level policy effect means, each averaged over **all 160 visitor contexts**, are **negative in 151, zero in 2, positive in 7** at B8, versus **positive in all 160** at B4.5. Visitor-level means, each averaged over **all 160 founders**, are negative in 147, zero in 4 and positive in 9 at B8, versus positive in all 160 at B4.5. The interaction (B4.5 effect minus B8 effect) is positive for **all 160 founder means and all 160 visitor means**. Founder/visitor levels share the 25,600 crossing cells; these counts are **not** 320 independent sign-test observations or per-source significance tests.

## Why the sign reverses — and what is not identified

The correct competing first-event categories are: **G** (target-compatible support first lost while occupied), **E** (extinction before G), and **X** (still alive and target-accessible at census 80). These partition the original all-start denominator as `P(X)=1−CIF(G)−CIF(E)`, with CIFs being cumulative *first-event* probabilities and not cause-specific genetic-loss hazards.

- **B8:** First-G is 16,000 evolving vs 13,868 fixed, and first-E is zero in both: joint X is reduced by 2,132 cells (**−8.328125 pp**).
- **B4.5:** First-G is 16,018 evolving vs 12,896 fixed (+3,122 cells), **but first-E falls from 12,564 fixed to 337 evolving** (−12,227 cells); joint X therefore increases by 9,105 cells (**+35.56640625 pp**).

The greater first-G cumulative incidence at B4.5 does **not** identify a faster direct genetic-loss hazard: severe fixed-A extinction removes populations from future risk. Among terminal survivors, high-I support is present in **36.81% of evolving** vs **10.11% of fixed** B4.5 cases, but that conditional contrast is post-treatment survivor selection; it is **not** a causal effect on genetic maintenance. Nothing here partitions the net gain into unique genetic versus demographic mediation shares.

The biologically useful supported question is thus *when can reproductive assurance evolution increase, rather than decrease, the set of living descendant populations retaining a specified ancestral-compatible genetic option?* These two synthetic resource conditions exhibit opposite total-policy effects and the comparison is robust across sampled founder/visitor-axis marginal averages. The analysis **does not** establish the number/location of resource thresholds, natural island/environment effect, universal direction, population size dependence independent of reproduction, actual restored-pollinator recovery, or biological generalization outside this model.
