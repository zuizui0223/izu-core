# Current comparative island evidence state

Updated: 2026-08-22

This is the top-level state for the island comparative programme. `docs/CURRENT_EVIDENCE_STATE.md` is retained as the **Izu/Campanula calibration substate** and must not be read as the programme-wide scope.

## Decision

The inferential unit is the **independent island system**, not Izu alone. Multiple biological systems from one archipelago can inform different mechanism links, but they do not automatically create additional independent geographic replicates.

Current programme question:

> Across independent island systems, which upstream pollinator-functional changes recur, which downstream response branches follow, and what determines whether those changes propagate into reproductive or floral evolutionary response?

## Current response / propagation vocabulary

- pollinator functional environment / matching;
- floral morphology;
- mating and reproductive assurance;
- visual signal, including nectar guides / UV signal;
- pollinator effectiveness and effective service;
- interaction-network structure / rewiring;
- reproductive outcome;
- propagation state: same-direction propagation, downstream branching, buffering/resilience, counterdirectional response, adjacent links, or an unresolved missing link.

Systems are not required to measure every axis. Missing is not zero. A blocked or buffered propagation step is itself an admissible biological result.

These counts are routing/architecture counts, not inferential sample sizes. Panels, flowers, visits, plants, seasons, papers and biological systems nested within one geography are not promoted mechanically to independent evolutionary replicates.

## Strongest current comparative result

A universal directional island syndrome is not supported. The recurring result is **state-dependent propagation**: an upstream functional or interaction change can propagate, branch, be buffered, run counterdirectionally, or remain only partially linked to reproduction.

The clearest contrasting cases are now:

- **Ogasawara *Psychotria*** — contemporary physical access failure at the hidden S-morph stigma aligns with S→L directional pollen transfer and strong morph-specific field fruit asymmetry: `propagates_same_direction` for this bounded contemporary chain.
- **Hawaiian lobelioids 2026** — signed bill–flower mismatch predicts pollen contact / nectar robbing and damage alters nectar replenishment, while fruit and seed performance remain high: `buffered_or_resilient`.
- **Izu** — broadly lower corrected matching coexists with divergent pollen and tube responses: `branches_downstream`.
- **Dominica *Heliconia*** — the first frozen negative signed-position-to-selection prediction ran in the opposite direction: `counterdirectional`.

The propagation-state counts in the layer matrix are descriptive only: 1 same-direction propagation, 2 branching, 3 buffered/resilient, 1 counterdirectional, 4 adjacent-links-only, and 3 unresolved/missing-link layers.

## Ogasawara *Psychotria* propagation contrast

The admitted Ogasawara bridge deliberately uses a **categorical physical-access construct**, not an invented numeric signed trait position.

The contemporary source-supported chain is:

```text
hidden S-morph stigma inaccessible to dominant Apis
        -> S-to-L directional pollen transfer
        -> much higher field fruit set in accessible L morph
```

Published same-species intermorph hand crosses set fruit at 82.4% and 82.6% in the two directions, while open fruit set is 2.8–8.9% for L morphs and 0.2–1.4% for S morphs. Thus the field asymmetry cannot be reduced simply to a directional inability of one morph to produce fruit after compatible intermorph pollen is supplied.

The classification does **not** establish experimental historical pollinator-replacement causation, a numeric signed mismatch, standardized single-visit effectiveness, controlled total pollinator dependency, or an already-realized floral evolutionary transition.

## ABM mechanism state

The failure-driven ABM ladder separates **branch generation** from **branch reallocation**.

- v4 survives held-out Izu qualitatively: lower best matching is the majority synthetic outcome while reproductive responses retain both signs.
- v10 shows partner effectiveness can change individual branch identity but does not broaden the aggregate positive tail.
- v11 shows local support, dependency heterogeneity, adaptive assurance and partner effectiveness are not individually necessary to generate two-sided branching conditional on the frozen upstream state.
- v12 identifies pre-existing lineage position in functional trait space as the minimal generator of same-environment within-run sign branching in the declared model.
- Dominica rejects universalizing that position into a simple direction rule: its first frozen real-data projection failed.

Thus v12 is a **synthetic mechanism result**, not an empirically established causal law.

## Next admissible work

1. Build a **propagation-vs-buffering discriminator** using the already-admitted Ogasawara, Hawaii, Nicotiana, Guaiacum and Izu cases.
2. For each case, separate candidate filters—autonomous assurance, resource compensation, alternative effective partners, establishment/colonisation filtering, lineage functional position, or genuinely unknown—from measurements that are actually present.
3. Rank the next missing measurement by its ability to distinguish those mechanisms, not by raw data volume.
4. Continue Issue #91 direct dependency / FDQ field calibration in parallel when real data become available.
5. Do not add another generic ABM layer until a new empirical discriminator exposes a specific missing mechanism class.
6. Keep formal same-estimand pooling closed until independent systems provide compatible estimands, uncertainty and hierarchy.

## Claim boundary

Do not infer a universal island response, do not pool noncommensurate response axes into one coefficient, and do not treat missing cells as no response. Do not call v12 empirically confirmed, do not retune the failed Dominica mapping, and do not manufacture an Ogasawara numeric signed position from secondary honeybee or floral measurements. The current contribution is a falsification-driven comparison of **where functional change propagates, branches or is buffered**, not a completed universal causal law.
