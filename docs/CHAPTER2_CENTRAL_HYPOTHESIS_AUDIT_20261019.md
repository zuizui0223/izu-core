## What the audit changes in the four-part story

1. Existing four-setting evidence that floral investment can decline without
   evolving assurance remains the established starting point; this audit does
   not rerun or strengthen its independent replication count.
2. In the equal-A pilot, living populations lose a necessary I support bound.
   Even with .05 tolerance this occurs in 22/72 fixed and 30/72 evolving paths.
   This supports a qualified exploratory distinction between persistence and
   inherited options, not universal loss of recovery.
3. Assurance evolution changes the exploratory closure clock in the predicted
   direction, with substantial target sensitivity. Independent confirmation
   remains outstanding. Both initial arms contain the **same allele variation**
   and every adult expresses A=.5; only subsequent assurance policy differs.
4. A support closure is robust over this tolerance grid, but actual restored
   evolution is untested. In the replay's 648 evolving-arm static snapshots,
   local monomorphic I gradients are positive in 648, while A gradients are
   negative in 485. These correlated diagnostic snapshots are not 648 independent
   restoration trials or polymorphic invasion tests.

The newer [budget companion reconciliation](CHAPTER2_BUDGET_COMPANION_STREAM_RECONCILIATION_20261018.md)

Consequently, assurance can preserve living options under severe resource

six-by-six backgrounds and must not be counted as independent confirmations.

## Cost-free delayed selfing: a precise remaining prediction

Native `scripts/model3_island/selection.py::syndrome_thresholds` implies, for
delayed selfing, assurance cost=0 and pollen discount=0,

`beta_A = (1-d)(1-q) / [q + A(1-d)(1-q)] >= 0`,

where d is inbreeding depression and q the resident outcross fraction, provided
fitness is positive and the resident is interior. It is strictly positive when
d<1 and q<1, and zero in the corresponding limiting cases. Thus the conditional
prediction is **no local selection to lower A**, not necessarily neutrality.
The 36 existing founder/visitor resident combinations pass the native positive
gradient check without evolutionary updates. This mathematical statement does
not show that a population evolves irreversibly or returns after pollination
is restored; costs, pollen discount, prior selfing or different states can
change it. The cost-.5 result above is a separate setting.
