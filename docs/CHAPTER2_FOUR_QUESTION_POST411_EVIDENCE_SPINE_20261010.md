## Central scientific question

**When do the local reproductive payoffs of floral investment and assurance change sign; does the order of phenotypic responses matter; are the favoured changes genetically realized in finite populations; and when do those realized changes alter collective reproduction and persistence?**

The requested inferential order is **threshold → order → realization → consequence**. The four words do **not** denote four already confirmed causal links. The study must distinguish a local static source diagnostic, a randomized transient-expression schedule, naturally realized inherited genotypes, and the later demographic response. None can be substituted for the next.

## Q1 — Where does selection change sign?

The merged four-setting #411 experiment showed that floral investment declines under low visitor replenishment even with assurance evolution fixed, while allowing assurance evolution compresses the near–far contrast (+0.1007 to +0.2200 in all four frozen settings). Those are realized model-evolution comparisons, **not** evidence that all components of the current four-question chain have been identified.

The exploratory source-state grid in #452 found **14/384** floral-investment cells with a negative focal investment selection gradient but a positive collective viable-seed derivative (362 aligned, eight inconclusive). In its stated monomorphic B48/fixed-visitor context, the sign-conflict window was **N=6–9**, not a universal density threshold. In original genotype/census-matched dynamic visitor states with *four visitor types in both arms*, replacing visitor composition restored the sign-conflict classification in **176/505** states. These are correlated source-year comparisons and an instantaneous manipulation of synthetic visitors, **not** 505 independent island populations.

The **latest #452 delivery-matched falsifier matters**: in 36 artificial source contexts, holding *total delivered pollen* equal abolished the collective seed difference for all 18 monomorphic fixtures to numerical precision and left only 0.000904 mean absolute difference for 18 mixed-diploid fixtures, versus 0.353998 before the match. Thus no large additional **recipient-routing benefit at equal total delivery** is established in this source kernel. Genetically mixed paternal shares can still change even when total maternal seed converges, but the observed group seed effect is predominantly a delivered-quantity phenomenon.

The merged retrospective #457 audit used actual original #411 evolving diploid genomes, **64 old visitor histories × one of eight demographic repeats**, not a fresh confirmatory cohort. Unilateral higher investment increased other mothers' expected viable seed return in 62–63 of 64 near histories, depending on mating setting. Crucially, checking full expected finite **focal parental** return `W=.5F+.5P+S` narrowed the stronger `W<0 / whole-group viable-seed response>0` result to **196–438/3,072 correlated individual perturbations**, occurring in **12–23/64 history blocks** depending on setting. A negative focal *maternal* return is not synonymous with negative total genetic fitness.

**Q1 verdict:** Conditional local selection remains distinct from exact event timing; the complete tempo cohort adds sampled, genotype-dependent sign paths with late feedback. Identify the ecological domain of conditional private-versus-nonfocal conflict. Do not substitute an observed range of synthetic N for a natural evolutionary threshold, or call a positive nonfocal benefit a generally positive **group** benefit.

## Q2 — Does the order actually matter?

The #415 intervention was a *transient expression schedule* of equal prescribed A and I exposure, with the A-first and I-first main arms matched on joint-expression duration. It did **not** randomize the naturally occurring order of mutation or inherited allele changes.

The complete #418 experiment (`3,072` source populations, `86,016` future branches; **64 independent visitor-history units**) reported pooled far–near A-first versus I-first history-paired DID **−0.0135891**, 95% cluster bootstrap **[−0.0228119, −0.0043024]**. Though this excludes numerical zero, the entire interval is inside the frozen **±0.05 practical-equivalence region**. The predeclared conclusion is *equivalent within the declared ROPE*, **not** an ecologically important order effect. The budget-3 post-hoc trough did not independently replicate under #422, whose budget-window hypothesis failed its registered gates.

The prior independent mutation-access priority hypothesis in #413 also **failed**. Scheduling a phenotype, manipulating mutation opportunity and observing spontaneous allele trajectories are three distinct interventions/observables.

**Q2 verdict:** The completed, separately frozen tempo contrast shows context-dependent crossing-frequency changes, while the earlier assigned-expression and mutation-access verdicts remain unchanged. An observed A-only outcome or inherited sequence cannot be claimed necessary or generally causal; visitor-tempo assignment does not identify a causal mutation-order effect.

## Q3 — Does selection become inherited evolutionary change?

Finite diploid source states retain parental identity, segregation and allele variance. The #455 analysis constructs one-step conditional examples in which equal adult phenotype and equal expected reproductive accounting can lead to different inherited offspring distributions because of heterozygosity and parental lottery. This is an application of standard conditional variance accounting, **not a new evolutionary law**, and the fixtures condition on realized offspring number rather than measuring unconditional Model 3 survival.

#452's 192-new-history assurance-locus-source transplant produced a descriptive K8 survival difference of **+19.35 percentage points**, but the **precommitted joint clustered-bootstrap plus Hoeffding lower-bound criterion failed** (Hoeffding lower −1.61 pp). This remains **INCONCLUSIVE**, is conditional on jointly surviving donors, and manipulates inherited genomic origins rather than the natural causal effect of assurance-first evolutionary order.

**Q3 verdict:** Native inherited trajectories under contrasting visitor tempos are now measured, but a general causal mediator linking local `W` to a sustained A/I response remains unestablished. Use founder-paired, unconditional annual trajectories and explicit genetic-state accounting; never select only histories that evolved or survived to claim an order-mediated effect.

## Q4 — What are the population consequences?

The independent #442 preregistered full-period contrast (64 new visitor-history clusters, matched up-to-eight founder genomes, fixed pollen-background `B=48`) found the **K8 − K48 moderator of the imposed expression-order × half-viable-selfed-seed sensitivity** to be **+0.0077457139** absolute occupancy probability, 95% history-bootstrap **[+0.0024971581, +0.0130154788]**. This is a **small, model-conditional, controlled demographic moderator**; it is neither spontaneous evolutionary-order causality nor an effect of investment evolution on extinction.

#448's separately registered **early versus late** selfed-seed viability timing moderator was **INCONCLUSIVE**. In the original #411 trial all terminal occupancy arms equalled 1.0. The actual-genome #457 replay found lower pollen delivery under the evolutionary investment state versus an imposed investment restoration, but **three of four settings had greater mean total viable maternal seed output** under the evolved state; source allocation/receipt contributions had broad post-hoc uncertainty. Native K48 one-year Poisson-capped expected census differences were below **0.00025 plants**, and lower-budget exploratory screens did not produce a general adverse group sign. #452's independently collected pilot had occupancy floor/ceiling and changing selection signs across census sizes; its investment→persistent tragedy hypothesis was explicitly **NO-GO** for a confirmatory launch based on that selected envelope.

**Q4 verdict:** The complete tempo cohort changes realized pollen/overlap and recruitment composition but has saturated occupancy at 100/400, so it supplies no persistence conclusion. Neither `delivered pollen↓` nor `other-mother seed effects` alone identifies `population survival↓`. Resource allocation, recipient-specific receipt, selfing, inbreeding depression, density regulation and genetic transmission must remain separate causal stages.

## The three links that must be tested, not assumed

1. **Q1 → Q2:** A corrected local selection gradient is not the first realized allele crossing. The hypothesized threshold and the timing intervention must be independent of observed future outcomes; mutation-access order and transient phenotypic order cannot be conflated.
2. **Q2 → Q3:** The randomized assigned-expression contrast identifies the effect of *assignment*, not natural inherited sequence. Preserve the full founder and annual genotype/pedigree histories, including extinction-before-crossing, and analyze realized trajectories without conditioning the primary contrast on a post-treatment state.
3. **Q3 → Q4:** The difference between paternal/maternal reproductive returns, viable maternal seed, actual recruits and survival must be measured along **the same matched inherited source histories**. A source-level positive nonfocal effect or a separate K-moderation cohort cannot be turned into an evolutionary mediation fraction.

## Historical October 10 proposal: a go/no-go **identification and feasibility contract**, not another confirmatory run

**Historical scope:** The following proposal is retained as the original October 10 contract, before the separately frozen #475 tempo experiment. Its “zero new outcomes” statement applies to that proposal; it is not a claim that the now-complete tempo outcomes are unavailable. This evidence update grants no future execution authorization and changes no registration.

Before running additional biological histories, assemble a *prospective* decision specification that is independent of already exposed #452/#457 outcomes:

- **Target:** within Model 3, distinguish (a) selection-favoured lower investment with positive nonfocal service, (b) a sign-opposed **whole-group** viable offspring response, and (c) a genuine nonsaturated inherited-population occupancy consequence. Treat three levels as separate registered endpoints; a result at (a) is not evidence for (c).
- **Unit and matching:** independent fresh visitor-history clusters, matched original diploid founders and environmental stream, explicit functional assemblage; matched individual genetic response `W`, `F/P/S`, pollen-delivery and receipt accounting, `K` distinct from `B`. Do not bootstrap plants or repeated years as independent histories.
- **Critical controls:** no-visitor zero externality, same-assemblage control, matched *total delivered pollen*, matched genomic starting states, appropriate intervention on future expression/inheritance rather than comparison of different evolved branch means, and seed-allocation compensation.
- **Feasibility stop:** externally justified demographic envelope with nonsaturated recruitment **and** the relevant selection conflict while plant census changes. If all source regimes are at the floor/ceiling, or if the sign-conflict disappears before recruitment becomes informative, conclude **not identifiable with current Model 3 biology**; do not choose a favourable budget from exposed pilot cells.
- **Prospective decision:** register a biological minimum effect, a single primary causal contrast, the history-cluster CI/power calculation, independent-history count, first-extinction and unconditional occupancy rules, null/adverse outcomes and stop rules before selecting or generating any new histories. Current document is **NOT** that registration and authorizes **zero** new outcomes.
