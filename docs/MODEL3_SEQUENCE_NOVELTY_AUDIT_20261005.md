# Selfing before attraction reduction: targeted novelty audit

Checked 2026-10-05. Targeted primary-literature assessment, not an exhaustive systematic review. No simulation setting, result or claim of numerical closure is changed.

## What already exists

**Bodbyl Roels & Kelly (2011), experimental evolution in Mimulus guttatus.** Five generations with abundant bumblebees versus no pollinators produced early reproductive failure followed by improved autonomous self-fertilization. The authors explicitly interpret their findings as a sequential selfing-syndrome model: traits improving selfing efficiency change before other floral characters. This is direct precedent against presenting "selfing first" as a new general hypothesis. The interpretation should not be inflated into a measured universal lag between selfing and flower-size reduction. Sources: [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21884055/), [primary full-text record, Summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5958604/), [publisher](https://academic.oup.com/evolut/article/65/9/2541/6854748). Direct PMC opening returned CAPTCHA and publisher opening returned HTTP 429; the primary-source indexed Summary and PubMed abstract were accessible. DOI: 10.1111/j.1558-5646.2011.01326.x.

**Sicard et al. (2011), Capsella genetics and functional tests.** The study links reduced flower opening to improved self-pollination and explicitly predicts loss of self-incompatibility before flower-size reduction under reproductive assurance. It also explicitly states that the actual historical order could not be determined. Thus genetic/functional support and a sequence hypothesis predate Model 3; historical timing was not resolved by this paper. Source: [primary paper, Evolutionary History and Adaptive Value sections](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3203431/). DOI: 10.1105/tpc.111.088237.

**Sakai (1995), resource-allocation theory.** Attraction allocation, selfing, delayed/competing selfing and inbreeding depression already appear in a joint theoretical framework. The reported optimum depends on allocation constraints; inbreeding depression can maintain greater attraction and lower selfing. Model 3 therefore cannot claim that representing attraction costs and inbreeding depression together is novel. This audit verified the publisher abstract, not all equations in the paywalled PDF. Source: [primary publisher abstract](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.1995.tb02287.x).

**Additional close precedent.** The nine-generation Mimulus follow-up documents adaptive selfing and loss of genomic variation after bee exclusion. It makes a blanket claim that finite-population/genetic consequences of pollinator loss are unstudied untenable. Source: [Busch et al. (2022), primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9543508/), DOI: 10.1111/evo.14572. Model 3 uses fixed inbreeding depression and does not reproduce evolving deleterious load or purging.

## What the current Model 3 evidence adds

The defensible contribution is a **model-conditional separation of sequence, necessity and geographic divergence under persistent arrival limitation**, not discovery of the selfing syndrome or of a universal selfing-first sequence.

| Question | Current evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Which substantial within-population change occurs first? | At the declared 0.05 change held for 20 updates, capacity precedes investment in 51/64 far-history means in the delayed/cost setting; 13 are within five updates. | Threshold crossing, not first infinitesimal onset. Equal abstract-axis changes need not be biologically equivalent. |
| Is that also the order of additional isolation effects? | In the same setting, far-minus-near investment divergence crosses first in 32 histories, capacity in 10, with 20 ties and two investment-only cases. | Distinct estimand, not a contradiction or proof of mediation. |
| Must selfing capacity evolve for investment to fall? | The separate fixed-capacity intervention still produces far-treatment investment decline. | Capacity remains 0.5; realized selfing can change. The cohort has different initial capacity variation from the main experiment. |
| What initiates a return change? | Fixed plants under different visitor exposures show a change in net investment return without plant evolution. | Visitor number and composition change together; not a number-only causal estimate. |
| Does low pollen receipt uniquely determine attraction selection? | The existing rare-mutant threshold retains recipient response, paternal export and allocation cost separately. | A local identity, not a universal distance threshold or the exact heterogeneous ABM trajectory. |

## Island-ecology contribution and precise wording

In the declared model, distance reduces ongoing immigration while per-type disappearance hazard stays the same. The far treatment consequently experiences prolonged visitor absence. This makes replenishment failure an explicit upstream ecological process rather than imposing a direction of floral evolution. It is a severe, uncalibrated contrast, not a fitted description of typical oceanic islands. No claim that Q1 measured visitor loss follows.

Suggested statement:

> Under persistent visitor replenishment limitation, autonomous-selfing capacity can change before attraction investment, yet evolving capacity is not required for investment decline. Temporal sequence therefore need not identify a single selfing-mediated pathway: visitor limitation can alter reproductive assurance and investment returns through distinguishable processes.

The distinction is useful even though the component ideas have precedent. Its publication-level originality rests on the linked interventions, explicitly island-assembly conditions and resulting ecological predictions. This targeted search cannot certify that the entire combination has never been studied.

Do not claim: first evidence that selfing precedes floral reduction; selfing-independent decline in the absence of any selfing; causal fractions for two pathways; empirical flower-colour evolution; universally stronger physiological costs on islands; or completed positive-mutation ABM/deterministic/PDE corroboration.

## Most useful next claim test

Report the existing investment gradient components separately before assigning a mechanism: recipient-side contribution, paternal contribution and allocation cost. Preserve fixed capacity versus absent selfing as different interventions. Distinguish pollen level from its response to marginal investment. A visitor-number-only interpretation would require a declared composition-controlled comparison, and a realized-selfing-independent interpretation needs a separate intervention. Neither follows from temporal precedence. These are inference boundaries, not permission to retune the frozen model.
