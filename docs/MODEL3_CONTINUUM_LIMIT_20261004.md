## Bottom line

The current Model 3 deterministic density implementation is **not a PDE**. Its exact continuous-state analogue is a **nonlinear, nonlocal, discrete-time integro-difference / measure-valued dynamical system for sexually reproducing diploid genotypes**.

One component does possess a clean PDE limit: the reflected-normal mutation kernel used in `scripts/model3_island/density.py` is the Neumann heat semigroup on `[0,1]`. For mutation probability `u` and mutation SD `sigma`, the weak-mutation diffusion coefficient per mutable gametic allele is

[

A pure trait-density PDE for the full Model 3 requires additional closure assumptions beyond the implemented model, because Mendelian segregation, outcrossing between distinct genotypes, recombination, and global capacity regulation remain nonlocal.

This distinction matters especially for the focal Chapter 2 bridge: its frozen configuration has `mutation_rate = 0`, so the diffusion coefficient is exactly zero. The headline isolation/repeatability results are therefore generated by pollinator-mediated reproduction, inheritance, and finite/density realization, not by a mutation-diffusion term.

would require several extra approximations not present in Model 3:

1. **Local offspring approximation.** Sexual offspring would need to remain close to a single parental trait state. Model 3 can mate distinct parental genotypes and generate nonlocal offspring.
2. **Genotype-to-phenotype closure.** A density over the three expressed means `z` does not retain within-genotype allele pairing, heterozygosity, or linkage information needed for exact Mendelian inheritance.
3. **Linkage-equilibrium / Hardy–Weinberg closure.** The exact diploid distribution would need to be reconstructed from a lower-dimensional allele or trait density.
4. **Weak-selection / small-step scaling.** One reproductive year would need to become a small `dt`; the current reproduction operator can make order-one changes in one step.
5. **Smooth capacity regulation.** The hard global retention factor `min(1, space/total)` would need a continuous-time density-regulation replacement.

Only after those assumptions can a reduced replicator–mutator or Fokker–Planck PDE be proposed.

## 9. The immediate falsification test for a PDE reduction

Do not compare a fitted PDE only to final means. A valid reduction should recover, without retuning per scenario:

1. one-step changes in mean access and investment;
2. one-step changes in trait variance;
3. the sign of the near–far investment contrast;
4. the reversal caused by response-blind visitor-richness matching;
5. the distinction between visitor-history pooling and larger plant capacity where applicable;
6. the persistence/occupancy boundary, or explicitly declare that persistence is outside the PDE state space.

A useful first gate is controlled, occupied, mutation-positive conditions. The focal Chapter 2 bridge is not the correct first diffusion test because `mutation_rate=0`.

## 10. What this means for the island-syndrome claim

The derivation strengthens one point and blocks another.

**Strengthened:** the syndrome-like directional response in the current model is not an artifact of a hand-coded diffusion toward an island optimum. The focal bridge has no mutation diffusion at all. Its coarse floral-investment response emerges from pollinator-mediated frequency-dependent reproduction and inheritance.

**Blocked:** we cannot yet say that the island syndrome has been analytically derived as a PDE attractor. The exact continuum object remains a sexual integro-difference system, and the long-run stationary analysis does not establish a natural attractor.

The mathematically defensible claim at this stage is:

> Model 3 admits an exact continuous-genotype, nonlocal deterministic formulation. Its mutation operator has a Neumann diffusion limit, but the syndrome-generating reproductive mechanism remains nonlocal. A pure PDE is therefore a reduced approximation to be validated, not a relabeling of the existing density model.

This is stronger than a sign approximation. Under additive expression, no mutation, no survival and no immigration, Mendelian segregation preserves the expected parental allele value, so the **first moment closes exactly** even though the full offspring distribution does not.

### Sixty-year fixed-community fidelity

The same reduction was propagated for all 25 frozen fixed-community cells for 60 reproductive years in two forms:

- discrete replicator map: p' proportional to p w;
- continuous-time weak-form replicator PDE: partial_t p = (w/mean(w) - 1)p, with D=0.

Both recover **25/25 deterministic-density response directions**. In the exploratory fidelity audit:

- discrete map mean absolute error ≈ **0.00417**, maximum ≈ **0.01226**;
- continuous replicator PDE mean absolute error ≈ **0.00434**, maximum ≈ **0.01234**.

Thus the coarse investment trajectory in controlled visitor environments is already largely captured by the continuous phenotype-selection field.

### Frozen isolation bridge confrontation

The reduced continuous-phenotype selection model was then run on the exact 128 frozen near/far visitor-history seeds and the same projected founders. No natural-island target was fitted.

| intervention | exact genotype-density mean | reduced selection mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| natural near→far | -0.450983 | about -0.251735 |

The reduced model recovers:

- **9/9 start-level signs** across the three starts × three interventions;
- the same regime ordering, pooled < natural < richness-matched;
- condition-level correlation of approximately **0.987** with the exact density effects.

But the slope of reduced versus exact effects is only about **0.586**. Therefore the phenotype-selection reduction captures the **direction and intervention structure** while systematically attenuating the inherited response magnitude.

This localizes the missing mathematics: the main island-like direction does not require an imposed island optimum or a mutation-diffusion force, but the full magnitude does require the nonlocal genetic redistribution removed by the phenotype-only closure.

### Why a single extra diffusion coefficient is not enough

The one-generation second-moment audit clarifies the failure.

Across the same 25 frozen cells:

- the reduced Price closure still matches the exact next-generation mean to machine precision;
- the exact Mendelian inheritance correction to investment variance is **positive in some ecological contexts and negative in others**.

Therefore sexual inheritance cannot be recovered exactly by simply adding one context-independent nonnegative diffusion coefficient to the selection PDE. Mating averages parental states while segregation re-expands variation; the net second-moment correction depends on genotype and mating structure.

The exact continuum description must therefore retain the nonlocal inheritance kernel, or a later PDE reduction must use a justified state-dependent moment/QLE closure.

This gives a more useful mathematical statement of the island-syndrome mechanism:

> **A syndrome-like reduction in floral investment emerges when isolation-driven pollination opportunity lowers the marginal reproductive return to investment below its marginal cost. Reproductive assurance further lowers the relative value of pollinator-mediated gain. The exact magnitude and variance trajectory then depend on nonlocal sexual inheritance and finite realization.**

## 13. Current mathematical hierarchy

The work now separates four objects that should not be conflated:

1. **Exact finite ABM** — stochastic individuals, Mendelian inheritance, finite demography.
2. **Exact deterministic genotype-density closure** — discrete genotype masses, same reproductive/inheritance operator, no demographic sampling.
3. **Exact continuous-genotype sexual equation** — nonlinear nonlocal integro-difference system obtained by replacing genotype sums with measures/integrals.
4. **Reduced continuous phenotype equation** — replicator / replicator-mutator PDE after collapsing genotype structure.

The new audits show that level 4 is already sufficient for the **coarse sign and regime structure** of the island-like floral response, but not for its full inherited magnitude or second-moment dynamics.

That is the current strongest mathematical result. It is more precise than calling the existing density model a PDE and more informative than treating the PDE as a cosmetic continuum rewrite.
