Audit base: commit4638d5a4 and its retained result artifacts. This inventory covers
the current Model3 scientific result families and the earlier bridge used by the
active manuscript. It does not certify every historical repository file or claim
priority over all literature. Numerical verification and literature novelty are

## Confirmatory update — 2026-10-06

The paper-level sequence/necessity result is no longer supported only by the
exploratory 2026-10-05 cohort. A frozen 4,096-case replication with 64 new
visitor histories and eight new demographic repeats confirmed both primary
components: delayed/costly positive-mutation assurance-first order was 51/64
(95% history-bootstrap 0.6875–0.8906), and fixed-assurance investment decline
remained negative with its full interval below zero. The sequence claim remains
setting-specific because prior selfing with positive mutation gave 30/64
assurance-first histories at the same threshold.

## Main contribution

Isolation changes the returns to autonomous reproduction and floral attraction
through ongoing visitor replenishment. The same explicit reproductive model
separates selection conditions, the order of inherited changes, and whether
capacity evolution is required. A visible sequence need not identify a single
causal pathway, and weak geographic divergence need not indicate weak evolution.

## Manuscript organization

1. Ecological process and local selection conditions: continuing isolation changes
   visitor exposure and the returns on two reproductive investments.
2. Realized sequence AND its causal limits: trajectories, paired crossing times,
   fixed-capacity control. Put the geographic-divergence attenuation next to this
   control as the strongest secondary surprise.
3. Reproductive consequences: outcross-return decomposition and the disconnect
   between fractional pollen deficit and viable offspring production.
4. Genetic accessibility: finite-versus-deterministic evidence with matched scope;
   restricted mutation/closure diagnostics explain what the broader comparison
   cannot yet establish. Do not imply the stopped calculation will be completed.

Primary claim wording: "Under sustained visitor replenishment limitation,
selfing capacity can change before attraction investment, yet its evolution is
not required for investment decline. Capacity evolution can also reduce the
contrast between isolation environments because investment changes in both."

## Poster organization within the user's three stages

Q1 remains the upper half. Q2 asks how arrival limitation changes reproductive
returns and the sequence of selfing and attraction changes. Its model diagram
must show continuous visitor arrival/loss, pollen transfer, reproduction costs,
depression, inheritance and parallel ABM/density propagation.

- Stage1: "When does attraction stop paying?" A simple cost/return condition
  plus the observed fixed-plant return contrast. Put full equations in bottom SI.
- Stage2: "Which changes first, and which requires the other?" Largest evidence
  area: early trajectories plus history-level order; a matched fixed/evolving
  capacity panel shows necessity and attenuation without mixing cohorts.
- Stage3: "Can selection continue after variation is lost?" Smaller supporting
  area: same phenotype/different genotype inset and a scoped mutation diagnostic.
  Label the full positive-mutation long comparison unresolved; no empty promise.
- Discussion: lower pollen deficit is not automatically higher viable output.
  Keep this one concrete consequence, with a small contrast figure if space allows.

Use three separate visual encodings: schematic arrows for hypothesized/process
links, actual trajectories for evolution, paired intervention plots for causal
comparisons. Keep individual-history marks visible. Do not use an illustration
as if it were a simulated phenotype or plot selected trajectories as all outcomes.
