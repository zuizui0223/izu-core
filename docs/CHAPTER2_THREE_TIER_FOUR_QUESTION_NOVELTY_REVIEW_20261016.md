## Verdict: what izu-core adds to prior theory

The originality is **not** the existence of floral-resource costs, reproductive assurance, stochastic plant population models, or genetic variation depletion. Its potentially defensible joint contribution is to **separate observed temporal precedence from evolutionary necessity** in a model with independently inherited floral investment (I) and reproductive assurance (A), then show that **inherited access and finite reproduction can restrict which threshold-defined responses actually occur**, with occupancy tested in a *separate* registered experiment.

**Short bounded claim:** Under specified pollen-limited synthetic visitor regimes, assurance evolution is not required for floral-investment decline even in settings where assurance-first ordering is frequent; genotype access and finite transmission condition the realizable order. Population persistence is a separate, condition-dependent endpoint rather than a proven outcome of spontaneous A-first evolution.

**Evidence strength is NOT novelty strength.** A replicated, well-estimated contrast may replicate a mechanism already established in general form. A more distinctive genotype/order observation can be scientifically important yet have narrower causal attribution or generality.

## Three evidence tiers (orthogonal to Q1–Q4)

**Tier 1 = positive finding supported under its predeclared source setting**, not a law for independently sampled island lineages.  
**Tier 2 = conditional intervention / restricted founder-reproductive regime; broader transport unconfirmed.**  
**Tier 3 = sensitivity, variability, model-prediction gap or an unresolved causal/transport question.**  
These are **evidence and transport tiers**, not universal-vs-chance ontological bins. Registered negative/inconclusive outcomes must be retained.

### Specific model-construction and mathematical side result

- Pollen receipt aggregated at stigmas does not identify paternal identity in the **broad constrained transfer-matrix class** for ≥3 eligible donors: dimension `n(n-2)` at fixed positive maternal receipts, with a three-plant exact example. See [mathematical supplement](CHAPTER2_POLLEN_FLOW_IDENTIFIABILITY_SUPPLEMENT_20261010.md) and [source proof](../scripts/prove_pollen_flow_identifiability.py). This is **not** proof every transfer matrix is attainable in native visitor-affinity Model 3, a new general plant-mating theorem, or evidence that DNA parentage is the *only* possible donor-resolving method (genetic parentage, tagged pollen and suitable experimental tracking can differ). Recipient-only records cannot uniquely infer fathers without additional donor information/assumptions.
- Source kernels explicitly account for maternal outcross, paternal outcross, viable selfing, resource allocation and diploid transmission. **Do not claim density and finite ABM share exactly the same mathematical operator**: density closure and finite individual self-pollen exclusion differ. The reproducible benefit is making those differences testable and their inferential ceilings explicit.

## Recommended single-paper and poster positioning

**Single-paper scientific question:**

> Under pollinator limitation, when do predicted floral-investment and reproductive-assurance responses actually arise in finite diploid populations, and can their apparent order, genetic reachability and persistence be inferred from one another?

**One-line bounded contribution:**

> In a genetically explicit plant–pollinator model, reproductive-assurance change can precede floral-investment decline without being required for it; finite inheritance and available alleles limit the realized response pathways, while later population occupancy depends on separate demographic treatments.

The sentence does **not** say wild island colonization history was reconstructed or that the Q1→Q4 chain was jointly causally identified.

**Paper figure hierarchy:** Fig 1 reproductive ledger/source mechanism; Fig 2 old vs fresh-founder Q1 effect with 4-setting registered qualification; Fig 3 model-conditional order mismatch and *separate* allele-support diagnostic; Fig 4 registered Q4 occupancy interaction(s) shown as separate estimands. Do not silently change the source-pinned four main figure assignments or manuscript 5,000-word/150-abstract ceiling without an explicit package review. This document is an editorial candidate, **not a merged paper/figure change**.

**If linking to observational Chapter 1:** Its finding concerns *observed island floras*—repeated breeding functions need not correspond to uniform floral morphology. Chapter 2 supplies a **plausible, model-bounded mechanism for nonparallel inherited responses**, not a fitted causal explanation of the actual natural island distributions. A poster must not print “mechanism generalized to natural islands” from Q1's 8×8 synthetic founder/visitor grid.

## Not established and priority gates

- Not first stochastic/individual-based selfing model (Lepers 2014 Appendix B and other precedents).
- Not an isolated variance-only switch for order; allelic half-width also changes the support and reachability relative to the prespecified 0.05 response.
- Not an exact finite stochastic expectation vs deterministic-law drift decomposition.
- Not a theorem that all predicted island evolution is random: initial ecological/genetic conditions provide information but do not uniquely fix six response classes.
- Not “49% upper bound on predictive accuracy”: historical 1,117/2,304=48.48% is an **in-sample** 18-cell six-class majority lookup, not a new-founder prospective ceiling.
- Not universal environmental convergence or far-side I preservation (new direct-A-cost setting inconclusive; far-side signs contingent).
- Not naturally evolved A-first survival rescue or direct Q1→Q3→Q4 causal mediation.
- Not independent island-systems replication, stationarity at 1,000 model steps, or cross-host deposited raw evidence.

**Next discriminating studies:** independently manipulate genetically available I/A support while keeping initial expression and visitor histories matched, separately vary pollen/demographic source operators to isolate the 3/32 density gap, and test Q4 on common matched diploid states with future A permission frozen (if identifying historical genetic legacy is the goal). Predefine negative outcomes and do not tune old readouts. The current one-paper manuscript can already report its registered model-conditional claims without pretending these studies are complete.
