## Decision

for the preregistered primary cell: delayed selfing, assurance cost 0.5,
mutation probability 0.01.

The frozen paper-level statement is:

> Under sustained visitor-replenishment limitation, realized reproductive

## Establishment criteria

### 1. Central claim frozen before confirmation — PASS

The confirmatory design
`data/design/chapter2_1005_confirmatory_replication_20261006.json`
was frozen before the new-history outcome was generated. It fixed the primary
cell, visitor-history and demographic seeds, primary 0.05 threshold, 20-update
persistence rule, five-update tie window, bootstrap seeds, success criteria,
admissibility criteria and no-retuning rule.

### 2. New-history confirmation of the 51/64 sequence — PASS

The frozen replication used 64 new visitor histories
26100601–26100664 and eight new nested demographic repeats
26101601–26101608; discovery histories 76001–76064 and repeats 7101–7108 were
not reused.

Primary result:

- assurance first: 51/64;
- near-simultaneous: 13/64;
- assurance-first proportion: 0.796875;
- 95% visitor-history bootstrap: 0.6875–0.890625.

The frozen rule required proportion > 0.50 and lower 95% bound > 0.50. Both
conditions passed.

### 3. Threshold sensitivity — PASS within the declared primary setting

For delayed selfing, assurance cost 0.5, mutation 0.01:

- threshold 0.025: 48/64 assurance-first, bootstrap 0.640625–0.84453125;
- threshold 0.05: 51/64, bootstrap 0.6875–0.890625;
- threshold 0.10: 59/64, bootstrap 0.84375–0.984375.

The assurance-first majority therefore persists across all three thresholds
declared before the confirmatory readout.

This does not imply that the numerical thresholds are biologically equivalent
or that the first infinitesimal response has been identified.

### 4. Reproductive-setting scope — BOUNDED, explicitly reported

All four setting-by-mutation sequence cells are retained. The primary sequence
is not universal.

At threshold 0.05:

- delayed/costly, mutation 0.01: 51/64 assurance-first, confirmed;
- prior selfing, mutation 0.01: 30/64 assurance-first, 95% bootstrap
  0.34375–0.59375, not a confirmed majority.

Therefore the paper-level sequence claim is restricted to the delayed-selfing,
assurance-cost 0.5, positive-mutation primary cell. Other cells cannot rescue or
overturn the primary adjudication.

### 5. Separate causal-necessity confirmation — PASS

A separate preregistered fixed-assurance campaign held assurance capacity at
0.5 and used the new visitor histories/repeats.

Primary delayed/costly positive-mutation result:

- far investment change: -0.306021
  [95% bootstrap -0.318054, -0.294111];
- far-minus-near investment: -0.435389
  [-0.453330, -0.417191];
- occupancy: 1.0 near and far;
- eligible histories: 64/64.

Both frozen negative-effect criteria passed. Thus assurance **evolution** is not
necessary for investment decline under this intervention. Fixed assurance is
not absence of realized selfing and does not identify a full mediation fraction.

### 6. Numerical unresolved branch separated — PASS

The high-resolution positive-mutation deterministic/PDE comparison remains
closed unresolved. It is not used in the primary biological claim or its
confirmation.

The independent ecological denominator is 64 visitor histories. The eight
demographic repeats are nested and never counted as 512 independent ecological
replicates.

### 7. Literature and overlapping manuscript routes — PASS

The literature-position audit explicitly treats selfing-first sequence,
pollinator-driven joint floral/mating-system evolution, and pollen-limitation
measurement caveats as precedented.

The paper's novelty is therefore the **separation of observed temporal order
from causal necessity by a matched intervention under one explicit
island-replenishment process**, not first discovery of a selfing syndrome.

The former Evolution Letters repeatability manuscript is retired as a standalone
submission route. Its distinct repeatability results may be used only as
Supporting Information or future work with overlap disclosure. The historical
Oikos submission remains a frozen snapshot.
