# Chapter 2 journal route — 2026-10-06

## Decision

**Primary target: Evolution Letters.**

**Second target: The American Naturalist.**

**Conservative fallback: Oikos.**

**Ecology Letters is a stretch target, not the default submission route.**

This routing is based on the now-confirmed 2026-10-05 process result, not on the
retired repeatability manuscript.

## Why Evolution Letters is first

The paper now has one compact evolutionary inference rather than a broad Model 3
catalogue:

> In the delayed-selfing, assurance-cost 0.5, positive-mutation setting,
> reproductive assurance can change before floral investment declines, yet
> assurance evolution is not required for that investment decline.

The contribution is not that selfing can precede floral reduction. That sequence
has direct precedent. The advance is the intervention-based separation of
**temporal precedence from causal necessity** under one explicit reproductive
process, followed by independent preregistered confirmation on new visitor
histories.

The manuscript can be written as a concise evolutionary Letter with four
biological steps:

1. visitor replenishment limitation changes the reproductive return to
   attraction before plant evolution;
2. reproductive assurance changes first in the declared primary setting;
3. blocking assurance evolution does not block investment decline;
4. therefore the observed sequence does not identify a necessary serial
   selfing-mediated pathway.

The setting-specific failure under prior selfing is an asset, not a defect: it
sets the domain of the result and prevents a universal selfing-syndrome claim.

## Claim language

Use:

> **Temporal precedence is not causal necessity.**

and:

> **Assurance evolution is not required for investment decline under the
> declared delayed-selfing/costly conditions.**

Do not use:

- "assurance is unnecessary";
- "selfing does not matter";
- "selfing-first is universal";
- "pollinator isolation always causes floral reduction";
- "the model reproduces a named island";
- "pollen limitation is not fitness" as a novelty claim.

The fixed intervention retains assurance capacity at 0.5. It tests the necessity
of **assurance evolution**, not the absence of assurance or realized selfing.

## Why not lead with Ecology Letters

The result is conceptually sharp and independently confirmed, but the external
validation is still model-level rather than a new natural-system test. Ecology
Letters is therefore a reasonable ambitious transfer after a strong editorial
signal, but it should not distort the manuscript into a broader ecology claim
that the evidence does not support.

## Why The American Naturalist is the strongest second route

If Evolution Letters judges the biological scope too narrow for a Letter, the
paper's strongest alternative identity is conceptual/theoretical evolutionary
ecology: a general warning that sequence and causal necessity must be separated
in multivariate evolutionary responses. The American Naturalist is better suited
than a narrower pollination journal to that conceptual framing.

## Why Oikos remains the conservative route

Oikos directly welcomes theoretical work on ecological mechanisms and processes.
The paper already has a viable Oikos-shaped version, so it remains the lowest-risk
route if the higher conceptual journals reject without review.
