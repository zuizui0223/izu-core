## The realized process — from reproductive returns to allele-copy loss

The paper's mechanistic question is **not** simply why a density calculation and ABM disagree. It is: *Given an allele needed for a specified floral response, what happens to its copies between expected reproductive output and the next finite cohort, and when does that alter which future responses remain genetically possible?*

1. **Visitor-dependent reproductive parent weighting.** Native maternal outcross pollen receipt, paternal export and viable selfed offspring form the original parent-pair contribution matrix `W = O + diag(S)`, where rows are fathers, columns are mothers, and `S` is viable selfing. Each parent `i` has required low-I allele-copy fraction `x_i`. With `T = sum(W)`, the conditional expected offspring frequency is `e = Σ_i x_i (row_i(W) + col_i(W)) / (2T)`; the unweighted current copy frequency is `p`. A positive `e−p` describes **conditional genotype-weighted expected frequency change** at this archived state, **not** a universal selection coefficient.
2. **Finite parental and Mendelian draws.** Native reproduction samples finite parent pairs and their homologs with replacement. For one child, the conditional probability that neither inherited investment allele is in the needed set is `q0 = (1−x)^T W (1−x) / T`, so the zero-copy probability among `n` retained children under the archived independent sampling law is `q0^n`. Even when `e−p>0`, `q0^n` can be substantial if only a few needed allele copies remain.
3. **Observed bottleneck at the allele level.** In the **315 outcome-selected first-loss transitions** with `e−p>0`, the immediately preceding **48 diploid parents** retained a **median of 2 needed copies out of 96 homologs**, and the median archived `q0^48` was **0.3260053119547402**. These figures are a **new read-only descriptive quantile of the already source-verified ALL_CASES.csv**, not a new simulation, probability of extinction, prospective selection trial or population-wide hazard. Every one of these 315 actually produced **zero needed copies** in the child census. The median describes *the selected source states*, not an estimated empirical loss frequency. In these same 315 original transition records, the signed viable-selfed-offspring component `self_response` was positive in **305**, while maternal outcross `female_response` was negative in **234** and paternal outcross `paternal_response` in **266**; all three patterns coincided in **234**. These expected-frequency components are an exact decomposition of parental-genome weighting **under the observed source state**. They do **not** separately manipulate selfing or certify that selfing causally preserved the allele.
4. **Genetic reachability closes.** In the no-I-mutation/no-immigration source law, after the last needed allele disappears and the minimum remaining investment allele exceeds `I0−0.05`, no new future plant can express a 0.05 founder-relative I decrease. This can happen **before complete fixation**, as independently recorded in **703 of the original 2,304 histories**; one of the 703 had already realized the earlier response. Losing a future *specified* threshold is narrower than proving that investment can never decrease.

This is a concrete **parental returns → finite transmission → loss of accessible alleles → restricted evolutionary response** mechanism. It demonstrates a source-specific route by which realized trajectories depart from return-based predictions, without identifying *why each native history* got its weights or allele counts, unique female/paternal/selfing mediation, pure drift share or effects on Q4 persistence. The prospective 128-history bridge and these retrospective 2,304 originals are **different experiments**.

### Why 703 histories and 315 transitions must not be combined

The two retrospective results reuse the **same 2,304 original diploid histories**. The first checks whether a genotype-support threshold becomes impossible **while allelic variation is still present**. The second audits the **single first-exclusion transition** for the **1,238 histories that experienced it**, decomposing conditional reproductive parentage versus realized inherited copies. They answer **different questions**; 315/1,238 is a **selected-transition fraction**, *not* the incidence of positive selection followed by allele loss across independent natural populations.

The [independent support/ancestry audit](../data/results/directional_access_20261013/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.json) checked **2,315,520 census descriptors** and **44,236,800 parental homologs**; the [independent last-loss audit](../data/results/low_allele_loss_20261014/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.json) checked **23,522 scalar expressions and 10,994,304 gamete outcomes**. Both retained **zero new biological histories**. The unified-review builder already includes the original readouts, case data and their independent admission receipts; this note points to them and changes no original bytes.

## Claim hierarchy in the paper

- **Q1 selection:** At fixed plant state, imposed visitor limitation changes maternal/paternal returns to attraction. The four-setting assurance/evolving contrast remains the **registered primary**.
- **Q2 order:** A can meet the specified sustained-response criterion before I, without being a *necessary* evolutionary prerequisite. The exact A-first label depends on threshold, window, allele access and mating mode.
- **Q3 realization (this edit):** Selection direction **≠** maintained allelic support **≠** outcome of finite birth/genetic transmission. The 128-history bridge tests conditional density vs finite realization and the two sensitivity interventions; the **separate** 2,304-history source pedigree documents a concrete support-loss route.
- **Q4 persistence:** Later occupancy belongs to separately registered preparation and expression experiments. Neither the Q3 finite/density contrast nor 315 selected transition records establishes evolved-order survival mediation.

**Do not write:** “ABM changes evolutionary investment by exactly one third because of drift”; “visitor randomness and plant randomness explain fixed percentages”; “315 favorable-loss events are population-level selection failures”; “allelic fixation is necessary for loss of response”; “all forms of investment decline become impossible”; “stochasticity alone explains a particular island phenotype”; or “the density-only model cannot represent any extinction.” The inbreeding-depression 0.75 **later sensitivity** produced sub-individual deterministic density mass after finite extinction, but [the separate population-scale audit](CHAPTER2_DENSITY_CLOSURE_AUDIT_20261003.md) confirms this issue does **not** apply to the focal 0.50 depression / 200-update comparison.

**Publication scope:** No new selection coefficient, significance test, bootstrap, simulation run, interval, original result modification, field-island calibration or DOI claim. The original source-ranked Q1–Q4 verdicts and ecological limitations remain unchanged.
