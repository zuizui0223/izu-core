# Chapter 2 closure — world saturation to Izu continuity

Updated: 2026-09-06

## Closure decision

Chapter 2 closes after four tasks are complete:

1. define a conditional response geometry and its proximal mechanism;
2. show that empirical island systems require a response vocabulary broader than one syndrome;
3. demonstrate, with a geography-first stopping rule, that further breadth stops adding materially new mechanism states while the transition-measurement bottleneck persists;
4. justify Izu as the depth system by cross-layer measurement continuity and use it to resolve the contemporary functional-realization half without claiming historical causation.

The chapter does not remain open merely because the full historical source-state → partner transition → realized community → effectiveness/dependency → mature reproductive-response contract is not yet measured in one system. Identifying that missing contract after breadth saturation is itself the empirical identifiability result.

## Why Izu follows rather than precedes world breadth

Izu is not selected because it is in Japan, close to the researcher, logistically convenient or representative of all islands. It is selected only after world expansion reaches the declared stopping condition.

The focal criterion is measurement continuity: one regional series should connect as many of the missing mechanism layers as possible while retaining alternative historical explanations and the ability to falsify the preferred mechanism.

Izu satisfies that criterion because it combines:

- historical *Campanula microdonta* floral, mating-system and autonomous-reproduction response channels;
- explicit population-history alternatives;
- repeated mainland/Oshima/post-Oshima contemporary networks;
- source-native numeric pollinator functional traits;
- present corrected matching and pollen-response measurements;
- negative as well as positive model-facing results;
- an independently measured multichannel focal phenotype reserved for Chapter 3;
- a prospective tagged-population route to visitor effectiveness and reproductive dependency.

## Chapter 2 / Chapter 3 boundary

Chapter 2 owns the mechanism, world saturation/identifiability result, Izu selection logic and contemporary functional resolution.

Chapter 3 owns the direct *C. microdonta* phenotype and any new focal effectiveness/dependency measurements. The existing `shimahotarubukuro` phenotype is therefore downstream empirical realization, not retrospective validation of Chapter 2 and not evidence of historical *Bombus* causation.

## Final claim

> A common interaction reorganization need not produce one island response because response direction is relational to starting state and realized community. World island evidence supports the need for multiple response states but remains transition-poor even after geography-first saturation. Izu is the appropriate depth system because it maximizes measurement continuity and falsification capacity across that bottleneck, resolving present functional organization while leaving historical transition causation explicitly open for direct future measurement.
