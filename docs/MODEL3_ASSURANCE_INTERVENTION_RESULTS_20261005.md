# Investment reduction does not require evolving selfing capacity in this intervention

All8,192 declared cases completed. The full integrity audit checked source hashes, task identities and fixed-capacity states; all2,048 zero-mutation matched pairs were exactly identical. Independent row-wise reconstruction verified all8,192 source arrays and84 estimates, including bootstrap intervals, eligible replicates and occupancy.

The intervention starts every plant at capacity0.5 with no capacity standing variation; investment and matching retain their founder variation. Fixed and evolving modes differ in whether capacity can mutate and respond. This is a distinct cohort from the main standing-variation experiment. A fixed capacity is not a fixed realized selfing fraction or the absence of selfing.

The supported claim is that increased selfing capacity is not necessary for investment reduction under these declared conditions. It is not proof of independence from realized selfing, a universal mediation fraction or a direct explanation of empirical flower colour.

## Independent fixed-assurance confirmation — 2026-10-06

The fixed-assurance necessity result was independently repeated under
the same prospectively frozen new-history design. Assurance capacity remained
fixed at 0.5; 64 new visitor histories and eight new demographic repeats were
used. Both near and far arms retained 100% terminal occupancy and all 64
histories were eligible.

In the primary delayed-selfing/costly cell, far investment change was
**−0.306021** with 95% visitor-history bootstrap
**[−0.318054, −0.294111]**. The paired far-minus-near investment effect was
**−0.435389 [−0.453330, −0.417191]**. Both means and both upper interval bounds
were below zero, so the frozen necessity criterion passed.

The prior-selfing secondary cell also retained negative responses:
far change **−0.331600 [−0.341782, −0.320989]** and far-minus-near
**−0.309376 [−0.324630, −0.294177]**.

This confirms that assurance-capacity evolution is not required for investment
decline under the declared intervention. It does not imply absence of realized
selfing and is not a complete mediation analysis.
