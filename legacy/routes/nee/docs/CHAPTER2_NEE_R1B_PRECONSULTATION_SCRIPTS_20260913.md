These scripts are deliberately written before focal reproductive outcomes. The same planned-action list is shown to authorities regardless of whether any candidate site is expected to support or falsify H1–H5.

## Common study-action description

The proposed future pilot would use tagged flowering plants within predeclared site × time blocks. Potential actions are:

1. repeated visual/camera observation of tagged flowering plants;
2. temporary placement of cameras or observation equipment near flowers;
3. temporary flower bagging for autonomous-pollination controls and single-visit controls;
4. single-visit pollen-deposition measurements using receptive flowers/stigmas with no-visit controls;
5. hand supplemental outcross pollination of designated flowers;
6. open-pollinated control flowers;
7. repeated measurement of floral geometry before reproductive outcome;
8. follow-up of treated flowers to mature fruit/seed, with only the minimum collection explicitly approved;
9. removal of all temporary equipment and materials after the declared block.

The inquiry is only to determine required permissions, site management, and whether these actions are feasible. No candidate is considered admitted until the R1b audit passes.

## Outcome-blind update rule after replies

Replies may update only the R1b feasibility fields supported by the reply:

- `access_status`;
- `permit_status`;
- exact `site_name` / `population_site_id`;
- `phenology_status` if an authoritative current-season source directly supports it;
- treatment/SVD feasibility only when the authority or field reconnaissance actually addresses the proposed operation;
- outcome-blind exclusion reason if a site is legally, logistically, phenologically, or structurally infeasible.

A positive administrative reply does **not** set H1–H5 to supported and does not count toward R2/R3. A negative reply excludes or keeps a site pending without changing the scientific prediction.
