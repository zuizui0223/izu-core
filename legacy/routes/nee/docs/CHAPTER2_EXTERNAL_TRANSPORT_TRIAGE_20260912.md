# Chapter 2 external transport triage — 2026-09-12

Status: **pre-Izu external evidence triage for the NEE promotion lane**.

This document asks a narrower question than the frozen 25-system Chapter 2 audit:

> Which already-audited natural systems can test one of the new pre-data predictions P1-P4 without being misrepresented as a complete Chapter 2 validation?

The source of the predictions is `data/design/chapter2_nee_predata_promotion_lock_20260912.json`.

## Decision first

No currently admitted external system closes the full natural chain

```text
pre-outcome plant state
x realized effective community
-> independent visitor-effectiveness measure
-> reproductive dependency
-> mature reproductive endpoint
```

at one outcome-independent linked unit.

Therefore external public data can play only two legitimate pre-Izu roles:

1. **architecture transport** — show that plant-level visitor exposure and reproductive-dependency measurements can coexist and be linked without outcome-driven reconstruction;
2. **component transport** — challenge one source prediction such as visitor-specific effectiveness or exposure/dependency separation.

They cannot replace the prospective Izu same-block design.

## Candidate ranking

### 1. Seychelles / *Thespesia populnea* — strongest individual-level architecture prototype

Current source state:

- exact individual census/breeding linkage after identifier normalization;
- 12 overlapping plants;
- 8 plants with census exposure plus both Auto and Xenogamy treatments;
- direct source-scale dependency contrast is strong at the population level;
- broad visitor-group exposure metrics are available for the linked eight plants.

What it can test:

- **P2-lite:** whether an outcome-independent plant-level exposure representation can be linked to a direct reproductive-dependency contrast;
- the feasibility of preserving plant identity across visitor observations and breeding treatments.

What it cannot test cleanly:

- harmonized effective-community composition;
- a pre-outcome plant functional-state vector comparable to Izu;
- a same-plant single-visit effectiveness bridge;
- exposure-aggregation/determinant-order prediction.

Current conclusion: retain as an **external architecture prototype**, not a confirmatory transport replicate.

### 2. California Channel Islands / *Nicotiana glauca* — strongest effectiveness/dependency component bridge

Current source state:

- island/mainland floral response and visitor composition;
- pollen removal/stigma deposition;
- open versus supplemental hand-cross pollen limitation;
- autonomous versus hand-self reproduction;
- single-visit hummingbird pollen deposition/removal with visitor-specific effectiveness differences;
- overlapping named sites and partly overlapping years across source studies.

What it can test:

- **P3 component transport:** visitor identity can map to an independent per-visit effectiveness difference;
- **P4 component transport:** reproductive dependency/pollen limitation can be separated from simple visitation;
- a strong alternative-mechanism boundary: current island service did not simply explain stronger selfing capacity.

Current blockers:

- stable lawful source bytes for the declared papers were not recovered through the frozen routes;
- component measurements are not established on the same tagged plants and same field block;
- reproductive-dependency and single-visit-effectiveness measurements are not one exact outcome-independent chain.

Current conclusion: highest-value external component system if provenance can be lawfully reopened, but **not a full P2-P4 replicate**.

### 3. Puerto Rico / Mona / *Guaiacum sanctum* — strongest population-level exposure/dependency separation

Current source state:

- very strong visitor-assemblage contrast between Guánica and Mona;
- same populations received a blocked breeding-system experiment;
- open/self/outcross treatment outcomes and source-reported breeding index are available;
- pollinator assemblage differs strongly while the breeding-system index remains similar.

What it can test:

- **P1/P4 architecture boundary:** pollinator exposure and reproductive dependency are distinct ecological axes and must not be collapsed;
- a population-level natural example of why visitor assemblage is not itself dependency.

What it cannot test:

- exact same-tree visitor/breeding linkage;
- single-visit effectiveness;
- pre-outcome plant-state x effective-community interaction;
- P5 exposure-aggregation prediction.

Current conclusion: strong conceptual transport, not a field validation of the Chapter 2 mechanism.

## Other audited systems

The frozen 25-entry audit remains authoritative for the rest of the source universe. It found zero complete outcome-independent Chapter 2 contracts. Systems with direct partner turnover/network shifts remain useful reality boundaries, but absence of the full chain must not be converted into biological support or failure of P1-P5.

## External-data action before Izu

Only two external actions currently have high information value:

1. **Seychelles:** freeze a common, outcome-independent exposure representation before any new cross-lineage comparison is calculated; do not invent an FDQ analogue after seeing dependency outcomes.
2. **Nicotiana:** reopen source provenance only if a genuinely new lawful stable artifact becomes available; do not retry already-frozen failed PDF routes.

Do not expand broad literature hunting merely to increase the number of partial systems. The current source audit already establishes the key gap: no existing dataset supplies the full linked natural contract.

## Stage-1 implication

The lack of a complete external natural contract is not a weakness to hide. It motivates the prospective design:

```text
existing literature
    -> components measured separately
    -> no complete outcome-independent same-unit chain

prospective Izu design
    -> pre-outcome state
    -> realized effective community
    -> effectiveness
    -> dependency
    -> mature seed
```

The Registered Report should therefore present external systems as prior empirical constraints and component precedents, not as pilot confirmation of the primary field hypothesis.

## Stop rule

> Public-data transport may test frozen components, but it cannot be used to tune the Izu state vector, effective-community definition, aggregation scale or endpoint after outcome inspection.
