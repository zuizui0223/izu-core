# Cabrera 2022–2023 source gate for ABM v8

This gate is source/schema admission only.

Candidate: Serra-Marin et al. 2025, Methods in Ecology and Evolution, DOI 10.1111/2041-210X.70165; public dataset `Plant_pollinator interactions Cabrera Island 2022-2023`, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17130777 / Digital.CSIC handle 10261/420466.

Published sampling scope: Cabrera Island, six sites, 13 field campaigns across 2022–2023, with direct observations and automatic camera recordings.

Before any ABM v8 target is inspected, this branch only verifies public bytes, checksums, exact CSV headers, structural context fields, and whether raw repeated plant–pollinator records are present. It does not calculate interaction diversity, niche overlap, pair support, plant support, pollinator support, empirical ranges, or v8 predictive adequacy.

Menorca and Giannutri are consumed falsification systems and cannot confirm v8. Cabrera method/site/campaign subsets may not be chosen after seeing network outcomes.
