# v8/v9 independent prospective source audit — 2026-08-21

## Decision

**Source GO: Thousand Island Lake (Ren, Si & Ding 2022) is the next executable candidate.** The author deposit exposes quantitative plant×pollinator pair counts for 42 spatial networks and three temporal totals. It is independent of Menorca, Giannutri, and Cabrera, but is a fragmented land-bridge landscape rather than an oceanic archipelago. **Design is not yet GO:** the spatial and temporal files are separate marginals, not demonstrated site×year networks, and sampling exposure plus plant-opportunity handling remain unfrozen. No target, network metric, empirical range, or v9 fit was calculated in this audit.

## Independence and claim ceiling

- Thousand Island Lake is a new empirical system for v9 relative to Menorca/Giannutri/Cabrera and has source-native quantitative pair support. Its land-bridge fragmentation context cannot be reported as an oceanic-island replication.
- The 42-column spatial matrix aggregates over time, while the three-column temporal matrix aggregates over space. They do **not** license 42 × 3 site-year contexts or a temporal-within-site validation.
- Pair codes and network IDs are structural evidence only. They do not yet establish observation exposure, flowering opportunity, true structural zeros, or which v8/v9 estimand is admissible.

Freeze a reconstruction/design contract before reading any outcome: use the 42 spatial networks only unless a source-native site×year key is demonstrated; decode `PO…_PL…`; map all 42 `B…`/`S…` IDs; verify count semantics and observation exposure; and decide whether plant opportunity is observable independently of realized pairs.
3. If independent plant opportunity is absent, cap the prospective test at v8 pair support (or an explicitly reduced v9 prediction) rather than inferring opportunity from visited plants. Only after this contract passes may target code be written.
