# Model 3 Chapter 2 bridge — prospective interpretation rules

Frozen after production design/workflow launch but before production outcomes were inspected.
Date: 2026-09-27

## Purpose

The 24,576-case bridge campaign answers two original Chapter 2 controls inside Model 3. No direction, mixed fraction, rank or agreement with legacy Model 2 is a success criterion. A null, opposite or adverse result is a valid scientific answer.

## Primary endpoint

`far minus near` difference in terminal-minus-initial inherited floral investment, separately for:

- natural visitor histories;
- response-blind annual realized-richness-matched histories;
- pooled eight-history visitor environments with count-scaled activity normalization;
- larger plant capacity with the same visitor history.

Each is evaluated for finite ABM and deterministic genotype-density endpoints.

Thresholds are fixed at `epsilon = 0, 0.01, 0.05`. Mixed means at least one starting population above `+epsilon` and one below `-epsilon` within the same independent history after averaging the eight demographic repeats. Extinction/undefined trait endpoints remain undefined, not zero.

## Question 1 — does dynamic richness matching remove heterogeneous isolation response?

Interpret the richness-matched arm regardless of direction:

- **mixed remains in deterministic density:** visitor composition/state dependence can preserve heterogeneous inherited isolation response after annual count matching without plant demographic sampling;
- **mixed remains only in finite ABM:** annual count matching does not eliminate realized heterogeneity, but finite plant demography is required for the observed mixed labels under this intervention;
- **mixed disappears in both:** the legacy Model 2 richness-controlled branching does not transport to the mechanistic inherited-investment endpoint under this Model 3 assembly intervention;
- **precision/eligibility fails:** report the gate as unresolved; do not use legacy Model 2 agreement to rescue it.

Annual thinning changes identity persistence as well as count, so none of these outcomes is called a pure species-richness causal effect.

## Question 2 — finite visitor environment versus finite plant population

The pooled-visitor and large-capacity arms are orthogonal diagnostics, not equivalent manipulations.

- **visitor pooling reduces heterogeneity while larger plant capacity does not:** environmental/community sampling is the stronger modifier in this design;
- **larger plant capacity reduces heterogeneity while visitor pooling does not:** finite plant demography is the stronger modifier;
- **both reduce heterogeneity:** both environmental sampling and finite plant demography contribute;
- **neither reduces heterogeneity:** systematic composition/history/state dependence remains after both reductions;
- **effects differ between ABM and density or are threshold-sensitive:** report the interaction/instability rather than force a single finite-community label.

Pooling visitor histories changes environmental averaging and functional composition under a nonlinear reproductive operator. It is not interpreted as island number, lifespan or a pure richness manipulation.

## Precision and stability

The predeclared design targets remain authoritative:

- history-cluster bootstrap mean half-width target: `<= 0.025`;
- Hoeffding mixed-fraction half-width target: `<= 0.125`;
- report `epsilon = 0, 0.01, 0.05` without choosing the most favorable threshold;
- report all eight repeat-specific labels plus first-four versus last-four average labels;
- if trait support is undefined because either paired arm is extinct, exclude the trait sign history but retain unconditional occupancy.

Failure of a precision target does not trigger more seeds. It limits the conclusion.

## Model 2 retirement decision

Model 2 is already unnecessary as a **separate biological mechanism**. Its remaining active role is two control questions: dynamic realized-richness matching and finite visitor-environment averaging.

After the prospective Model 3 bridge is complete:

- if both questions are evaluable, Model 2 exact-richness and synthetic-`k` analyses move fully to historical/SI comparison regardless of whether Model 3 agrees with their old direction;
- if either question is not evaluable because of support or precision failure, that specific legacy control remains a benchmark and the corresponding Model 3 question stays explicitly unresolved;
- heuristic response-rule and S/C/I ranking results remain legacy/SI in all cases.

Thus Model 2 retirement is determined by whether Model 3 directly answers the old controls, not by whether it reproduces the preferred old result.

## Chapter 1 bridge consequence

Only after this gate can Chapter 2 state how much of Chapter 1's non-uniform isolation response is attributable, within Model 3, to:

1. state-dependent functional matching;
2. realized visitor number/composition under isolation-driven assembly;
3. finite visitor-environment sampling;
4. finite plant demography;
5. reproductive assurance, connectivity and history.

Natural Chapter 1 regional patterns are never assigned to individual simulation cells.
