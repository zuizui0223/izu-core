# Why Izu is the focal depth system

Updated: 2026-09-06

## Decision

Izu is not the focal system because it is geographically close, easy to visit, representative of islands in general, or the strongest positive match to the synthetic model.

The world-confrontation programme now provides a stronger rationale. Geography-first expansion reached the declared large-island saturation rule and the required small-island supplement still failed to close the historical transition contract. The remaining information gap is therefore specific: the field lacks systems that link a **pre-transition plant/source state**, a **direct partner loss or arrival history**, the **realized functional community**, and a **comparable downstream plant response** on one matched unit.

After that diagnosis, the correct focal-system criterion is **measurement continuity across the missing chain**.

Izu is used for depth because one regional island series already links more of that chain than the broad comparison can link jointly, while still preserving explicit negative results and alternative explanations.

## The continuity stack

### 1. Historical focal-lineage response exists

Source-locked *Campanula microdonta* records distinguish several response channels across the island series:

- flower size declines along the ordered island scaffold;
- multilocus outcrossing declines along the same broad ordering;
- autonomous reproductive capacity changes much more sharply between Oshima and Toshima/post-Oshima populations.

These channels do not collapse into one response shape. Population-genetic work also makes colonisation/demographic history an explicit alternative explanation for the ordered components. Izu therefore supplies historical leverage **and** a built-in competing explanation rather than a one-story positive control.

Sources:

- `data/inoue_literature_island_traits.csv`
- `docs/CAMPANULA_GENETIC_HISTORY_CONSTRAINTS.md`

### 2. The same regional series has repeated contemporary interaction networks

The Hiraiwa–Ushimaru source programme contains:

- three mainland coastal sites;
- Oshima as one bridge-state site;
- Niijima, Kozu, Miyake and Hachijo as post-Oshima sites;
- five repeated seasonal network snapshots per site.

This gives contemporary functional variation inside one regional system rather than requiring a cross-continent comparison to supply every mechanism layer.

Source: `data/design/hiraiwa_ushimaru_boundary_identifiability.json`.

### 3. Pollinator functional traits are source-native and densely recovered

Numeric proboscis values are safely recovered for 202/209 current named pollinator taxa under exact or whitespace-only matching. The remaining site-specific and plant-specific limitations are explicit.

This matters because the key Chapter 2 mechanism is relational: a plant state must be evaluated against a realized functional community. Izu can represent that community quantitatively without substituting family means or guild midpoints.

Source: `data/design/izu_pollinator_proboscis_recovery_status.json`.

### 4. The contemporary chain contains both support and failure

Within Izu:

- FDQ → corrected trait matching is positive and leave-one-island sign robust;
- matching → pollen receipt is positive on average but not leave-one-island sign stable;
- eight shared targets with lower corrected matching divide into shorter, longer and unchanged floral responses and into higher and lower pollen responses;
- the older signed-position projection does **not** explain null-corrected matching;
- the Oshima-source sensitivity is unsupported.

Thus Izu is not useful because every result agrees. It is useful because different causal stories can be separated within one source-linked system.

## Chapter 3 supplies the independent phenotypic endpoint

The focal phenotype is already measured independently in `zuizui0223/shimahotarubukuro`.

At commit `5c0494bdd0a37f0dae31f442afddcd6982c5e7fa`, Chapter 3 retains 218 corollas from 125 plants across five Izu islands and concludes that *C. microdonta* divergence is best described as a strong coordinated size/investment trajectory plus selected departures from common allometric scaling in access, reproductive-interface and visual-investment channels.

That result belongs to Chapter 3. It is **not** evidence used to tune Chapter 2, select a synthetic regime, prove pollinator selection or infer historical *Bombus* causation.

Its relevance to Chapter 2 is structural: the same focal lineage has a directly measured multichannel endpoint whose complexity is exactly the kind of response architecture for which a one-direction island syndrome is inadequate.

## What remains missing

The remaining empirical bridge is narrower than before the world audit:

```text
historical partner transition
    + matched pre-transition plant/source state
        -> realized visitor identity and functional exposure
            -> single-visit pollen deposition / effective service
                -> controlled reproductive dependency
                    -> mature fruit / seed
                        -> phenotype as a downstream endpoint, not a cause label
```

The current field schema is already built to collect the contemporary middle of this chain in tagged populations. Historical identification still needs either a true temporal partner-regime transition with matched pre/post response or independent replication of the bridge-state exposure.

## Why this is not a convenience choice

Scientific selection would be weaker if the rationale were simply that Izu is nearby or logistically feasible. The actual rationale is the opposite: **Izu remains focal even though some of its strongest model-facing analyses fail or weaken.**

That makes Izu a high-value resolution system because it can adjudicate among mechanisms rather than merely illustrate one.

The selection rule is therefore:

> **After world breadth saturates, move to the system that maximizes linked measurement of the unresolved mechanism while retaining independent downstream phenotype, alternative historical explanations and falsification capacity.**

Under the current programme, that system is Izu.

## Chapter boundary

Chapter 2 closes with:

1. a conditional response geometry;
2. geography-first evidence that the empirical response vocabulary has saturated while the transition-measurement bottleneck persists;
3. a precise statement of the missing longitudinal coordinates;
4. Izu identified as the continuity system that resolves the contemporary half without claiming historical causation.

Chapter 3 owns:

- the directly measured *C. microdonta* phenotype;
- its coordinated-versus-residual allometric structure;
- any new focal effectiveness/dependency measurements added to close the remaining empirical bridge.

The chapter sequence is therefore breadth → mechanism → identifiability → continuity-system depth → direct phenotypic realization, not global survey → convenient local case study.
