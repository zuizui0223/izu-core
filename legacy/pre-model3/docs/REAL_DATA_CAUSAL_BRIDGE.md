# Real-data causal bridge

## Current result

The repository now separates three empirical statements that were previously easy to blur together.

1. In the current Izu network archive, FDQ remains positively associated with corrected trait matching in the post-Oshima islands and the leave-one-island direction remains positive.
2. The downstream trait-matching -> pollen-receipt point estimate is also positive, but the site-season-clustered interval includes zero and the island-only result is network-state-sensitive.
3. No exact Izu population currently has the linked direct reproductive-dependency panel required by Issue #91.

The real-data chain therefore stops at a conditional pollen-function link. It does not become an empirical dependency x FDQ model.

## External direct endpoints already present in izu-core

Two source-native island experiments provide directly observed reproductive-assurance/dependence contrasts on their own scales:

- *Malva arborea*, Cabrera (Balearic Islands): autogamy/open weighted fruit-set ratio = 0.886685, giving a within-source fruit-set shortfall of 0.113315.
- *Lotus maculatus*, Tenerife (Canary Islands): bagged/control weighted fruit-set ratio = 0.021739, giving a within-source fruit-set shortfall of 0.978261.

These are not declared to be the same estimand and are not pooled as a common coefficient. Their useful result is narrower but important: real island plant systems occupy very different positions on a direct reproductive-dependence axis, so the wide dependency support used in prospective design work is biologically plausible rather than purely imaginary.

## Taxon-specific function is also real

The Seychelles source-native archive contributes 2,292 raw rows, 489 single-visit exclusion rows and 557 breeding-treatment rows across three plant species. The source-reported overall effectiveness winner differs among the plants (Phelsuma geckos, sunbirds, flying insects). This directly rejects a universal visitor-guild effectiveness ordering.

The 2017 Izu reproductive-sensitivity registry likewise contains three different source-native response modes: long-tongued-pollinator sensitive, resilient to RBLP, and counterdirectional. A universal one-direction reproductive response is therefore not supported.

## Consequence for Issue #91

External real data now do two things:

- demonstrate real low-to-high reproductive-dependence states;
- demonstrate that pollinator effectiveness and reproductive response are taxon/system specific.

They do **not** replace the linked Campanula pilot. The exact Izu population still needs same-unit observation effort, visitor bouts, controlled SVD plus no-visit controls, open/bagged/supplemental reproduction, and fruit/seed linkage before a direct Campanula dependency estimate can enter the causal chain.

No Balearic or Canary magnitude is numerically transported to Izu, and no historical Bombus-loss, dependency x FDQ, or F-versus-E claim is opened by this audit.
