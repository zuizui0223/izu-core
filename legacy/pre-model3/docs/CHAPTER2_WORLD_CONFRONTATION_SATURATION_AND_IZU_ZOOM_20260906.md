# Chapter 2 world-confrontation saturation and final Izu zoom

Updated: 2026-09-06

## Decision

The geography-independent world confrontation has now reached its preregistered stopping condition for the >=20 km2 island master, and the mandatory <20 km2 supplement has been completed. The final Izu mechanistic-resolution zoom is therefore open.

The central result is not that the empirical bottleneck disappeared. It became more sharply localized.

> Across broader geography, current community state, breeding system and plant response are repeatedly measurable. Historical partner loss/arrival is much harder to recover. Small islands finally provide direct transition history in special cases, but the full source-state -> transition -> realized community -> plant-response chain still does not close in one focal system. Izu then resolves the contemporary realization and response-branching half of that chain, while leaving the historical transition half unidentified.

The active manuscript breadth remains **42 research entries / 37 exact geographic labels** and the frozen formal identifiability audit remains **25 research entries / 21 labels, 0/25 full contracts, formal external prediction `not_evaluable`**. The geography-first review is not silently converted into a new validation denominator.

## 1. Why the old 111-target frame was not enough

The completed 111-target systematic-search frame remains a valid record of a literature-driven search programme, but its own audit states that it is not the number of island systems on Earth.

The expansion therefore used a geography source assembled independently of the Chapter 2 literature search: the historical Global Islands v3 / WCMC-USGS-derived artifact in `zuizui0223/island`, containing **4,663 island polygons >=20 km2**.

Raw polygons were not treated as independent ecological systems. `data/design/chapter2_global_island_system_grouping_protocol_20260906.json` freezes the rules for aggregation into archipelago or biogeographic system units and for nested-island de-duplication.

This breaks the circularity in which a geography enters the review universe only because a useful pollination paper had already been found.

## 2. The bottleneck survived geography-first expansion

The first 20 omitted systems already showed the pattern clearly:

- direct plant response: **10/20**;
- direct breeding / assurance state: **9/20**;
- direct realized-community/process contrast: **7/20**;
- direct local filtering: **2/20**;
- direct historical partner loss: **0/20**;
- direct historical partner arrival/replacement: **0/20**;
- full Chapter 2 contracts: **0/20**.

Useful additions included California Channel Islands, Chiloé, Newfoundland and Tierra del Fuego. Importantly, several were falsifiers or alternative-process cases rather than confirmatory examples.

The broader result is therefore not merely “more island examples”. Geography-first searching readily recovers outcomes and contemporary mechanisms while continuing to miss the directional transition coordinates that were among the strongest sign-stable regime variables in the synthetic model.

## 3. Outcome-independent saturation rule

Before later tranches were searched, `data/design/chapter2_world_confrontation_saturation_rule_20260906.json` froze the stopping rule.

An eligible tranche had to contain at least eight new system units selected before literature outcomes were known. Material novelty was defined as any new:

- response state;
- process state;
- falsification / negative-control role;
- direct historical partner-loss measurement;
- direct historical partner-arrival/replacement measurement; or
- full Chapter 2 contract.

Large-island saturation required **two consecutive eligible geography-first tranches with zero material novelty and zero full contracts**.

### Tranche 3 reset the count

The third tranche still added a materially new alternative mechanism in the Gulf of California: island diploidy/hermaphroditism versus mainland polyploidy/unisexuality, together with independent failure of a simple current-pollinator-abundance explanation. Saturation therefore remained open.

### Tranches 4 and 5 closed it

The fourth tranche added useful direct process measurements but no new mechanism/falsification class and no historical turnover coordinate.

The fifth tranche was frozen before source review and again produced:

- material novelty: **0**;
- direct historical partner loss: **0**;
- direct historical partner arrival/replacement: **0**;
- full contracts: **0**.

Tranches 4 and 5 therefore form the required consecutive **zero-novelty 2/2**, and the >=20 km2 geography-first confrontation is saturated under the frozen rule.

This does not mean every island on Earth has been searched. It means additional large-island geography stopped changing the mechanism/response/falsification vocabulary under the declared sampling rule.

## 4. The <20 km2 supplement moves the bottleneck one step

Because the >=20 km2 master excludes many small islands, a separate geography-first supplement was required before Izu.

Eight systems were fixed before pollination-source review:

- Surtsey;
- Saba;
- Tromelin;
- Pitcairn;
- Clipperton;
- Wadjemup / Rottnest;
- Tiritiri Matangi;
- Udo.

The result is the first genuine movement in the historical-transition bottleneck:

- direct historical partner loss: **0/8**;
- direct historical partner arrival/replacement: **1/8** — Tiritiri Matangi;
- partially bounded arrival/colonization history: **Surtsey**;
- full contracts: **0/8**.

### Surtsey — an empty-start natural chronology

Surtsey supplies a rare naturally dated starting condition. The island formed during 1963–1967, *Honckenya peploides* arrived in 1967, early establishment occurred under very low pollinator availability, and later source/island comparisons quantify reproductive-system and seed consequences.

This moves the evidence beyond an ordinary cross-sectional island comparison. It still does not supply a directly dated matched focal-pollinator arrival trajectory linked to the same pre/post plant-response unit, so the full transition contract remains open.

### Tiritiri Matangi — direct partner reintroduction, then compensation

Tiritiri supplies the cleanest direct partner-arrival history recovered in the programme. Hihi were deliberately reintroduced in the 1990s. In the later hangehange experiment, hihi contributed a large share of observed avian visits and pollinator-exclusion treatments measured reproductive consequences.

The experiment is especially informative because restoration of the focal visitor did **not** translate into a simple one-for-one restoration of total pollination function: other birds could compensate. This is a direct transition-informed buffering/falsification case.

Yet the full contract still fails because a matched pre-reintroduction plant functional state and plant outcome are not available for the same focal unit.

The empirical bottleneck has therefore changed from:

> no direct historical transition coordinate in new geography-first waves

to:

> direct transition history can be recovered in special small-island systems, but the pre-transition baseline and post-transition plant response are still rarely joined in one longitudinal unit.

## 5. Post-saturation manuscript-value review

A separate value review prevents the global search from bloating the paper with every recovered system.

Only three systems are promoted to the final synthesis layer:

1. **Surtsey** — dated empty-start colonization and persistent reproductive response;
2. **Tiritiri Matangi** — documented partner reintroduction, direct pollination-function experiment and compensatory buffering;
3. **Gulf of California large islands** — historical/ploidy alternative mechanism that limits current-pollinator causal interpretations.

Chiloé, Newfoundland, Tierra del Fuego, Sumatra and Saba remain useful Supporting Information examples. Source gaps remain source gaps. None of these decisions changes the active 42/37 or frozen 25 denominators.

## 6. Final Izu zoom: what existing data actually resolve

The final Izu audit uses the same bottleneck coordinates rather than introducing another story.

### 6.1 Historical trait recovery is no longer the main technical block

The 2017 Hiraiwa–Ushimaru supplement now supplies safe source-native species-level numeric proboscis values for **202/209** current named pollinator taxa by exact or whitespace-only identity matching.

This supports the already frozen community-centre signed-position transfer. It does **not** make every site-specific proboscis value exact and does not recover plant x pollinator weights for an exact plant-specific partner centre or complete site-exact Rao-Q reconstruction.

The field-readiness state has therefore been updated: the real missing input is empirical linked field data, not zero recovery of the historical trait table.

### 6.2 Contemporary functional exposure -> matching is robust

Within the source-native 2024 networks, corrected trait matching remains positively associated with pollinator functional diversity:

- Izu five islands FDQ coefficient: **+1.943**;
- post-Oshima four islands: **+2.059**;
- every leave-one-island coefficient remains positive in both subsets.

The post-Oshima subset contains no recorded *Bombus* rows in the archived site-season network table, so the within-subset relationship cannot be reduced to a binary sampled-network *Bombus* present/absent contrast.

The strongest current Izu result is therefore a continuous functional one: contemporary pollinator functional structure organizes matching even inside the post-boundary region.

### 6.3 Matching -> pollen is directional but conditional

The source reproductive model and fixed-effect sensitivities remain positive on average:

- Izu five-island TM coefficient: **+0.0353**;
- post-Oshima four-island coefficient: **+0.0342**.

But leave-one-island ranges cross zero. The matching-to-pollen link is therefore weaker and network-state dependent, not a fixed deterministic cascade.

### 6.4 A shared matching decline branches downstream

Among eight source-defined plants with matched Oshima-to-post contrasts:

- corrected matching: **8/8 lower** post-Oshima;
- floral tube: **3 shorter / 4 longer / 1 unchanged**;
- pollen receipt: **4 lower / 4 higher**;
- only **2/8** show the full matching-lower + tube-shorter + pollen-lower combination.

This is direct empirical response branching. One contemporary functional shift does not produce a universal floral or reproductive syndrome.

### 6.5 Signed-position triangulation is useful but not causal history

The frozen raw signed-position projection is strong descriptively:

- slope **+0.5669**;
- 95% CI **+0.2977 to +0.8361**;
- all five leave-one-island slopes positive.

But the exact island-centre assignment is not uniquely identified, a source-position-only comparator explains at least as much raw structure, and the background-community-corrected target is unsupported.

Therefore the supported reading remains source floral state + broad contemporary community composition, not historical *Bombus* causation.

## 7. What Izu still cannot identify

The missing pieces are now specific rather than vague:

- no directly observed historical partner-loss trajectory in the focal Izu system;
- no directly observed historical partner-arrival/replacement trajectory linked to the same focal transition;
- no direct effective-pollinator dependency measurements from the same tagged contemporary populations;
- no matched visitor -> single-visit pollen deposition -> open/bagged/outcross -> mature-seed chain in those populations;
- only one Oshima bridge-state geographic unit versus four post-boundary sites;
- no temporal pre/post transition with matched plant response.

Issue #91 was closed as `not_planned`, not because these data were collected. The current field status remains `implementation_ready_field_data_missing`.

## 8. Final empirical main line

The next information gain is no longer another world-island search or another synthetic perturbation sweep.

The highest-value design is:

```text
same tagged Izu population / plant
  -> usable observation effort including zero-visit windows
  -> visitor identity + site-linked proboscis
  -> legitimate floral contact
  -> single-visit pollen deposition + no-visit control
  -> open / bagged-autonomous / supplemental-outcross reproduction
  -> mature fruit and seed
```

and, for historical identification, this chain must be coupled to either:

- an independently replicated Oshima-like bridge-state exposure; or
- a genuine temporal partner-regime transition with pre/post plant response.

Adding more plants or seasons at the single Oshima site is useful for variance and mechanism estimation, but it does not manufacture an independent geographic boundary replicate.

## 9. Current Chapter 2 interpretation

The combined result is now coherent:

1. the frozen simulation establishes multiple possible response states and identifies partner loss/arrival as strong regime coordinates;
2. geography-first world expansion shows that outcomes and contemporary mechanisms are common, while those historical transition coordinates are systematically sparse;
3. small islands demonstrate that transition history can be recovered and can reveal buffering or founding-state effects, but still rarely close the full longitudinal contract;
4. Izu resolves how contemporary functional community structure maps into matching and how downstream responses branch, while exposing exactly why historical causal identification remains open.

That is a stronger result than a catalogue of positive island examples. The empirical limitation is itself structured: **the processes most capable of determining regime history are the ones least often measured longitudinally, whereas the downstream contemporary state is comparatively well observed.**
