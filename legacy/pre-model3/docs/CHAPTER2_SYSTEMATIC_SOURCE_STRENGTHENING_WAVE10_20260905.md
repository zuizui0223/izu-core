# Chapter 2 systematic source-strengthening wave 10

Updated: 2026-09-05

## Decision

Wave 10 continues the exhaustive 111-target island-universe review after wave 9 reduced active source work to 19 targets.

The goal of this wave is **terminal review completion**, not evidence inflation. Repeatedly searched geographies are allowed to leave the active queue when their literature state is itself clear: no qualifying local primary process source was recovered, a source explicitly reports that pollination is unknown, geography is covered but local process evidence remains unrecovered, or authoritative sources conflict on mechanism.

These are source-review states. They are **not biological absence** and are reopened if a new qualifying source appears.

Wave 10 reviews ten targets and closes all ten from active source work, reducing the queue from **19 to 9**.

## Special terminal states

### Chatham Islands

Chatham geography is already represented source-natively in the current Southwest Pacific morphology layer through Ciarle et al. 2025 (`10.1093/aob/mcaf005`). Repeated local-process searching did not recover a qualifying Chatham-specific pollination or breeding-system experiment.

Decision: `terminal_geography_covered_process_gap`.

The geography is therefore covered, while the local interaction mechanism remains unresolved.

### Norfolk Island

Flora of Australia resolves *Elatostema montanum* as monoecious with male and female inflorescences, whereas the Parks Australia conservation account describes rare male and female plants whose proximity enabled seed production. Repeated searching did not recover a source-native experimental pollination study capable of resolving the wording/mechanism conflict.

Decision: `terminal_authoritative_conflict`.

The conflict remains explicit and can be reopened if a primary mechanism study appears.

### Turks and Caicos

Primary anchor: `10.1017/S0030605311000251`.

The Oryx study profiles endemic *Argythamnia argentea* and *Encyclia caicensis* and explicitly reports that their pollination is unknown. Repeated follow-up searching did not recover a later local direct mechanism study.

Decision: `terminal_primary_source_explicit_unknown`.

This is stronger than an unsearched gap: the best source-native primary evidence explicitly records the knowledge gap.
