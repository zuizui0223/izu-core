## HOW / proximal WHY / ultimate WHY

### HOW — directly represented

```text
partner turnover
    -> deformation of the community interaction kernel
        -> state- and trajectory-conditioned functional service
            -> local support and weight reallocation
                -> downstream reproductive propagation
```

### Proximal WHY — supported within the model

Opposite response signs arise because a broad perturbation does not specify one endpoint kernel. Turnover changes the regime, starting position supplies the input state, realized communities change the endpoint geometry, and their combination is non-additive. Local filtering can then change branch identity. This is a model-conditional explanation, not an inference about the history of a real island.

### Ultimate WHY — outside Chapter 2

The analyses do not identify why a regional pollinator pool formed, why a lineage began at a particular state, why colonization and persistence selected one assemblage, or why a historical interaction shift occurred. Assembly, biogeographic history and evolution remain untested causes.

## Claim-boundary audit

### Supported

- community reorganization can generate conditional response geometry in the declared model;
- the exact sign coordinate is a trajectory-conditioned community-kernel difference;
- turnover, starting state, realized community, non-additivity, local filtering and downstream assurance occupy distinct explanatory levels;
- real island research entries contain multiple qualitative response states;
- the current literature inventory does not admit the frozen joint state-community-context-outcome comparison;
- Izu resolution localizes the current raw-matching signal to source state plus background community composition and does not support additional null-corrected sorting.

### Prohibited

- interpreting `41/96`, `16/48` or filtering transition rates as natural prevalence;
- calling the loss/arrival regression causal or field-calibrated;
- using starting state alone as the response explanation;
- replacing `G_omega(x0)` by a same-`x0` kernel difference when trait adjustment changes endpoints;
- calling the 25 entries independent archipelagos, a meta-analysis or validation;
- fitting `H0`–`H4` after reconstructing predictors from observed responses;
- presenting Izu as a global ranking winner or raw matching as beyond-composition sorting;
- using Chapter 3 phenotype as Chapter 2 validation, causal pollinator-selection evidence or external prediction.

## Final theory-level decision

The existing evidence can support **Tier B** as defined for this project: a general conditional-response framework, an empirical reality constraint and a breadth-to-depth mechanistic-resolution funnel. It does not support Tier C. The interaction-kernel derivation is exact but remains an analytic representation of one model class; external prediction is not evaluable; and transportability beyond island plant–pollinator reorganization is a hypothesis rather than demonstrated field-wide theory.

The rational submission sequence is **Oikos first**, then **Journal of Ecology** if editorial feedback indicates that the general theory claim is too broad for the current empirical depth. *Ecology* is a stretch. *Ecology Letters* and *The American Naturalist* are not justified under the no-new-results constraint.
