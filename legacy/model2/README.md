## Allowed use

Model 2 may be used to:

- reconstruct the development history of Chapter 2;
- trace the origin of the ecological-averaging idea;
- audit why exact richness, synthetic-k and S/C/I ceased to be active biological claims.

It must not be presented as:

- current Supporting Information;
- a second required biological mechanism;
- an unresolved Chapter 2 gate;
- evidence for a natural k threshold, natural branch prevalence or calibrated island quantity.
