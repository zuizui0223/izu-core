# Chapter 2 scientific gate run

Updated: 2026-08-27

The scientific reassessment gate opened after the manuscript critique is **complete**. This was not a seed search and did not calibrate to empirical island outcomes.

## Fixed design

- Matched response-geometry stability: 96 pollinator-community realizations across the full 0–1 plant starting-trait grid.
- Joint transition surface: 48 fixed-seed Latin-hypercube points across the 10 declared perturbation/matching parameters, with 24 matched realizations per point.
- Local-context and assurance thresholds: 12 replicates per saturation, 4 contexts, 24 lineages and 120 steps; local filtering stress spans 0–0.75 and assurance spans 0×–4×.

## Final results

### Response geometry

- 41 of 96 matched pollinator-community realizations contained both positive and negative island-minus-mainland service responses across starting positions.
- The mean response geometry was mixed-sign rather than monotonic.
- Mean sign changes occurred around starting-trait intervals 0.30–0.35 and 0.65–0.70: the central part of the synthetic trait axis tended to show negative island-minus-mainland service response while both outer regions tended to show positive response.

### Joint parameter robustness

Across the fixed 48-point, 10-parameter Latin-hypercube design:

- 16 points produced mixed mean response geometry;
- 22 produced all-positive mean geometry;
- 10 produced all-negative mean geometry.

Thus mixed mean geometry occupied one third of the declared synthetic joint design rather than one selected parameter corner. This is a robustness descriptor, not an estimate of ecological prevalence.

### Local-context filtering

- 737 lineage contrasts showed at least one response-sign change somewhere in the declared local-filtering envelope.
- Median first sign-change strength was 0.40.
- Sign-change fractions increased across the filtering envelope, from about 13.2% at strength 0.10 to about 73.5% at strength 0.75.

`support_strength` remains an availability / interaction-filtering stress axis. It must not be described as added beneficial support.

### Autonomous assurance

- 580 lineage contrasts met the eligible baseline-decline condition.
- No sign rescue occurred at any tested multiplier from 0.5× through 4×.
- Upstream effective service was identical across assurance multipliers (mismatch count 0).
- Magnitude improvement remained broad: 565 of 580 eligible declines improved at 0.5× and 539 of 580 improved at 4×.

The defensible role of autonomous assurance is therefore **magnitude attenuation**, not a strong sign-rescue branch.

## Integrated decision

The model-side route is:

> **Research Article candidate — conditional response geometry.**

The headline is not that initial heterogeneity is a universal minimal generator. The supported synthetic result is that the sign of post-establishment response to pollinator reorganization depends on starting functional position and realized partner context; local filtering can reallocate branch identity in either direction, whereas the implemented autonomous-assurance route changes magnitude without crossing the sign boundary in the tested 0–4× envelope.
