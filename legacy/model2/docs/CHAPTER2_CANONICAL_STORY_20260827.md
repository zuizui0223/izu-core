# Chapter 2 canonical story

Updated: 2026-09-11

## One-sentence claim

> **Pollinator-community reorganization produces conditional rather than fixed plant responses, and even the relative importance of plant starting state versus realized community can change with the stochastic/system-size regime.**

This is the Chapter 2 paper mainline. It is a mechanistic and synthetic claim, not a field-validation claim.

## Main inferential spine

```text
broad pollinator-interaction reorganization
        ↓
conditional response geometry
        ×
plant starting functional state
        ×
realized community
        ↓
response branch
        ↓
regime-dependent determinant ordering
```

The paper asks why the same broad interaction perturbation need not generate one reproductive response. The answer is that response sign and magnitude are relational: they depend on the response regime, plant starting state and the particular community realization, with substantial non-additivity.

The September 2026 system-size audit adds a second result. Determinant ordering is itself conditional. In the baseline finite-community regime, community realization explains most cell-level variation; when independent community trajectories are pooled, the community-realization share declines and the starting-state share can become larger while state × community non-additivity remains substantial.

The supported conclusion is therefore **not** that starting state is universally dominant, and **not** that realized community is universally dominant. The hierarchy of determinants can itself depend on the stochastic/system-size regime.

## Frozen results that support the mainline

### 1. Conditional response geometry

Across 96 matched pollinator-community realizations, the frozen baseline contains mixed-sign, all-positive and all-negative response worlds. Starting position organizes a mean U-shaped response boundary, but does not determine the response by itself.

Across the fixed 48-point joint design, mixed and one-direction regimes coexist. This coexistence is part of the result: the model does not imply one universal island response.

### 2. Partner turnover moves the response regime

Within the declared design, stronger partner loss and weaker partner arrival are associated with a larger negative portion of the response surface. These are design-space associations, not natural causal effect sizes.

### 3. Starting state and realized community are relational

For the baseline `21 × 96` response matrix, the frozen variance partition is:

- starting-position main effect: `2.18%`;
- community-realization main effect: `80.17%`;
- non-additive starting-position × community remainder: `17.64%`.

Observed sign differs from the fitted additive sign in `271/2016 = 13.44%` of cells. The biologically relevant unit is therefore a lineage **relative to the community that is realized**, not starting position or island status alone.

### 4. Determinant ordering is regime-dependent

With active plant adjustment retained and independent community trajectories pooled at `k={1,2,4,8,16}`, median starting-position share rises while median community-realization share falls. Starting-position share exceeds community-realization share in all six prespecified seeds at `k=4`, `k=8` and `k=16`, but not at `k=1` or `k=2`. Mixed-sign realizations persist and non-additivity remains substantial.

This is a **rank-crossover result inside the synthetic model**. It does not establish a natural threshold near `k=4`.

Synthetic `k` pools independent pollinator trajectories. Therefore:

- raw visitor richness is not synthetic `k`;
- effective-service Hill diversity is not synthetic `k`;
- the numerical crossover near `k=4` must not be transferred to field systems.

### 5. Local filtering and reproductive assurance remain downstream modifiers

Local filtering can reallocate response branches bidirectionally but asymmetrically. Reproductive assurance attenuates downstream magnitude without rescuing response sign in the declared tested envelope. Neither result turns the paper into a claim about one universal field mechanism.

## Relationship to world evidence

The world/literature programme is used to establish biological plausibility, identify what kinds of measurements exist, and define the empirical claim ceiling. It is **not** required to demonstrate a complete natural analogue of the synthetic model.

Breadth searching has already saturated for the current paper: additional island systems are not a Chapter 2 completion requirement unless they are needed to resolve a claim-critical contradiction.

The absence of a fully transition-linked natural chain is an identifiability limitation, not an unfinished obligation that keeps the paper open.

## Izu and the same-block field chain

The prospective Izu design remains useful as a future falsification protocol:

```text
pre-outcome plant state × visitor exposure/composition
        → single-visit deposition / effective service
        → dependency treatment
        → mature seed
```

Its strict unit is the same prespecified `block_id × plant_id`, and the E3/E4 tooling can be retained in the repository for later work.

However, **this chain is not part of the current Chapter 2 paper mainline, claim ceiling or completion criterion**. Collecting same-block visitor/effectiveness/dependency/seed data would constitute a separate prospective validation study or later extension.

No Chapter 2 conclusion depends on field confirmation of the synthetic rank crossover.

## Relationship to Chapter 3

Chapter 3 owns the realized multivariate phenotype of the focal *Campanula microdonta* lineage. Its phenotype values are not used to tune, rescue or validate the Chapter 2 model.

The dissertation handoff is therefore:

```text
Chapter 1
WHEN / WHERE does island-associated filtering occur?
        ↓
Chapter 2
HOW can interaction reorganization generate conditional response branches?
WHEN can the ordering of response determinants itself change?
        ↓
Chapter 3
WHAT multivariate phenotype is actually realized in the focal lineage?
```

The optional Izu same-block validation programme runs in parallel to this sequence; it is not a required arrow between Chapter 2 and Chapter 3.

## Claim boundary

Chapter 2 must not imply that:

- one universal island syndrome or one universal response mechanism has been identified;
- Chapter 1 regional response vectors have been assigned to particular Chapter 2 parameter regimes;
- pollinator occurrence equals effectiveness or dependency;
- starting state alone determines response;
- realized community is universally the dominant determinant;
- synthetic branch frequencies estimate natural prevalence;
- the crossover near `k=4` is a natural threshold;
- raw visitor richness or Hill diversity is literally synthetic `k`;
- additive design-space coefficients are causal ecological effect sizes;
- historical *Bombus* loss, evolutionary selection or an Oshima–Toshima causal boundary has been identified;
- the prospective Izu field chain is required for the present paper to be complete; or
- Chapter 3 phenotype divergence proves the Chapter 2 mechanism.

## Completion rule

Chapter 2 is scientifically closed when the manuscript, figures and robustness checks consistently support the frozen conditional-response and regime-dependent determinant-ordering claims within the boundaries above.

**Field E3/E4 data are optional future validation, not a remaining empirical gate.**
