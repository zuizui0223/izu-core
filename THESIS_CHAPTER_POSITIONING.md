# Thesis positioning — Chapter 2

Updated: 2026-09-11

## Role in the dissertation

`izu-core` is the **Chapter 2 / conditional-response mechanism** component of the dissertation.

The dissertation sequence is deliberately separated by inferential level:

- `zuizui0223/island` — **Chapter 1:** when and where island-associated floral/reproductive filtering is detectable and where multivariate response vectors differ;
- `izu-core` — **Chapter 2:** how a common broad reorganization of pollinator interactions can generate different response branches, and when the relative importance of response determinants changes;
- `zuizui0223/shimahotarubukuro` — **Chapter 3:** what multivariate phenotype is actually realized in the focal Izu lineage.

## Canonical Chapter 2 question

> **Why need not a common island-like reorganization of pollinator interactions produce one post-establishment plant response?**

The current answer is a **conditional response geometry**:

```text
broad pollinator-interaction reorganization
        ↓
response regime
        ×
plant starting functional state
        ×
realized pollinator community
        ↓
response branch
```

The later system-size audit adds a second claim:

> **The ordering of response determinants is itself regime-dependent.**

In the baseline finite-community regime, realized community dominates much of the cell-level variation. When independent community trajectories are pooled, the community-realization share declines and the starting-state share can become larger while non-additivity persists.

This is the Chapter 2 paper spine.

## HOW, proximal WHY and ultimate WHY

| Level | Question | Current Chapter 2 answer | Claim ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HOW** | Through what response architecture does pollinator reorganization propagate? | Partner turnover moves the response regime; starting state and realized community jointly determine response branch; downstream local filtering and reproductive assurance modify the realized response. | Directly represented and audited within the declared synthetic model. |
| **Proximal WHY** | Why can the same broad perturbation yield different responses? | Response is conditional on regime, starting state and realized community, with non-additivity; the relative importance of starting state and realized community can itself change across stochastic/system-size regimes. | Diagnostic explanation within the frozen synthetic design. The numerical synthetic crossover is not transferred to nature. |
| **Ultimate WHY** | Why did an island acquire its biota, starting states or interaction architecture? | Not tested. | Assembly, colonization, persistence, historical partner loss and evolutionary history remain upstream explanations. |

## What the current paper does not require

The paper does **not** require a same-block field chain of

```text
visitor exposure → single-visit deposition/effective service
→ dependency treatment → mature seed
```

to be scientifically complete.

The E3/E4 Izu field design added in September 2026 remains a useful, preregisterable **future falsification/validation protocol**, but it is not:

- part of the present paper's main inferential spine;
- part of the Chapter 2 claim ceiling;
- a required empirical gate for manuscript completion; or
- a prerequisite for the Chapter 2 → Chapter 3 handoff.

The field tooling stays in the repository because it may support a later prospective study. Its presence must not be read as evidence that the current paper is unfinished.

## Synthetic rank crossover: exact interpretation

The synthetic system-size result is retained as a Chapter 2 result because it changes the mechanistic interpretation of the model itself.

It supports:

> **determinant ordering can depend on community stochasticity/system scale while relational non-additivity persists.**

It does not support:

- a universal natural crossover threshold;
- a claim that `k≈4` exists in island pollination systems;
- treating visitor richness as synthetic `k`; or
- treating effective-service Hill diversity as synthetic `k`.

Synthetic `k` pools independent pollinator trajectories and remains a model coordinate.

## Relationship to world evidence

World/literature evidence is used to:

1. establish biological plausibility;
2. identify the empirical measurement ceiling; and
3. prevent synthetic results from being narrated as already demonstrated natural causal chains.

The world programme has reached sufficient breadth for the present Chapter 2 claim. The lack of a complete transition-linked natural chain is an **identifiability limitation**, not a remaining fieldwork obligation.

Additional island-system searching is justified only if it resolves a claim-critical contradiction or is needed for a separate empirical study.

## Relationship to Izu

Izu remains scientifically valuable as a future high-continuity validation system and as the geographic setting of the focal lineage used in Chapter 3.

For the current Chapter 2 paper, however, Izu is **not the endpoint needed to validate the synthetic mechanism**. The paper should not be rewritten around visitor → effectiveness → dependency → seed measurements.

Prospective Izu E3/E4 measurements may later ask whether a qualitative field analogue of determinant-order change exists, but a positive result would strengthen a later validation study rather than retroactively create the present paper's main claim.

## Relationship to Chapter 3

The Chapter 2 → Chapter 3 handoff is a change in inferential scale:

```text
Chapter 2
conditional-response mechanism
    → regime-dependent determinant ordering
        ↓
Chapter 3
realized multivariate phenotype in the focal lineage
```

Chapter 3 phenotype values are not used to tune, rescue or validate Chapter 2. Likewise, Chapter 2 does not need the prospective field E3/E4 chain to hand off to Chapter 3.

## Claim boundary

Chapter 2 must not imply that:

- Chapter 1 identified *Bombus* loss or another pollinator as the cause of regional differences;
- Chapter 1 regional vectors have been assigned to specific Chapter 2 parameter regimes;
- one universal island syndrome or universal response mechanism has been identified;
- starting state alone determines response;
- realized community is universally dominant;
- synthetic branch frequencies estimate natural prevalence;
- the crossover near `k=4` is a natural threshold;
- raw visitor richness or effective-service diversity is literally synthetic `k`;
- historical *Bombus* loss, evolutionary selection or an Oshima–Toshima causal boundary has been identified;
- the prospective Izu same-block chain is required for Chapter 2 completion; or
- Chapter 3 phenotype divergence proves the Chapter 2 mechanism.
