**Interpretation boundaries.** In Figure 1, fixed A does not prevent selfing and fresh generality remains inconclusive for direct assurance cost. In Figure 3, Q2 is inconclusive while Q3 bridge and structural target exclusion come from separate source cohorts, not an observed Q2→Q3 mechanism. The earlier [exploratory matched endpoint/repeatability figure](supplement_S1_order_repeatability_exploratory.svg) is retained in the supplement, not counted among the six main displays. In Figure 2, different baseline effectiveness regimes also create different histories and changes in absolute pollen service. In Figure 4, genetic closure at B8 and the B4.5/B8 survival interaction do not identify genetic mediation. In Figure 5, the longer B4.5 polymorphic duration includes time gained by avoiding extinction. In Figure 6, extinction remains in all-start occupancy denominators, but common-context performance is survivor-conditioned. Neither a population-wide recovery-probability clock, mutation-enabled irreversibility nor empirical island filtering is demonstrated by these figures.
